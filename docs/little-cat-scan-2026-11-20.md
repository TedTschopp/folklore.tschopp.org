# The Little Cat: missing V restored

The [readable printed opening on p. 210](https://babel.hathitrust.org/cgi/pt?id=hvd.32044089074066&seq=232)
has a large ornamental capital **V**, followed by `or vielen hundert Jahren`.
The public original now restores that missing letter. The complete telling
continues and ends on [p. 211](https://babel.hathitrust.org/cgi/pt?id=hvd.32044089074066&seq=233).

The witness is Harvard University's copy of Meinrad Lienert's *Schweizer Sagen
und Heldengeschichten, der Jugend erzählt*, illustrated by Wilhelm Roegge,
Stuttgart and Olten: Levy u. Müller, HathiTrust item `hvd.32044089074066`.
Its inspected title and imprint pages are undated; the
[catalogue supplies **[1917]**](https://catalog.hathitrust.org/Record/102663077).
The exact 1915 edition requested in the follow-up has not been obtained, and
this copy is not described as a verified 1915 scan.

The complete printed account was compared with the approved telling. Minor
variants such as `nicht allzu schwer machen` versus `nicht allzu sehr bedrücken`
are not imported. The 1915 source credit inherited from Märchenstiftung stays
distinct from the actual printed witness named in the new reader note.

The release changes one original-language paragraph by one inserted letter and
adds one evidence note. All 3,858 English texts, story titles, URLs, source groups,
comparison pairs and 14,059 existing reader-note texts and identifiers remain
intact. The reader-note total is 14,060. No held story is newly published.

All three opening-letter questions now have readable printed witnesses. The
exact 1915 edition remains a separate bibliographic follow-up. Details and
before/after hashes are in the
[cumulative correction ledger](transcription-scans-2026-11-20.json); source images
and the full inventory stay in the private authoring repository.
