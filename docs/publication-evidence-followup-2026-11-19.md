# November 19 evidence follow-up

This release adds two translations after a second review of the 63 remaining
evidence holds:

| English title | Dated source | Reading page |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Magdhilde’s Spring near Dingten | Ernst Ludwig Rochholz, *Naturmythen. Neue Schweizersagen*, Leipzig: Teubner, 1862, section 6, no. 14, p. 143 | [Read the story](/stories/magdhildes-spring-near-dingten/) |
| The Spirits of the Jungfrau | Hermann Hartmann, *Berner Oberland in Sage und Geschichte*, I: *Sagen*, Bümpliz: Benteli, 1910, p. 42 | [Read the story](/stories/the-spirits-of-the-jungfrau/) |

Both complete narratives were compared with original printed pages, and their
transcription differences are described in reader notes. The Rochholz scan
resolves the catalogue's `Lee` as `See`, a lake. Its commentary is identified as
the collector's historical interpretation, and the limits of the quoted note
are explicit. The Hartmann page preserves the full winter-spirit account and
restores the author's appended Vogt source credit as reader context.

The collection now contains **3,853 bilingual pages and 14,021 reader notes**,
plus two source-note pages. The 78-page catalogue, search index and sitemap
include both additions.

**61 of this review's stories remain held.** The missing evidence includes
complete original Siegen and Keller editions, exact matches for modern
retellings and translations, and undated or abridged expressions. The Dwarf
King is identified in an Englert-Faye volume published posthumously in 1984;
that manuscript history supplies no pre-1931 publication basis. Across the
original 649-story hold review, 25 stories are now cleared and 624 remain held.

contains all 63 findings, evidence and individual next steps. Private source
