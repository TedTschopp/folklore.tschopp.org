# November 19 source-clearance release

This release adds **23 translations**, bringing the collection to **3,851
bilingual pages, 14,001 reader notes, and two source-note pages**. The 78-page
catalogue, full-text search and sitemap include the new stories.

The private authoring review assesses all 649 prior publication holds. Twenty
Uri records are identified by exact numbered entries in the 1926 or 1929
volumes. Two Jecklin stories are checked against the complete 1874 and 1876 scan
pages, and *Choosing a Bride* is identified as Grimm KHM 155 in the 1857 seventh
edition. Each new page cites its particular edition. Minor transcription and
orthographic differences are disclosed in reader notes; source records and
English narrative fields remain preserved.

**626 records remain held:** 563 have identified 1945 Uri expressions; 63 still
require an exact edition, complete wording comparison, or another publication
basis. One Uri index link returned the wrong body and is explicitly unresolved.
The original German Siegen collection is documented in 1921; the catalogue's
unverified 1905 parenthesis does not clear its expanded 1979 texts. Keller's
narrator dates do not date the 1940 expression or establish unchanged 1927 text.

The [private review](https://github.com/TedTschopp/swiss-folklore/tree/main/publication/reviews/2026-11-19-source-clearances)
contains all 649 dispositions, evidence, remaining research steps and the
[23 publication URLs](https://github.com/TedTschopp/swiss-folklore/blob/main/publication/reviews/2026-11-19-source-clearances/publication-urls.csv).
Private source documents, held texts and unreviewed translations are excluded
from this public repository. An export check binds the reviewed edition ledger
to the immutable corpus snapshot and rejects held expressions, even when an old
combined citation contains an eligible-looking earlier year.

All existing story pages and reader guides are unchanged. The eighteen approved
combined-copy groups retain their sources, and all four comparison pairs remain
separate. New pages use English headings, readable title URLs, cultural notes,
separate English/notes/original navigation, discussion links and canonical
Webmention targets. The two Carbuncle versions have distinct edition/place URLs.
No TTRPG interface, domain-router, account or moderation setting changes.

Validation covers the Jekyll production build, complete paragraph/note and
catalogue checks, Pagefind, analytics markup, mobile and keyboard reading,
accessibility, mocked response submission, the seven domain-router scenarios,
and 157 private-corpus tests. Deployment and read-only live verification are
recorded separately.
