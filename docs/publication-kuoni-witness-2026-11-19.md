# Kuoni source review — November 19, 2026

This release adds [The Three-Legged Hare](/stories/the-three-legged-hare/),
verified against Jakob Kuoni, *Sagen des Kantons St. Gallen* (St. Gallen:
Wiser & Frey, Buchdruckerei Merkur, **1903**), no. 81, printed **p. 37**.
The [original story page](https://babel.hathitrust.org/cgi/pt?id=chi.088179859&seq=67)
and [title/imprint](https://babel.hathitrust.org/cgi/pt?id=chi.088179859&seq=5)
were visually inspected. The catalogue's three narrative sentences and source
credit match that printing apart from punctuation. Kuoni's separate closing
comment identifies the hare as a witch; it is translated in Reader notes.
The existing English translation and catalogue original are preserved.

The four reader notes explain Alp, the omitted historical comment, Rüti's
locality, and the verified edition. The public archive now contains **3,858
bilingual tales**, **14,038 reader notes**, and two held source-note pages.
All previous story pages, guides, readable URLs and response aliases are
preserved. The four unresolved comparison pairs remain separate and the 18
combined-copy groups are unchanged. Discussion and Webmention links use the
canonical story URL; outgoing submissions remain disabled in the publication
registry. Browser verification uses mocked submissions.

Of the 57 starting evidence holds, one is cleared and 56 remain held. The
complete early Bundi *Engadiner Märchen, Zweite Folge*, catalogued [1903],
was recovered and compared, but its Dragon and Tredeschin differ from the
later German retellings currently translated. Those retellings remain held.
The complete German 1921 *Gletschermärchen* was not located in the checked
digital collections. Physical bibliographic leads were recorded for further
acquisition; they do not clear the 26 expanded-edition expressions.
