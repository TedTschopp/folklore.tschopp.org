release corrects 314 public titles and 349 canonical URLs to English wording,
retaining personal, place and traditional folklore names. The two Lady of
Valnüglia versions use `short-telling` and `long-telling` locators.

to their canonical pages. Old TedT.org destinations also resolve to the current

story identities. Existing footnote fragment identifiers are preserved when
the reading order changes.

an editorial reader note explains the change. Another 204 exact repeated title
paragraphs receive their reviewed English headings. Every following narrative
paragraph and every original-language paragraph is preserved. No new narrative
event is supplied.

and remain separate by the editor's explicit choice. The four protected

All 14,038 existing reader-note texts and identifiers are retained, with 19 new
explanations: 14,057 total. No held story is newly published, and no telling is
newly merged. The private authoring repository contains the complete inventory
of 7,381 records, screening dispositions, source excerpts and pending original
transcription queries. This public repository contains only approved editions
and the [published correction ledger](editorial-integrity-2026-11-20.json).

Validation includes the immutable-source exporter checks, full corpus validation,
the Jekyll build, Pagefind, analytics audit, all rendered text and footnote checks,
response identities, all 379 domain aliases, and browser/accessibility checks for
reading, search, navigation and mocked Webmention submission. Collection-wide
screening does not constitute a new line-by-line translation or facsimile
collation of every story.
