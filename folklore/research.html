How the indexes are read

Topic collections group the existing story annotations; a tale can appear in more than one. Broad recorded labels such as Local memory are retained here. The collections do not establish a new scholarly classification.

Place labels can identify a setting, a region or a place mentioned in the telling. A canton label does not locate every episode precisely. Regional pages show names occurring together in those stories; the site does not infer exact map coordinates.

Reading times estimate the English story at 200 words per minute. Allow more time for the original text, notes and source links.
