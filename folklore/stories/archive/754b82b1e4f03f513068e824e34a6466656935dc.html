That is, she stripped off the turf, made small piles of wood, laid the pieces of sod over them, and burned them so that she could use the ash as fertilizer, since she had no other kind.", "Cattle were grazing nearby on the Allmende. Carrying the brushwood became burdensome for the maiden, especially because the day in question was very hot, so she had the idea of using a young cow to haul the wood. She tied the bundles of brush to the animal's tail and transported them in this fashion. But the cow was then stricken by what the source calls Blutanstossen oder Blutstocken and died. When the farmer learned of this, he cursed the woman, saying that after her death she should have no rest but must wander ceaselessly around the scene of her offense until Judgment Day. The curse came true. Since the unfortunate woman's death, she has often been seen by day, and still more often in the evening, wandering over the Flumser Alpen and the highest Maiensässe in simple country dress and a white apron. Sometimes, she even visited the Alp and Maiensäss huts for a few minutes, answered questions put to her, and ate from the food she was given, although the food did not diminish.", "When she had stopped at an alpine hut one evening and eaten, she said that it was high time for her to depart, for that same evening she still had to wander through all the Flumser Alpen.
