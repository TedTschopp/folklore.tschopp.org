From its head, at the Lombach Alp, flows the Lombach, which often still becomes terrible through flooding. A mysterious little man is said to walk along the streambed every time before the waters rise, striking the banks to his right and left with a long staff wherever they will be torn away by the surge. In former times, a chapel stood at Saint Niklausen, where the stream emerges from the valley gorge. At the rear of the valley, however, where the path rises toward the dreadful and desolate Hohgant on the border of the canton of Lucerne, lies an alp named Allgäu. There, a sizable earthen rampart raised by human hands is believed to be the remains of a city. A raiding party from Entlebuch once attacked the herds and alpine dairymen from the Oberland on this alp and murdered them cruelly; the cowherds were drowned in boiling whey. A strong young herdsman escaped with a Volle, a wooden milk funnel, in his hand. He stood on a height in the Habkern valley and shouted through the Volle like a speaking trumpet toward the floor of the valley at Unterseen, straining so violently with the cry that his body burst. His beloved was the first to hear him below.
