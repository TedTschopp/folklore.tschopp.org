After many pilgrimages, the parents were prevented by the Föhn from traveling by boat and came instead across Seelisberg to this place. There, in a small structure, stood the image of Mary that can now be seen at the top of the high altar. They prayed and, exhausted by the mountain journey, fell asleep.
