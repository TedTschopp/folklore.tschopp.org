Built so remotely in an Alpine region, it owes its origin to the following event:", "A man whose name is still known, although upright and honorable, became so mentally ill that he formed the sorrowful resolution to hang himself. He therefore took a rope and hurried high into the forest with it. When he reached a place that seemed suitable for his dreadful purpose, an icy chill ran through all his limbs. Nevertheless, he meant to carry it out, but first he wished to pray one Lord's Prayer. He knelt and began to pray. Then, on the lowest branch of a nearby larch tree, he noticed a little green man who lowered and offered him a long rope. Horrified, the man at once recognized all the wickedness and terrible consequences of his evil intention. He sincerely repented of it and begged God's forgiveness. When he made the sign of the cross, the Evil One immediately vanished from his sight, and he breathed more freely again. Before leaving the place, however, he vowed in gratitude for his miraculous rescue to build a prayer house there in honor of St. Joseph.
