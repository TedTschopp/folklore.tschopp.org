When he looked into the pool, he saw the little tree upside down in it, and among the branches of the reflected tree a bird’s nest made of twigs, with three young ravens in the nest. He stood up and tried to fetch the ravens. But at no price could he find either the nest or the birds. He tried and puzzled for a long time, looked into the pool, and climbed the fir, but all in vain, until it occurred to him to count the tree’s branches in the reflection. He now noted exactly upon which numbered branch the sought-for nest rested, climbed up again, felt along the branch, and finally held a stone in his hand; now the nest and its three young also became visible. He took the stone home and showed it to his father. ‘Look here, Father, what sort of stone I found inside a raven’s nest!’ he said. ‘The Devil take it!’ cried his father. ‘Where have you come from now, and where are you, that no one can see you?’ ‘Why, I’m standing right in front of you; can’t you see?’ asked the boy. ‘I can’t see a bit of you,’ his father replied helplessly. For some time the herders on a Schächen Valley Alp had noticed that the ravens always flew to one particular spot on a neighboring high cliff. They correctly concluded that their nest must be there, though they could not see it. They therefore lowered one of their companions down the cliff on a strong rope to search for the nest. For a long time he felt around the exact spot he knew, until at last he held a strange stone that he had not noticed before. Suddenly the nest became visible. The stone had lain in its very center. But there were no longer any eggs or young birds in it. The Älpler put the stone into his trouser pocket and gave the signal to haul him up. His companions pulled; they felt the weight on the rope but saw no one hanging from it. As soon as the young man reached the top, he began to speak. But not a soul could see him. Everyone thought he had met with an accident and that it was his spirit speaking to them. Terror and confusion held them until it finally occurred to the invisible man to throw the stone away. And now he stood before them in the flesh, and everyone breathed a sigh of relief. But the hat into which he had thrown the magic stone had disappeared.
