Old people who saw him here consistently describe his appearance as follows: he is a small, thin little man wearing blue trousers, a light-green tailcoat, a gray felt hat, and very wide, large riding boots. His voice is highly disagreeable, resembling the shrieking of birds and the whimpering of a night owl. Boys relate that when they prepared to lift their bundles of gathered wood to carry home, the loads often seemed to rise onto their backs by themselves; but when they looked around for the cause of such unexpected help, they would always notice some trace of Stiefeli as he vanished again, or hear a horse’s neigh, hoofbeats, or bright laughter.", "He even lifted one old woman’s bundle of wood onto her head, and to water his horse at the well, he does not hesitate to ride down among the people whose homes stand around the forest edge. He steps into their path, bars their passage, and drives them through the woods until morning. A butcher’s apprentice from Mägenwil had remained at the inn until evening and did not set out across the Maiengrün toward Hägglingen until night began to fall. Upon reaching the height, he encountered a rider standing across the path and could find no way around him. At last he reached for his stick to force a passage. Then horse and rider visibly grew taller, and the rider’s eyes began to glow like burning coals. The butcher’s apprentice fled.
