Above in the Steinberg is a rock cave that was the dwelling of such Bergmännchen. Among them, one in particular showed itself eager to help. In summertime, it frequently stopped at the Sennhütten of Obfluh and Rütimatt and gave the Älper various kinds of assistance tending the livestock, gathering hay, and the like. In return, they hospitably entertained it with Älplerspeisen; in short, it was the trusted hut companion of the Älper, but it always had its own seat. At Obfluh, they could even be absent and leave the care of the livestock entirely to the otherwise wild guest. Yet whenever they departed, it set them the cheerful condition that if the Föhn were approaching, they must return to the Alp without delay to care for the livestock, for in that event, it would not be at their service. — In the already mentioned Rütimatt, however, mischievous people played a prank on this good Manndli. When they once saw it coming down the steep rock slope, they quickly heated the stone on which it usually sat in the hut. It came and sat upon the strongly heated slab, immediately went away, and never appeared again.", "b) In Dallenwil, at the homestead there called the Tableten, another such Bergmännchen also appeared, especially at the time when the hay was gathered. It then appeared with two Haselzwicken, or hazel rods, stepped into the lying hay or Emde, and beat mightily around itself with these rods. At this, the hay began to rise from the ground, moved in a swirling circle, gradually approached the Gaden—the barn—and flew with all force into its openings, or Schwemmen, on every side, while behind it, the Manndli continued striking hard at the flying hay. After the work was done, and at other times too, the people of the house gave their skillful helper food.
