By the time it is finished, he will surely have gone so that I can close the chapel.” But when I had prayed that too, the stranger still would not move. I now stepped a little closer and ventured to address him softly: “Good friend, it is already late; I should like to close the chapel!” No answer. I repeated my address once more. No answer. I thought perhaps he did not hear well, stepped closer, and touched him ever so gently while repeating, “It is already late now; I must close the chapel!” Then it sounded as if someone had poured a Fischel of peas down from the vault over the prayer benches.
