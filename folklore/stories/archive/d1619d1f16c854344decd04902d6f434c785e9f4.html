If their herdsman does not call them back with his true voice, they go away and cannot be found. Three days later, however, they reappear on their usual pastures on the Alpen with full udders. Often too, especially at night when storm, rain, and hail have set in, such unrest seizes them that they flee madly over stick and stone and no call of the Sennen can bring them back. This sometimes happens even by day, though no outward cause can be discovered. Then even the deepest abyss does not stop their course: in blind haste they rush toward it, make one leap, and lie shattered below. Once, when such madness came over his herd, a daring shepherd boy is said to have caught hold of the tail of the hindmost cow and disappeared with her. After three days he returned with her to the Alp.
