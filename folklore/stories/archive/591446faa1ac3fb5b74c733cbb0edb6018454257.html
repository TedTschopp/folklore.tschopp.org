A man who had been a herdsman there once told me: I do not know whether everything people have related about the Bozo is true, but no one can argue me out of this one—the Sennu and I heard it clearly. First, something kept rumbling about at night; now it tapped on a vessel, now on the Senntum-Chessi, as though with a little hammer. At last it got among the cattle, so that they began to bellow and make a din as though everything were being driven over and under everything else. Then the Sennu seized an axe in anger, ran in front of the stable, and swore and cursed so horribly that it made one shudder. “Praying, I think, does no good here,” he cried. “If you can be helped, then be helped, even if it costs me everything; but if you cannot be helped, may the living Devil carry you to the lowest Hellblata, so that we may have rest and peace from you here!” Then it shot past him like a fiery torch.
