Many a hunter had already lain in wait for it, but it had escaped every one. Then two men from Bristen once again set themselves the task of killing it. They lay in wait in a fir tree. It came, coming close to the fir. One hunter raised his gun and aimed. But when he looked out over the gun, the chamois had vanished, and in its place lay something that looked like a white surplice. He put the rifle down, and the chamois appeared again. The same thing happened to him a second time, and he said to his companion, ‘You shoot!’ But the other had the same experience: as soon as he aimed, the animal vanished and the enigmatic surplice lay there. Now the first man took up the rifle again and boldly shot at the surplice. Both climbed down from the tree and searched, but they found nothing. Only on the Waldblacken did they see drops of blood.
