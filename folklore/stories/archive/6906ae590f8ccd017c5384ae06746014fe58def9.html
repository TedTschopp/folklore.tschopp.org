He was relieved and went to the barracks. The next morning he told a woman friend how he yearned for the Alps and described his native village to her. She listened with a smile and replied, “I know Haldenstein as well as you do, for only last night we danced at Lichtenstein, and tonight we are going there again.” The soldier stared at her. “If you want to come, you may; we shall fetch you.” Since he was free from guard duty that night, he accepted the proposal. Sure enough, toward midnight the woman arrived with a companion, and they brought a tub with them. “S'isch Zit,” said one of them. He let them blindfold him and sat down in the tub as instructed; the two women lifted the tub and—away it went—through all the airs—straight toward Lichtenstein. He was set down where Lichtenstein Castle stands, and from there he could watch all night as a host of witches danced so wondrously on the green ground below him and the music played so beautifully. But throughout the night he was not allowed to utter a single word.
