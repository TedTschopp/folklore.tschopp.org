But a dreadful clamor will precede me, so great that the ground on which I stand will tremble.” The farmer promised to do this, and the spirit vanished. The next day, the farmer went to a confessor and asked him whether it was permissible. The confessor readily supported him in the undertaking and entreated him earnestly not to let himself be frightened. The farmer went to the appointed place where the spirit was to appear to him. He arrived there at night. Suddenly he heard dreadful howling and cries of lamentation all around him; his hair stood on end at the fire-breathing devils, which threatened to drive their claws into his body. Fear and horror seized him. He forgot the promise he had given; sense and courage dissolved into nothing. He ran off, stumbling over stick and stone, so that he neither knew where he was nor noticed that his leg had been badly injured.
