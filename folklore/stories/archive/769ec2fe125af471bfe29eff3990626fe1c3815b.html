After the storming of the fortification at Pfin, when the enemy had already advanced as far as Visp, a large body of them is said to have gathered near the Rittikapelle to rest and strengthen themselves with wine. Then an exceedingly foolish farmer from Brig, raging with hatred of the French, fired a musket across the Rhône into the mass of soldiers. As a boulder falling into a calm lake throws it into turmoil, so that its waves rush furiously apart and together again, the mass of Frenchmen opened and closed once more with a terrible roar. The beating of the charge, the seizing of weapons, and the rush forward at full speed were the work of a single instant. But this was a great misfortune. Numerous old, exhausted, and lightly or severely wounded Valais men were on the main road. They could have escaped the enemy had this murderous shot not been fired.
