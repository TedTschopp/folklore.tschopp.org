Their assistance was as useful to the fieldworker as their skill in kitchen and household was welcome to the housewife. For this reason, they were treated just like the closest members of the family. He had also ordered cakes baked, and their fragrance drifted so sweetly up the lane that even the little men developed a craving for them, for, as is well known, they understand such things best.", "And so they accepted the miller's invitation. For that reason, before they appeared that day, he had ordered flour and bran strewn across the floor from the door to the large round slate table. Krautwähen and Ramwähen, bacon cakes, and Zipärtlikuchen had been served, so large that no platter could be found for them—all particular favorite foods of the little folk.
