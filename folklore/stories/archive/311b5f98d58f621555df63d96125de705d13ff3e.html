At other times terrible storms broke out, and lightning darted about the steep walls like snakes and killed the wanderers. Often, even when everything seemed calm, a cricket needed only chirp on a strip of grass or a little bird sing, and avalanches swept down over the Rissenen, Runsen, and rocky slopes, wiping everything then on the narrow mountain path into the depths. Everyone therefore dreaded setting foot upon the dangerous path, since it was so accursed and every earthly means of making it safer proved useless.", "The afflicted people, who nevertheless needed to use the evil path, conceived the idea of building a chapel at its worst point and placing an image of Mary inside, so that no one would forget that beside the evil path there was a helper upon whom one could call in distress.", "But who would carry out the dangerous work and build the chapel at that place in the path where even a chamois stepped cautiously? For a long time, despite every handsome offer, no one could be found who dared risk his life for the good of the land. The necessary rocky path could not be traversed. At last, however, several courageous men came forward to attempt construction of the chapel. After devoutly attending a holy Mass, they set out well equipped with everything necessary, climbed to that evil place in the path, and began building the chapel. With horror they looked into the abysses beneath them, which seemed like gigantic lindworms opening their jaws to swallow them.
