The Venediger is said to have left early each morning and searched for treasures in the mountains. In autumn, the Venediger richly rewarded the Älpler and then returned home with his treasures. After several years, curiosity and greed drove one of the Alpler to follow the Venediger secretly. He arrived just as the man brought a little jug full of gold out of the hole in the rock. From then on, the Venediger never returned. But the Alpler found neither gold nor any other treasure. ", "J. B.
