Jahrh.)", "Frau Baumann-Dubacher, 85 J. alt" ], "english_paragraphs": [ "At Häggrigen, a mother was busy melting down a Chessi full of Anken. It had already begun to liquefy when a strange old Müetterli entered the kitchen with her hair loose and hanging down over her head and begged for some Anken. “Do you have a little vessel?” the mother asked, and the stranger said she did not. “Then I cannot give you any Anken,” came the reply. “We have too few dishes.” The unknown woman moved a little to one side and, after some time, left the house through the back door. Now the Anken—not the fire—began to pop, crackle, crash, and whistle in a supernatural manner. The Anken suddenly rose in the Chessi, boiled over, and raged terribly, even after the fire had been put out. The mother poured some of it into a Mutte; it continued to boil and seethe in the Chessi and the Mutte. The haunting stopped only when she put something blessed into it. More than twenty pounds of Anken were lost that time. Afterward, people learned that the old woman had been a witch. The cantonal policeman seized her.
