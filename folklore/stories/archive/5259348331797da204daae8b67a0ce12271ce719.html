Yet my grandfather told this as certain truth: They lived in the Siëssbergen above Schattdorf. Their 4–5-year-old little boy often stayed away for entire days. When they asked him in the evening where he had been all day, he said, ‘With the great man.’ What did he look like? ‘A great, great man! He has no head, but he wears a great, great hat.’ In the past they always said that specters had no heads. Once, after a long, long search, they found the little boy sitting in their meadow and playing beside a great, mighty heap of snail shells. Another time they sent an older boy to fetch the goats. But after a considerable time, he came running back with every sign of terror and said that the great man had met him on the little footbridge. Yes, Grandfather told that story, and otherwise he was not a credulous man. Once we too missed a little girl and searched through the entire house. After a long search—where did we find her? Asleep on the chamber stairs, where we had walked up and down several times while searching. And yes, what happened to Professor Dittli in the Maderanertal when he was still a little boy?
