But an evil doom lay upon it. Every farmhand the owner had sent up had disappeared without a trace after a short time. Thus the splendid Alp gradually fell entirely into neglect. At last another fearless man presented himself and offered to serve as Alpknecht. He was hired with joy and soon climbed the mountain with a magnificent Sennten. After some time, a mysterious man came in the night and motioned to the new farmhand to accompany him. The farmhand followed. Outside, the spirit showed him a little spot and said that a great treasure was hidden there. He offered him a shovel and a Grebel and told him to dig. But the farmhand refused, saying, ‘I buried nothing, and I shall dig nothing up.’ Joy shone in the stranger's eyes, and he himself dug diligently for the treasure. After a good while, three large, mighty Häfen full of gleaming gold appeared. The farmhand had to take charge of them. From the money in one, he was to have holy Masses celebrated for the repose of the spirit's soul; the second Hafen-full he was to distribute among the poor; and the third he was permitted to keep for himself. He promised to carry this out faithfully. The spirit now thanked him joyfully and said, ‘Now I can be redeemed. Know this: had you touched the shovel or Grebel, I would have crushed you to dust and ashes like your predecessors.’ Then he vanished. Rejoicing and shouting, the farmhand left the Alp; he no longer wished to remain in service.
