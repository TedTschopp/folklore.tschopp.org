Albin Gnoss of Bristen had often seen a black cat on the wooden railing of the valley bridge in the Maderan Valley while going z'Stubätägah. Wagging its tail in a friendly way, it escorted him across the bridge. Once he had crossed, the animal made off and disappeared into the nearby lime or smelting kiln at Obermattli. One night, when he was returning home from military service and crossed the bridge between 11 and 12 o’clock, the cat was sitting at its post again. Albin quickly seized his bayonet and severed its tail from its body with a powerful blow. With a dreadful howl of pain, the animal fell into the stream. The traveler put the bleeding tail that had fallen onto the bridge into his pocket. The next morning he wanted to examine it more closely, but instead of the cat’s tail he pulled from his pocket the end of a woman’s skirt!
