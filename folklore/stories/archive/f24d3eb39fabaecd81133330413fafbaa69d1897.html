At night the Sennen hear a voice like that of a Senn calling the cows. Led by the Meisterkuh, the cows immediately follow this voice. They rush madly into the distance, no one knows where. If the herder does not call the lead cow back again in his true voice, the entire herd departs and cannot be found. When this happens, the Senn must perform every task in and around the stable as though nothing had occurred. Three days later, the herd reappears on its usual pastures, well fed and with full udders. It sometimes also happens that every cow brings a calf back with her. Sometimes a little dwarf drives the scattered animals back onto their alp. Once, when the cows rückten, a daring herdboy is said to have hung onto the tail of the rearmost cow and vanished with it. After three days, however, he returned safely to the alp with that same animal.
