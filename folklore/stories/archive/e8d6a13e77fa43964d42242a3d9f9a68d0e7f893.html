They did not associate with the other inhabitants of the land and lived in quiet seclusion in the most remote forests and rock caves. They were seldom seen, and whenever this happened, they sought to escape the stranger’s observation as quickly as possible.", "Their clothing was extremely scanty and usually consisted only of animal skins and other simple natural materials. The people of Flums had compassion for this half-naked but peaceful little Alpine folk. They therefore often placed better garments as gifts within its territory. The wild people took away what was offered as soon as they believed themselves unobserved, dressed in it, and seemed pleased with it.", "Once, during persistent snowy weather, people managed to catch a wild girl. She was then brought to Portels on the Kleinberg at Flums and placed in the care of an honorable farming family. There she soon grew accustomed to the members of the household and their circumstances and gradually served the family for several years as an intelligent and faithful maid. She never said anything about her people, admitting only that her name was Ruchrinde.", "During her stay in Portels, a plague-like disease arose among the people. It struck so violently that many families died out completely. No one died in the family with whom Ruchrinde lived. The wise wild woman explained that this was because she always fetched only perfectly clean water for household use from the nearby brook. At certain times, as in the other brooks, it contained much unhealthy spawn (eggs of aquatic animals) that must not be used for human food and drink.
