They sought out the spot and found a large, spacious cave called the Sandbalm. Inside it was an enormously large, magnificent black crystal worth many thousands of francs. Full of joy, they returned home, fetched tools, and set to work extracting the crystal. A strange rushing sound filled the cave. In their arrogance the three companions said to one another, “Now we no longer need the Lord God’s light!” They sealed the cave entrance with boards and blankets and prevented the bright day from looking inside. Once they were absorbed in their work, the rushing grew louder; a hideous clatter arose at the entrance to the Balm, and something rattled and tugged at the boards. “Who is there?” the workers cried. “No one has any business or authority here; we are masters here!” And they continued hammering and picking. Again came that mysterious rushing, and before the cave it rumbled and crashed as though a hundred boulders were rolling toward them. Terror closed the mouths of the insolent Strahler so that they no longer spoke a word to one another. At last they mastered themselves and sought the entrance. But it was now blocked high with stones and rubble. They were unable to move the oven-sized boulders, and no one came to help because they had kept their expedition secret. The three companions never saw the Lord God’s light again and starved miserably in the cave.", "Jos.
