The Holz-Nann lived nearby at Risershus; she spent more time in the forest than at home. She caused a great deal of mischief. She often bewitched the children so that they could not find their way out of the forest until it was dark night. Many a man sometimes found himself unable to walk any farther, though he himself did not know why. The neighbors lamented that their cows had been bewitched; in short, everyone feared her. Once, music was being played at the inn at Risershus, and it drew the Holz-Nann completely out of her little house. Then she had the mad idea of hanging herself from a thread. She tried it, and indeed it worked. One evening, she was buried up in the forest.
