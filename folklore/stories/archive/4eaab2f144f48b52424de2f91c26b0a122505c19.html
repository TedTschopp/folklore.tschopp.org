At last her parents noticed this, and one day her father hid at the corner of the house so that, unseen by the child, he could learn why she went out there every time. As the father lay in wait, he heard his child say to someone, “Oh! You are drinking and taking all my milk; eat some of the bread pieces too!” The words astonished the father. He leaned around the corner and saw in horror a serpent drinking milk from his child’s bowl. What he said to his little daughter and how he removed the snake, whether the child later still longed for the snake or the snake longed for milk, I no longer know precisely. Some maintain that, with her father’s consent, the girl continued to share her milk and bread with the snake every day; that she still did so as a young woman; and that at last, during her wedding feast, the snake entered the dining hall and laid a little golden crown in the bride’s lap.
