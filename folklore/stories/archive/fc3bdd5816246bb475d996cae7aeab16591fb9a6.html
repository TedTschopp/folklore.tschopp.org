Nevertheless, he went there once when the Gumpen held much water. Scarcely had he entered when he sank and was not seen again until his body was found several days later. From then on, his spirit wandered at that place. When questioned, he answered that he must wander for 50 years because his lifespan would otherwise have reached 70 years. People took pity on him and built a little house there to protect him from wind and weather. Not long ago, a young man passed the place and related in the village of Marbach that, outside by that Gumpen at the back among the Flühnen, someone had looked from a hole but given no answer to his repeated, lengthy questions.
