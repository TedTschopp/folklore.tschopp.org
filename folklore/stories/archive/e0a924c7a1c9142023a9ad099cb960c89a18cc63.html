It leaned motionless against a pine, and a broad, folded cloak enveloped it almost entirely. It appeared to face west toward the Nackberg. For a time, the two of us gazed at this figure; we saw clearly that it lacked a head. Terror then seized us, and in a wide detour we hurried across the height and down the other side to the nearest farmhouse.
