It is claimed that only a single man, a Frenchman who fell here in a duel, is buried there. In winter, a pig comes out of the Zurzach house called “Zum Rebstock,” goes around the block of houses, and proceeds straight here. No one knows what it does there. As the Zurzach night watchman said on the matter, no one has ever followed it, because this animal is difficult to deal with. Others believe that the French and Imperial troops who once fought one another in days-long engagements around Zurzach lie buried here.
