Walker, 72 J. alt." ], "english_paragraphs": [ "In the Fellibergen, in the municipality of Gurtnellen, stands the Waldbruderchäppeli. Beside it formerly stood the Brudergädemli, on whose site my forebears, the Riedmatterig, later built a large Gaden. In former times a Waldbruder lived there. He read holy Mass every day in the Chäppeli. He must have been a spirit, however, for no one could see him except his altar server. That was my forebears’ Geissbub. Every morning, when he drove the goats toward Fellenen, the boy served for him at the altar. But the Waldbruder strictly forbade him to tell anyone about it. Because the boy hurried so terribly every morning, however, the master’s household noticed, and at last they told him that if he did not reveal the secret, they would drive him away. He was a poor boy without parents, and so he confessed. When he returned to the chapel the next morning and the mornings after that, the priest had vanished; the candles were extinguished and only just still smoking.
