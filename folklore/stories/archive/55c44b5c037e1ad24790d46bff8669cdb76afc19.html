They therefore obtained a black cat that had not a single white hair either at the tip of its tail or on its breast. Carefully observing a variety of rites, they cut off its head, set the head upon a pole, and at midnight went with it to the place where the treasure was said to lie. They had all pledged themselves to unbroken silence and began to dig. The person who had to hold the pole with the cat’s head, however, found its length obstructive while digging, and briefly shared with his neighbor his sensible idea of sawing it shorter. ‘Hol ä’Säge’—‘Fetch a saw,’ he said. After that single little utterance, all further work was in vain.
