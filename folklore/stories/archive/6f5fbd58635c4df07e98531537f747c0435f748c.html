In late autumn, a hunter from the Schächental spent the night at Alplen, went into the hut, and cooked himself some warm soup. Then a thought came to him: “Well, you will go give a Jauchzer and see whether someone answers; it is terribly lonely.” So he went outside the hut and gave a hearty Jauchzer into the night. He immediately received an answer. He returned to the hut and sat down on a stone beside the fire pit. Soon, a hunter entered with a rifle over his shoulder. “Good evening!” the man from the Schächental said in a friendly manner.
