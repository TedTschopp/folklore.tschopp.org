No spirit will touch something once soiled, and thus the silver knife remained with the greedy young farmer. That had been his intention. The dwarf grew angry and complained bitterly about the sinful young generation. When his companions heard his lament, they swore never again to come into daylight. Together they hurried cross-country toward the forest and slipped down into their cave.
