At midnight, he awoke and heard wonderfully beautiful dance music. Undaunted, he left his bed and went to the door of the hut. What a sight! A short distance away, those present, both men and women, whirled to marvelous dance melodies that a musician seated in the middle of the circle drew from his instrument. A pretty girl approached the hunter and invited him to dance. He agreed to three dances; then the two went to one of the freshly set-up tavern tables, where food and drink were abundant. After the meal, he took his leave because he still needed to sleep for a while. The beautiful dancer slipped a sausage into his coat pocket. Now, at a short distance, he noticed his bride, whom he intended to marry soon. ‘You too are to be found among these damned souls!’ were the only words that passed his lips.
