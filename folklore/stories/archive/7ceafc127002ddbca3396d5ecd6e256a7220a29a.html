It had continual and undisturbed enjoyment and use of them. The territorial governments recognized the traditional rights of the four municipalities of Saas; even the French government did the same in the present century. — Thus the lively marmots whistled and grazed fearlessly and merrily on the high Alps of the Saas Valley, for the municipalities always treated them with tender paternal care.", "Powder and lead are never used, lest these beloved animals be frightened and driven from the rich grazing places; one therefore often sees them playing merrily in the grass among sheep", "and goats. Still less is the digging up of winter dens tolerated, because entire families are destroyed by it. Only at Ständern—winter resting places—known to be well populated are stone traps set in autumn through which the young can slip while the larger animals remain caught. As soon as preserving the family seems to require it, however, all are allowed to enter their winter quarters freely. Therefore a concerned Murmeltiervater, Johann Baptist Ruppen—who often in spring, when the snow melted too slowly, laboriously carried feed on his back to his beloved charges—dug up a young pair of marmots on another mountain, kept the sleeping young animals through the winter in a cool cellar, and in spring brought them to the empty Alp. There, well supplied with food, he introduced them into an abandoned burrow.
