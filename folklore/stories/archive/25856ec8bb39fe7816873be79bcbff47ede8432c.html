The business about which he had just been required to provide information was already vexing. Now he seized the nearest pitchfork and advanced in the direction from which the little dog had come, driving at the goblin so quickly that he nearly forced him out beyond the roof’s eaves. Here, for the first time, the spirit broke into humanlike weeping and sobbing and implored him for mercy.
