He remained undisturbed until he came near his house. Then he heard through the darkness the uncanny call: “Where shall I put it? Where shall I put it?” Franz thought someone wished to play a joke upon him and answered: “You fool, where you took it from!” Something shot toward him like lightning, and there before him stood a black man wearing a white pointed cap. Beside the man stood a Markstein. Now the late traveler knew with whom he was dealing. “All good spirits praise God the Lord,” he said, “and the first and last word shall be mine!” Then he asked the stranger where he came from. He even wished to offer him his hand but continually grasped only air. Then the stranger said that at this hour he had once moved a Markstein and therefore had to wander until someone compassionately set the stone back in the correct place. If that happened today, he would be redeemed; if not, he would have to wander on until a cradle was made from a fir sapling germinating at this midnight. The child rocked inside it could help him again once grown. The spirit could have talked Franz to death had Franz not reserved the last word for himself. The white cap indicated that the poor man could be released from his torment. Franz therefore went home, fetched a pick and shovel, and followed the uncanny figure to the indicated place. Scarcely had the stone been set in place when the apparition vanished and cries of joy sounded from the air. Franz admittedly fell ill soon afterward, but the redeemed man came to his deathbed and offered him his hand to lead him across into the land of eternal peace.\nAfter Dr. Henne-Am Rhyn, Deutsche Volkssage" ], "notes": [ "Swiss term “Der Markenrücker”: literally ‘the boundary-marker mover,’ a person condemned after death for shifting a property stone; the white cap signals that this particular spirit remains redeemable.", "Swiss term “Markstein”: a boundary stone marking land or property limits; moving one fraudulently was a serious violation of communal and property order and becomes the cause of the haunting." ] }, "legacy_urls": [] } ---
