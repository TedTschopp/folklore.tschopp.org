Just then, the boatman Joseph Zimmermann was also coming home from Säckingen in his Weidling. He saw the figure standing there and, without having much choice in the matter, resigned himself to the inevitable, rowed over, took the unwelcome guest aboard, and rowed back with him to the opposite bank. Because he already knew the manner in which fellows of this kind tend to take their leave, he offered him only the oar instead of his hand at parting, and in an instant all five fingers were burned gratefully into it.
