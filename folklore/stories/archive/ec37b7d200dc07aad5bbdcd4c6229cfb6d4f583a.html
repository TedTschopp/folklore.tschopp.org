The Karrhohlen owes its name, perhaps, to the folk legend that in ancient times this gorge served as a road when the knights of Schöritz—or, according to others, the devil and his retinue—came driving with horse and wagon over the ridges of the Hohgant to visit the lords of Ällgäu in their city encircled by ramparts. For where the poor Alpine huts of Bösälgäu now stand on a mountain terrace close to the highest saddle of the Hohgant, a city is said to have stood in former times.
