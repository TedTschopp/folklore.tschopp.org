The child searched for wildflowers and strawberries, which she joyfully brought to her mother, who had settled on a grassy place in the shade of a tree. Suddenly the mother heard a cry of fear. She leaped up and hurried to the place on the mountainside where she had last seen her child. A dreadful abyss yawned before her, but nowhere did she see the girl. In vain she called with cries of anguish into the horrifying depths; only the echo returned the unhappy mother's call. Entrusting her child to the protection of the Mother of God, she rushed down a steep path into the valley. But behold, what a wonder! Scarcely had the desperate woman reached the bottom when the child she had believed dead came joyfully toward her, her little basket full of strawberries, and related that a wondrously beautiful woman had caught her in her arms in the midst of the fall and set her lightly and gently upon the grass below in the valley. There she had picked the strawberries that she now wished to bring to her father.
