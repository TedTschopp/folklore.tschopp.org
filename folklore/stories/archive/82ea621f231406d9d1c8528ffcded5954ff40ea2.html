When she actually appeared at that place as arranged, the unknown man took her by the hand and led her up the steep Schlossfluh without the climb causing her the slightest difficulty. Upon reaching the top, she noticed by moonlight a large opening in the rock that she had never seen before. The stranger entered it with her, and after a few steps they found themselves in a large, spacious hall. It was brightly lit, and many pale and gloomy men sat around a table draped in black. One of them beckoned the maiden closer and ordered her to enter her name in a large book that lay open upon the table. Although the girl could not read what was written in the book, she realized that it was nothing Christian.
