The cave is inaccessible, and therefore no one knows whether the dragon may still be living inside it. Yet people claim that it was seen only a few years ago. It is said to have wings, and when it flies through the air, the fire it spits is said to illuminate the country for a great distance around. The population around Gansingen in the Fricktal commonly regards comets and dragons as much the same kind of thing and also maintains that the appearance of a dragon, like that of a comet, foretells death and war. Generalized as the Dorfthier, the dragon is still found in nearly every locality, each with its own particular local stories. Yet this monster shares with the dragons of other legends the traits that it usually lives only in the village stream; that its eyes are the size of plow wheels or meat platters; that its body is as long as a Bindbaum; that its gaze shines like church windows and carriage lanterns; and that its bulging eye is compared to a string of market onions tied together. This image has long been established in popular speech and has passed into rhymes printed in calendars. The Dorfthier has, for example, in Rud. Wyß, Schweiz.
