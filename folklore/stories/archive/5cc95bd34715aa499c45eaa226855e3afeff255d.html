One of them was especially unlucky and reached the point of staking his last pennies. Enraged, he cried while looking at what seemed this time to be an especially good play, “If I do not win now, truly, I will thrust my dagger into God’s body in heaven!” The others ought properly to have answered such dreadful blasphemy with harsh words, but they did not. They calmly continued playing, and behold, the blasphemer lost. Raving, he drew his dagger from his belt and hurled it toward heaven amid the most loathsome curses. It was never found again. Instead, three drops of blood, looking entirely fresh and red, fell from the air onto the gaming boards lying before the three men. At the same time a terrible storm arose. During it the Devil visibly came and seized the vile blasphemer to carry him away to his deserved punishment. Horrified, the others tried to wash the blood from the boards with water but could not. By order of the officials it was therefore taken to Willisau and preserved there in perpetual memory. The two other gamblers were then seized and taken to prison. One, however, fell beneath the doorway, was at that very moment attacked by millions of lice, and came to a miserable end amid the surrounding crowd.
