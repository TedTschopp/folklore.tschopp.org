In its deepest thicket, he dismounted to rest beside a rushing spring. Suddenly, a host of gray cats stood before him. The strange folk meowed and cried and pointed toward a half-hidden path, so the knight had to follow, leading his horse. The gray animals hopped, danced, and leaped ahead, showing the way and coaxing a quiet smile from the serious man. The peculiar guides moved and jumped through brush and thicket until knight, horse, and cats came before a shimmering castle upon a green hill. With ridiculous gestures, the troop of cats bade the foreign man enter the broad halls. He tied his horse to a pillar of marble and, still led by cats, reached a lofty hall where two extraordinarily beautiful cats—a white one and a black one—lay upon a magnificent throne. The other animals approached them with unmistakable signs of homage. The knight wished to address the strange occupants of the castle, for he well understood that something extraordinary was happening. But before he knew it, he found himself in another sumptuous chamber where an exquisite supper awaited him. He ate and drank his fill of the splendid dishes and the dark-red and golden-bright wines, then sought rest upon a silken bed in the nearby state bedroom, where he soon slept the sleep of the just. Before long, however, something tugged upon the silken cover. When the knight awoke, the black cat spoke to him as follows: ‘Several years ago, I was a mighty prince, the white cat was my daughter, and the gray cats were my court. An evil sorcerer whose wishes I had not obeyed came and transformed all of us into cats. But if you have the courage to climb tonight to that hill where the three golden crosses shine, fetch down the Zauberwurzel at the foot of the middle cross, and touch me, my daughter, and my servants with it, you will free us all. You shall have my daughter as your wife and rule my people with her. But I warn you of dangers.’", "The knight did not deliberate long. He reached the height where the three crosses stood and, with a courageous hand, broke off the Zauberwurzel while the mountain trembled in its deepest depths. When he descended again into the valley, every haunting had vanished. Before the castle gate, the cat prince and his vassals awaited him.
