He owned the Oberschwandberg, where things were very frequently stolen from him. He gradually grew tired of this and went to the local priest, a very devout gentleman, asking him to b'stellen—bind—the thief. The priest promised to do so, but only on condition that Brand send a farmhand up to the mountain holding every day, which the petitioner firmly and solemnly promised. For a long time the farmhand truly climbed the steep path each and every blessed day. But on one winter day, when the paths were covered with snow and ice and icy snowstorms raged through the valley, Landammann Brand said to the farmhand, ‘I shall not send you today. If there was nothing to find throughout all this time, there will surely be even less today.’ The next day, however, he sent him again. The farmhand now found a man in the kitchen of the mountain cottage who had packed a huge basket full of every kind of goods and stood there motionless. The farmhand approached him and tapped him on the shoulder.
