In it, he noticed a door for the first time, ran toward it, stuck his staff into the Letten found there, opened the door, and went inside. Farther ahead, he saw a second portal. He opened that as well and beheld three sleeping men. One of them raised his head, lifted his eyelids, and asked harshly: ‘What year is reckoned?’ The youth told him and heard the answer: ‘It is still much too early.’ The strange man immediately fell asleep again. At home, the herdsman, who had hurried away, recounted what had happened.
