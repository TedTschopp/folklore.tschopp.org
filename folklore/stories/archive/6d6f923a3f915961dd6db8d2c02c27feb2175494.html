There he heard a clear whistle. Thinking it was woodcutters, he whistled back. At once a whistle answered him, but it seemed to him much nearer than before. Still believing it was herdsmen or woodcutters, he gave another piercing whistle. The reply was so clear and strong that it passed through marrow and bone, echoed all around in mountain and valley, and seemed as though it were right beside him. When he came to the Staful-Türli, it was waiting for him. It was a Wättig’s, but in an ugly shape, like a little person with no head. As he went forward along his path, it always kept beside him and flamed a little, though not strongly. It went with him as far as the cross where he left the Alp path and took the new path toward his property. There it burned and flamed so strongly that for a considerable distance he could see the path clearly in the light.
