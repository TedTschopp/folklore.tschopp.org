On the left-hand side, as one goes farther back, there is a forest. Long ago, a pasture gate stood there. Around that time, a farmer rode back that way late one evening. Three times, a Chutz cried in the forest. “Huhuhuu!” the farmer mocked back, “welen eh bim Gattertöri—who is there by the little gate?” Then he spurred his horse. But that was unnecessary. The horse stretched into a gallop and raced back that way, sweat streaming down from it.
