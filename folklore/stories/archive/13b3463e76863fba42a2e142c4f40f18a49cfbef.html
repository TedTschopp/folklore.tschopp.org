For a long time they carried on their mischief, until at last a Capuchin friar came to the region who had learned to banish spirits at Salamanca. The people of Gsteig implored him to free them from their dangerous and uncanny neighbors. After long hesitation, the foreign Capuchin agreed to the dangerous undertaking and went to the place from which he was to banish the spirits forever. Standing on a limestone boulder, he began to hurl his formulas of banishment, holy words, and holy signs against the spirits. But they were not powerful enough against the satanic force. The spirits stormed wildly against the boulder on which the conjurer stood and tried with all their might to tear it out from under his feet. The devout father could scarcely hold his ground in the struggle; but his foot pressed down ever more firmly, boring deep into the rock, which had already begun to sway beneath him. Then—one more formula of banishment, the most powerful of all! And behold, the spirits retreated forever.
