It is an invisible, peculiar, enigmatic being—probably from the underworld—that respects neither sex nor age but seems to act entirely at whim. Here and there it attacks not only children, young men, and maidens but also men and women, even elderly people. These uninvited visits occur at night when a person lies asleep, specifically on the back. Those attacked are pressed down by an enormous weight, as though the greatest boulders lay upon them. The Toggi sits on the heart, and the helpless person, although able to feel and hear and seemingly awake, lies motionless and can move neither hand nor foot. Breath stops, and the victim is in the utmost danger of dying from the weight and breathlessness. Fortunately, it usually lasts only a few minutes. Once freed, the person starts up and draws breath, not rarely with a cry of terror. The heart expands, the chest swells, and the person then usually remains awake, restless, and fearful for several hours. Some people claim to have heard its approach, as if a cat were running swiftly toward them. If they still have time to turn quickly onto their side, this puts it to flight. Those moments must be used in haste, however, for in an instant they are attacked. People also claim to have seen it, in the form of a large marten-cat or an old marmot. Legend further says that one can prevent such troublesome visits by making proper preparations before going to sleep. Against this monster nothing holy is said to help—not prayer, the sign of the cross, or holy water—but only taking a well-honed saber or sharp knife into bed.
