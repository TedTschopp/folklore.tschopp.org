According to old tradition, the village of Vitznau likewise once stood somewhat higher toward the mountain cleft, with its houses much closer together. Once a fahrender Schüler, a traveling scholar, came there when a great dragon lived high in the mountain gorge and spread terror. He promised to remove the monster.
