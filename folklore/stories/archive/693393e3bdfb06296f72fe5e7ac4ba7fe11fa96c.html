Like a loudly grunting and rooting herd of pigs, it passes across the monastery field through the outer village and up the mountain toward Buttwil. Behind Geltwil it follows a hedge as far as the Schlattholz and continues across the high Lindenberg. Some young men from the village of Geltwil once saw it coming up the mountain, and one of them was persuaded to catch a little piglet running behind. His companions brought him a sack; he tied it inside, hoisted it up, and meant to carry it home. Then a voice called mightily down from the heights where the herd was just roaring away, “Hagöhrli, where are you?” To the young men’s horror, the piglet in the sack immediately answered, “Inside Heiniguggeli’s sack!” The carrier started and dropped the sack, then hurried away with the others. Passersby later found the sack, but it was empty.
