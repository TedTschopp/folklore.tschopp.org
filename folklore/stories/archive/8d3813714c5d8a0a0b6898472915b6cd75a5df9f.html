There, one friend once advised another that on the Holy Night of Christmas he should draw a circle around himself with chalk in the middle of the four-way intersection and stand within it from 11–12 o’clock. Three beings would then come, and if he did not fear them, he could have a great treasure and redeem the one who had to guard it. The friend, eager for the treasure, followed the advice. As he stood in the circle, one came; well, he was still bearable to look upon. The second to appear was more frightening.
