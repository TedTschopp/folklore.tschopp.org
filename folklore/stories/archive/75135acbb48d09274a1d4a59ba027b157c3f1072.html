In ancient times, part of an enormous city stood in this forest, together with a Zwingherrenschloss. The former owner of this castle still hunts through the woods with a terrible din. A white horse is also often seen there at night, and a rooster is heard crowing. No remains of buildings can be seen any longer; yet in more recent times, a great bell, now hanging in Herzogenbuchsee, was found there in a remarkable fashion. A goatherd was tending his flock at the edge of the forest. In high spirits, one goat once scraped away the loose soil with its feet, exposing something metallic. The herdsman saw it and reported the discovery, whereupon a mighty bell was dug out of the earth at that spot.
