Then three robbers seized their reins and ordered the traveler to hand over his money and luggage willingly. He thought of resisting and called upon the servant for help; but Hans remained sitting calmly on the driver’s box and went on smoking his little pipe, as silent and stupid as if he were at home and expected to help eat a bowl of white turnips. The traveler had to get out and could do nothing but surrender all his possessions to the highwaymen.
