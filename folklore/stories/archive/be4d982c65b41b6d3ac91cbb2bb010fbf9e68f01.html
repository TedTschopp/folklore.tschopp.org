A wildes Mannli asked for help for his wife, and it was granted. Along an entirely unknown path, they reached the cave after a short time. At daybreak, the woman took her leave and received as a reward an apron full of coals, which she accepted unwillingly. On the way, she threw away the worthless gift and carried only a single piece with her. Now she heard a distant call: ‘The more you throw away, the less you have!’ She turned and saw the Mannli, who had raised his forefinger and gave her a strange look. When she reached home, the supposed coal had transformed into a gold coin. Both spouses quickly set out to search for the discarded treasure. But they came too late, for the wildes Mannli had arrived before them, collected the coals, and carried them back into his cave.
