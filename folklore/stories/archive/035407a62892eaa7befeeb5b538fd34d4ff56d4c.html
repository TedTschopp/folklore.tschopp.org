Something red emerged beneath the white sleeves, almost like Stössli. They had rags tied around their heads, as I do, and their complexions were like other people's. That evening there was a terrible thunderstorm, and several Rübenen rushed down the valley and wreaked havoc until people began ringing the bells against the storm in the valley chapel. Yes, when the church bells ring, all storms must subside.
