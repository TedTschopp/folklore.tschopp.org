When they had finished eating and drinking, he asked his guest whether he no longer recognized the old Kräutermännchen in the gray coat who had always received a night’s lodging and food in his father’s house and who had carried a brown pitcher. He himself had been that little man. He would fill the pitcher only twice more, and then he would no longer need it. When the Venetian now noticed that this reminder of home had stirred homesickness in the poor boy, he brought out a Bergspiegel and, as a final kindness, showed him his parents and siblings at home in Weisstannen, just as they were gathered in the parlor for Chli z'Obet. The sight wonderfully soothed his homesickness.\nH.
