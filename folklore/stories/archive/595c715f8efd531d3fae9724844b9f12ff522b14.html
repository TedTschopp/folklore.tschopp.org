In a little transaction involving a meadow, however, he finally went too far. A widow had died and left several young children. Among her possessions, a beautiful, rich meadow was offered for separate sale, for people hoped that its considerable appraised value would pay for the orphans’ upbringing. But the Ammann’s intrigues contrived to have the plot appraised very low and then awarded to him. For many years he drew great profit from it. Yet only a few weeks after his death, people in Würenlingen claimed to have seen their deceased communal leader again at midnight on that meadow. He was said to rise from a small hollow in the middle of the property, his head bowed, walk thoughtfully around the spot, and after a short while disappear again. The report reached his two wealthy sons and all the proud family. They decided to go there together the next night, see the dead man, and, if possible, address him. At midnight they stood by the notorious hollow, and after only a few moments their father’s form rose before them.", "When he saw his two sons, he did not wait for them to speak but immediately began himself. The relatives listening were deeply moved, but not the two sons, who had grown up under the Ammann’s discipline.
