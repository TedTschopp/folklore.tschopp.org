One evening he stopped at a remote hut, where an old man received him hospitably. After various conversations, the old man revealed himself to the traveler as a man of the Oberhasli whom grief had driven from his beautiful homeland into foreign parts. His three beautiful daughters had been cursed. To this day, all three remain unredeemed from the enchantment and wander in the high Hasli mountains, where they often show themselves as apparitions. The Gauliweiblein dwells in the Gauli Glacier. Even today it sometimes appears to the herdsmen in the rear Urbachtal with its little dog. They then hear it say the words, “I und mi Kathrin und mini Chue Brün und min Hund Rhin müessen immer u ewig uf Blüemlisalp sin.” Now and then the bells of the invisible cattle are also said to be heard. Then the Engstlenfräulein wanders about the Engstlenalp. The young lady is seen very frequently by the herdsmen. Thirdly, the Geissmaidlein stays upon the heights of the beautiful Hasliberg and has surely lured many a solitary boy into courting. But once, when it meant to climb into a barn’s hayloft with a handsome, quiet-natured youth, it revealed a pair of goat’s feet. The frightened young man slipped away to the side, for after this sight he no longer felt safe. How this threefold enchantment may be broken is probably known only to the old man living abroad. Yet he has shared it with no one.
