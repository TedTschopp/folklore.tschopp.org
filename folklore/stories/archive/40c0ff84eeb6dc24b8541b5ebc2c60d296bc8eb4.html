Soon, however, people began to notice with annoyance that every morning the cow had less milk than could fairly be expected. At first they thought the cow was becoming ill and therefore losing her milk, but she always appeared lively and well. Beneath her it formed a ring on the floor and raised its head far enough to reach the full udder comfortably.
