He had a daughter who practiced charity solely from vanity and acted condescendingly out of pride. For this reason, she immediately agreed when a poor mother in Schöftland once won her as baptismal godmother. In a magnificent carriage drawn by four white horses, she drove from the Wartburg into the village of Schöftland and ordered the bells to be rung at once for the baptism. At the same time, she had endless lengths of fine red cloth unwound from her carriage and used them to cover the entire lane from the infant’s house to the church door. But all the village boys had already run ahead to the churchyard so they could watch more comfortably from atop its wall when the godmother, glittering in silk and precious stones, made her splendid entrance into the church. Consequently, there were not immediately enough hands to spread all the cloths over the road. About a hundred paces before the destination were still uncovered when the tired Sigrist in the tower stopped ringing.", "‘Then stop in the Devil’s name!’ the maiden said indignantly when the bells suddenly fell silent, for even today, it is considered a very bad omen for godmother and godchild to enter the church only after the ringing. At last, the people had to lift her into her carriage merely to move her from the spot. The child returned to its little bed unbaptized, but the godmother returned to her castle as a corpse. That black dog still walks through the village at certain times.
