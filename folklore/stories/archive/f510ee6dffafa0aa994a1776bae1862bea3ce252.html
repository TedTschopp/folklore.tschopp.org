The next morning, the woman was placed upon the rack a second time, and then confessed the following: “Eighteen or twenty years ago, at ès-Mollian, I met a great black man who scratched my head because I had cursed and to whom I promised submission and obedience. It was the Devil, called Gabriel, with whom I made a pact. Two years later, he came to my house and demanded lambs from me. I gave them to him some time afterwards, one above the village and the other above the town of Ruw; to catch them, the Devil had transformed himself into a long pole. Another time, the black man promised to make me rich, indeed very rich. He gave me much money, but it was mostly yellow, dried oak leaves, with only two good gold shillings among them. In Mollian I surrendered myself to the Devil by denying and renouncing God, accepting Gabriel as my lord and master, and paying him homage. After I had kissed his rod, he seized me by the nape and neck and pressed his claws into me.” At this dreadful confession, the Landvogt, Schöppen, clerk, and Weibel crossed themselves. Then, after being ordered to do so, Mia continued her confession of sins as follows:", "“At Granges,” the widow continued, “Gabriel gave me a powder for killing people and animals. The nocturnal witches’ dances, Schetta, took place in various locations, including the bridge over the Broye, Montet, Granges, Villeneuve, and elsewhere. At one of them, people danced in a circle around a bluish fire, where they ate roasted animals or drank must and brandy resembling a mare’s urine. Some witches and warlocks were masked. Two little devils tended the fire, hopping and leaping around it. Gabriel, the great Devil, demanded my children from me, but I refused them to him.”", "After these confessions, the witch was allowed to rest, but not for long, for she was placed upon the rack a third time with a weight of one hundred pounds, whereupon under the torment she confessed:", "“I also,” she cried with a groan, “killed a goat and a calf at Granges, a child at Stäffis-am-See, a goat and a cat at Villards-Bramaré, as well as several animals.
