Yet Gläusler really did have more than enough happen to him! One summer, on the first evening after they had driven up, he wanted, as usual, to go z'bättä riëffä—to call everyone to prayer. But that time he came back at once! Something simply would not let him call to prayer. It drove him as far as the hut, and he came properly flying in through the door. From then on, the herdsman went to call everyone to prayer. Each time he had to pass through a little door, and whenever he looked back, he saw a pitch-black figure standing beside it. That one would have been waiting there for him! It was better for Gläusler that he no longer went to call everyone to prayer: that black one, I believe, would have got him!
