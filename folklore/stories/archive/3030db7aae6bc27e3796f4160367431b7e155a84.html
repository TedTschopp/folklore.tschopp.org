He wanted to scream and could not; he wanted to move, sit up, and strike at it, but all his efforts were in vain. He was unable to bend even one finger. At last, the cat jumped down from the bed again and quietly slipped out through the door. Zurfluh, as the freed man was called, sprang up, ran to his family in the chamber above, and told them what he had experienced.
