The traveler asks in wonder about the origin of these streaks on the gray limestone face. Someone without more precise information will believe they are a reddish, iron-bearing deposit left by water that springs from beneath the earth's crust on the rock's crown and runs down the cliff. One of the Sennen hurried to the edge of the rock and blew the Alphorn for help so forcefully that his chest burst and he sank down dying. The blood flowing from his breast trickled down the cliff and colored it red, and neither rain nor sunshine can bleach or erase these streaks of blood.
