Birrcher in Laufenburg.)" ], "english_paragraphs": [ "Pasture boys near Vilmergen were tending their goats on the meadow slope toward the edge of a forest when they encountered a black figure beside a boundary stone and at once ran away in fright. The smallest of them initially called to his companions: ‘Stop, it is my Götti!’ But because they could not be stopped, he too followed them and at home related with great joy that he had seen his dear Götti again. The objection that the man had already died did no good. The boy stood by his assertion. So people considered helping the spirit find rest. They equipped the little boy with all kinds of consecrated objects so that he could return to that place in the forest on the following day. But he also had to take a Karst and a shovel with him. If the specter then behaved as though it wished to work in the ground, the child was merely to hand it these tools in silence. This is what happened. The Götti was already walking bent over around a boundary stone standing between the forest meadow and the neighboring property when his godchild arrived. He received the tools that had been brought, dug out the stone with them, and set it farther back in another place. When everything had been completed in the shortest time, he began: ‘Now I am redeemed, and you, my dear Gotte, will in return also be a child of blessedness after three days.’ The little boy died after three days.", "(The Frickthal relates the same event quite similarly, identifying its own location.
