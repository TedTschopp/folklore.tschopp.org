Huber." ], "english_paragraphs": [ "A sawmill has stood in the Aufeld since ancient times. A man once lived there who played music for the young people's dancing at Fasnacht and the Kirchweih. Because he always appeared with his fiddle on such occasions, he was called the Säge-Giger. But he was a dissolute fellow who often became drunk and then behaved like an irrational beast.", "His ugly conduct came to an abrupt end. Once, after making his dance fiddle sound into the early morning, he set out drunk for home. He strayed from the path, and in the morning he was found dead in the Sagebach. His limbs were frozen stiff, and the fiddle upon which he had fallen was smashed. He was buried without song or music in a remote corner of the cemetery, where otherwise only people who died by suicide were interred. But he found no rest in his grave. During the heiligen Zeit before Christmas, the lamenting notes of his fiddle were often heard. The Säge-Giger had risen and at midnight walked fiddling toward the Aufeld as far as the foot of the Rachlis and back again beneath Mosnang's old Dorflinde, where the fiddle fell silent and the fiddler descended once more into the realm of shadows.\nC.
