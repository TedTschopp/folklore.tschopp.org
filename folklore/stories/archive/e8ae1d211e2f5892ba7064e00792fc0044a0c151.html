Its late owner was in league with the Erdmännchen who had their dwelling there. They performed all sorts of services for him, gathered medicinal herbs, and gave him good advice in doubtful cases. Once he could not make the stone blasting work properly. Annoyed, he left the job and went away. Then, at home in the stable, something whispered to him from a corner: “Nimm numme es Tötzi Spinnhoppe und stoss sie is Loch.” The man immediately gathered a five-fingered handful of cobwebs in the stable, returned to the work, and pushed them into the previous borehole. And behold, this time the blast succeeded as desired; the finest ashlar piece of the stone wall lay detached in the quarry. Another time, he wished to do some friends a favor and show them all his Erdmännlein. A whole procession of comical figures marched through the room, though visible to no one but the master of the house. As the last and merriest of all passed him, it threw an entire handful of sand into his eyes, and the farmer had to treat and salve them for a long time.
