To obtain one, a person must go to the Herr Landammann and explain the facts. If the Herr Landammann finds that a genuinely urgent case exists, he permits the Gassengericht and immediately proceeds to form and hold one. This is done as follows: the Herr Landammann goes into the street with the petitioner and, walking forward, designates as street judges the first citizens whom chance puts in their way and commands them to come along. When at least eight judges have been gathered, although the number may rise to twelve, he stops, forms a circle around himself, and presents the urgent case to the Gassengericht formed in this manner for decision.
