Suddenly, one of them pulled her skirt up with both hands as far as modesty allowed and moved as though she had to wade through a mighty stream. Everyone laughed; a young lad walking behind the girls in particular nearly laughed himself to death. ‘What is wrong with you?’ her companion asked. ‘Are you not in your right mind?’ ‘Why, don’t you see the stream coming this way?’ ‘Oh, nonsense! Link your arm through mine.’ She did so, and now she could no longer see the stream. The other girl had a four-leaf clover beneath her hay, and magic and delusion can do nothing to anyone who carries one. The lad walking behind the two girls and laughing so hard was certainly a sorcerer.
