In gratitude for this important service, every evening the Sennen placed a bowl full of sweet, fresh Nidle, or cream, on the roof of the Stafel. The next morning, they invariably found it empty. He was there for the first time in his life and considered the spirit's herding office a foolish little tale invented from ignorance and credulity. He therefore resolved to test and mock the spirit; instead of fragrant cream, he put boiling filth into the bowl. At midnight, the snoring Sennen heard a dull, hollow voice that seemed to come down from the smoke opening and admonished the unbelieving cowherd to rise quickly and skin his seven cows. He paid no attention, believed someone was merely playing a joke on him, and peacefully went back to sleep.
