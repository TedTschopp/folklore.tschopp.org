He oppressed the land of Frutigen with unheard-of taxes. The Landleute therefore called upon Bern for help. When the knight saw the great number of enemies approaching, he despaired of a favorable outcome. In his distress he resolved to flee. To avoid betrayal by enemy scouts, however, he had a Rosskarren driven into the courtyard and loaded with scattered hay and straw. He then lay down upon the load and had himself covered with the same refuse. Thus the cart carried the hidden knight out of the valley, and the oppressor escaped pursuit. But the oppressed people took the Felsenburg without a stroke of the sword and destroyed it. Over in Valais, Imturn later oppressed the people in the same way as he had formerly oppressed those beyond the mountains. Vengeance then flared among the Valais peasants. They rose against him, and because this time he could not manage his escape, they seized him, shut him inside a barrel bristling with nails all around its interior, and rolled the hated man down the mountain inside it.
