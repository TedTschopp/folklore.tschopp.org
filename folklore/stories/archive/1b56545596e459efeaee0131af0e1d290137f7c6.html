A member of the Capuchin order did succeed, through the use of powerful means, in restricting the spirit’s sphere of activity and binding it fast within a single room. But every night a candle had to burn in this chamber. If this was done, the spirit not only refrained from all hostility and from disturbing anyone’s rest at night but even showed the greatest benevolence toward the castle’s inhabitants. The many servants whom the castle owner had to employ because of his extensive estates found every morning, whenever hauling was the day’s work, that the horses had already been fed and the wagons drawn out and harnessed — in a word, everything had been prepared so that they needed only give the signal to depart.\nC.
