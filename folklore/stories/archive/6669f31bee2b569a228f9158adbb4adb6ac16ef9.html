The wine must gradually have gone to their heads, and they agreed to play Cheigel, or skittles—but it had to be done in some unusual fashion. They halved Mütschi and set them upright; these were the pins, and for balls they used Zweupfünger. That is how some tell it. But others tell it differently again. In the room they put out the light. One man took a burning candle and moved it back and forth above the stove opening, so that the room was first bright and then pitch-dark. This was supposed to be the lightning; they imitated the thunder with the skittle balls. No one objected.
