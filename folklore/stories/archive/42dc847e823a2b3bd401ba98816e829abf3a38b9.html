Because he trusted no one from the lands of Baden and Aargau sufficiently, he appointed a man from Uri as steward of his farms. One winter night the two had again loaded a sleigh with stolen grain, and the steward was just carrying the final sack down behind the house when he fell down the stairs so unfortunately that he died on the spot. The miller left the rogue lying there and raced home with his cheaply obtained load. The next morning, a shoemaker from Rümikon found the dead man beneath his burden.
