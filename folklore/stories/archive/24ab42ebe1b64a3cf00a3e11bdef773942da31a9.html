This is a sandstone cave whose petrified formations are attributed to the Berg- oder Steinvölkchen. Dwarfs lived in this hole until the beginning of this century. They had beautiful rooms and all manner of precious things. They were very seldom seen by day; only at night did they emerge from their underground dwelling. Once, when travelers visited the hole, they threw stones down into the depths; after that, however, the dwarfs moved away and were never noticed again. Several men from Kaufdorf once lowered themselves on ropes, but their light went out in the lower rooms. Once, while the dwarfs still lived there, a farmer was plowing near that hole. The farmer and his servant suddenly smelled something baked. The farmer said, ‘A cake like that would do us good just now.’ They continued plowing, and when they turned and came closer to the hole, they saw a white cloth lying in front of the furrow. They went over and saw a magnificent cake, with a fork and a knife. They sat down and ate the cake; afterward, they folded the cloth neatly, placed the fork and knife with it, and continued their work.
