Often, when it rushes hurriedly through its favorite haunts—sunken lanes, timber chutes, forest clearings, or torrent gullies—the frightened traveler who encounters it can make out nothing clearly of its shape except perhaps its eyes, which look like two glowing coals. If it is teased or disturbed on its way, it clatters, rattles, and screams in so dreadful a manner that it chills one to the marrow. In Ragaz and Pfäfers, it likes to haunt the Fluppabach and is therefore called Bachgschrei there, while in Vilters it is called Bachgrääggi, in the region of the Walensee Gwääggi or Borzi, and in the Rhine Valley Wüetihöö.
