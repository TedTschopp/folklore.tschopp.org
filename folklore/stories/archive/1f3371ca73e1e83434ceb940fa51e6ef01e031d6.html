He had many children and was an upright, faithful father, but had great difficulty supporting himself and his family with his work. He labored day and night, and there was no lack of work. Now and then, when he had something very urgent to do and could scarcely finish it at the proper time, he noticed that when he rose early in the morning to complete it, the clothes already lay on the table fully sewn, ironed, and brushed. He could not understand how this happened. When he was once again hard-pressed by his work, he resolved to keep watch and see whether someone came and did it for him. Behold, in the middle of the night he noticed a pair of little men creeping inside, sitting down on the table, and sewing one garment after another with quick, skillful stitches. He watched them heat the iron, press and clean the clothes, and fold them until everything was in order, and then slip very quietly outside again with contented faces. The good tailor felt endless joy and pondered how he might show them his gratitude. Being a tailor, he had looked closely at their clothing and noticed that all the little men were poorly dressed. When he had time, he made a number of tiny little garments, of a size he thought would fit the dwarfs, and laid them ready at night as a gift for their secret services. They did indeed come again, but when they saw the clothes, they knew that they had been observed, and this offended them.
