When they finally steered their little boat toward ‘Uren,’ Uri’s strongest champion, the Föhn, plunged roaring and thundering into the lake and hurled stormy waves against them. Although the people of Schwyz nevertheless reached Altdorf, the last hour of the fixed term had elapsed, and Uri’s Seckelmeister refused to accept the redemption payment.
