But with what can the French republicans possibly excuse the atrocities they committed against a small people who were likewise free? After Valais had mostly been conquered and a pardon had already been proclaimed, a French officer and his beautiful young wife went walking above the village of Naters one morning. Suddenly a musket shot cracked in the vicinity of the Klosi, and the beautiful woman, struck mortally, collapsed beside her inconsolable husband. No one was ever able to learn what foolish devil fired this murderous shot. One can imagine the agitation of this officer and his brothers in arms over so shameful a deed! Like lions unchained, they threatened these people with death and destruction. A terrible storm broke over the heads of these poor mountain dwellers. Robbing, murdering, and devastating, they swept across the whole mountain of Naters. At night in Moos, Geimen, Melbaum, and elsewhere, the heavens opened in flame and made everything for a great distance as bright as day.
