One evening, sleds guided by strong youths sped swiftly down the slope near Waltensburg; many a youth had his girl sitting behind him on the sled. A strange feeling suddenly came over the youngest and last in the procession. He turned around—and behind him sat an unfamiliar maiden of wondrous beauty. For a long time he did not dare address her. At last he took heart and asked the stranger who she was and whence she came. She was the Burgfräulein from Jörgenburg across the way, the lovely maiden replied. For many, many years she had lain there under enchantment, and she could be released only by a young man who endured within circles she would draw until the morning bell rang.
