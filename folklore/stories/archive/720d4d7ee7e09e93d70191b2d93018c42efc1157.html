Strange things are said to have happened when Raster died. Raster had a brother-in-law who envied him and especially begrudged him his great name. He therefore resolved to kill him, by poisoning him with a diamond, which he considered the surest means for this purpose. The plan was carried out. Raster took the poison but immediately realized where it came from and who had given it to him. He asked for chalk and drew on the wall a picture of his brother-in-law, who was not present. When this was done, he asked for bow and arrow and shot the arrow into the picture’s heart. Behold, at the same moment the brother-in-law fell dead. The poisoned magician now asked to be left alone so that he could prepare an antidote. Everyone withdrew. He locked himself in his room and began his magic arts. But curiosity and concern drove the neighbors to watch through a crack in the room’s wall. Whether from terror at what they saw or for some other reason, they burst open the door, whereupon he cried to them in alarm, ‘You have killed me, friends!
