He was a man of gigantic stature, for his height was more than twelve Fuss. His strength was so tremendously great that he could uproot trees and crush stones with his fingertips. Yet he combined this extraordinary strength with a great love for the people of the surrounding region. He often left the dark woods to give them proofs of his affection. He frequently went out to them in the fields and offered his hand in friendly greeting. But despite every expression of friendship, no one dared offer him a bare hand in return, for fear of his extraordinary strength. Instead of a hand, the farmers extended the handles of their plows, which always retained the visibly impressed marks of his mighty handshake. When he felt death approaching, he dug his own grave, lay down in it, and died there. After his death, his sister, who lived with him in these woods and was almost as tall and strong, fell into deep mourning. She covered his body and carried two very large granite blocks in her apron, which she set upright on her brother's grave as a lasting memorial. When she too felt her end approaching, she dug a grave for herself about two paces from her brother's resting place and brought two large stones as well. After her death she was buried in it, and the two stones she had brought were erected upon it. Even now, the two collapsed graves can be seen in the Grauholz forest with their memorial stones upon them.", "Excavations there uncovered a gigantic skeleton.
