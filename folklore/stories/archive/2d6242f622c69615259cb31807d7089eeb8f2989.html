It was so dark and misty that I saw the rider only as a shadow; the horse stepped so softly that I could scarcely hear it, and it seemed so uncanny and ghostlike that I did not dare speak a word — yet I followed her without fear. The road seemed unknown to me. At last we arrived before a great house whose courtyard swarmed with horses, coaches, wagons, and servants. My rider was helped from the horse. A servant went ahead, and she beckoned me to follow. In the lower rooms and corridors of the great house it was dark and silent, but higher up the passages and stairways were illuminated as brightly as day, and a lovely fragrance of ambergris wafted toward us from the dining hall.
