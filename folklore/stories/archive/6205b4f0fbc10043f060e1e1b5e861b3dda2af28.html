The knight hurled his spear with all his might into the open jaws of the fire-breathing monster, but the iron spear shattered like a weak stick. Pouring out streams of flame, the serpent coiled upward and was about to cast itself upon the knight and crush him when he ordered the fox to run. At this the serpent moved backward. The knight used that instant to leap quickly, cover himself with his shield, and, invoking the name of God, thrust his broad, double-edged battle sword into the serpent’s heart so that it collapsed without a sound.
