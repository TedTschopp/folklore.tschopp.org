In ancient times, the witches of the surrounding region are said to have held their gatherings there. A merchant from Zuzwil who dealt in linen was riding home one evening from the market in St. Gallen when he saw this place brightly illuminated. A great many men and women sat there at richly laden tables, eating and drinking and making merry. They invited him to join them. Out of curiosity he rode closer and watched their activity for a while. Then he saw that every kind of good food had been served but that bread, the principal part of a meal, was missing. The merchant remembered that he still had a penny roll, or Bürli, in his coat pocket. He took it out and threw it onto one of the tables. At once the whole company scattered, and it became pitch-dark. The merchant now recognized that this had been a gathering of witches, devoutly crossed himself, and rode swiftly home.\nG.
