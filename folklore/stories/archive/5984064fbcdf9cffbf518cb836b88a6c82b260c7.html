The measures taken thus far to restore public safety had not been sufficient. Then Herr Kaspar von Stockalper is said to have resolved on his own initiative to undertake a daring exploit. He disguised himself as a filthy beggar and fool, went by night through the dangerous forest, and allowed the robbers to capture him so that he could discover their plans and hiding place. He played his role so well that they did not suspect him of being a spy. With wild delight and laughter, the robbers searched him and set him at their fire as a spit-turner. Because he often turned the roast backward instead of forward, the robbers reproached him. The disguised spit-turner replied, “Things do not always go this way—that is, forward.
