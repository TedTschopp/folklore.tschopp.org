A toll collector appointed by the holy fathers carried out this office.", "The legend relates that one such toll collector was approached late one evening by a maiden on horseback who wished to cross the bridge. When the maiden saw how useless it was to waste further words, she boldly spurred her horse to a daring leap and plunged from the bridge into the river so that she might reach the opposite bank and her destination, the grace-bestowing image of the Mother of God.
