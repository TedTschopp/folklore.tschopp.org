He also let it be understood that he knew how to conjure the Devil. Thus, at Sursee he found an honest fellow who prepared to ride with him. He promised him six Batzen and gave him three in cash toward the wage. They rode out of town by night, passed Sempach, and came to Truttenbach, where there is a farm called Truttingen. The servant rode ahead through the stream while the Kurtisan remained on the farther bank and called to his companion, saying: ‘Halta halta!’ When the servant looked back, perhaps fearing something had happened to him, he saw horse and man rise into the air above all the trees, bushes, and stumps.
