In autumn, a St. Antönier’s mare named Bläss escaped from the Alp pasture with her foal and could not be found despite every search. Bläss is a name given to horses, cattle, dogs, and other animals marked white from forehead to nose.—The following spring, a traveler crossing the pass saw Bläss and her young in a sheltered hollow beside the path to Montafun. They had wintered well, and he brought the animals’ owner this joyful news.
