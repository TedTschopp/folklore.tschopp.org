At first she was turned away, but then the household yielded to her urgent pleas and took her in. When she set out again the following day, she thanked her host profusely, wished the newborn child happiness and well-being, and urged everyone in the house to take especially good care of the child. For a dream of a tall forest fir that she had had there that night sadly indicated that this poor child was destined to hang herself once she reached twenty years of age. “Yet this misfortune can still be averted,” the woman concluded, “if, from the child's first speech and play onward, you carefully accustom her to begin everything in the name of God.”", "The family did not delay in faithfully following the old woman's advice. Thus, the child was raised in the fear of God and grew into a beautiful young woman. They had never let her go alone or unsupervised across the fields, nor to church without a companion, and her nineteen years of life had passed without mishap. Early in the morning on her twentieth birthday, her father woke his child and told her to rise and dress so that she could leave the house with him before anyone else had awakened. His daughter was to spend that day with him high on the deserted mountain, where neither company nor a visit from an acquaintance could occasion an unforeseen danger.", "The father put wine and bread into his shoulder bag, and the daughter carried the basket. Together, in the morning, they walked up through the mountain meadows into the forest. The girl was full of high spirits over the unexpected outing. But in the middle of her leap, the girl turned around. “Oh, Father,” she said in amazement, “now I can no longer do it!” The man understood these words and silently thanked God in his heart. The entire day passed without accident; only then was his daughter safe. (Through A.
