The sled also seemed to fly rather than glide, and it appeared to them that they traveled very far and high. In mortal fear, they finally vowed to found a little chapel at home if God delivered them from this distress. At this, all consciousness left them. When they came to themselves, they found themselves lying somewhere on the ground with the overturned sled beside them. Gradually, they recognized the region; they were at the bottom of the mountain upon which their homesteads stood above. Having returned home safely, they set about fulfilling their promise to God.
