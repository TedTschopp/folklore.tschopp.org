Because he had been born on a Quatembernacht, he could see every dwarf, poltergeist, and mountain spirit. His favorite usually stayed with him in his house: a small, tiny, ragged Schrätteli wearing a red cap. One evening, Dietrich warmed himself by the fire, and his house spirit kept him company. Out of mischief or misunderstood helpfulness, it mimicked everything he did. If Dietrich pulled a piece of wood from the hearth, a second followed as if by magic. If he put one in, another followed immediately. At last, this so angered the cooper that he seized a burning log and drove the mimic from the kitchen. The spirit bore a grudge for a long time and no longer showed itself. It is said later to have made peace with Dietrich once more, but the peace lasted only three days. On the fourth, they began quarreling in the barn over the Gaumen of the cows, and Dietrich had to flee from the malicious dwarf. As he leaped across the Tenn, the dwarf threw an iron pitchfork after him.
