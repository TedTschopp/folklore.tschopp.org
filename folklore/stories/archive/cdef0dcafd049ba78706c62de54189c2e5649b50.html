In idleness and play, they took it into their heads to baptize their billy goat, which was distinguished by its exceptional power and size. Immediately afterward, the animal became so uncontrollable and wild that it scattered and killed the herds on the Alps, put the herders to flight, and brought destruction as far as the valley. They were now advised to raise an ox and have a little girl give it its feed every day. When the child had reached her seventh year, she was to lead it up to the Alp on a thin thread. This was done: a pretty child was well brought up, and an ox was fattened to extraordinary size and strength. All the people in the valley gathered when, after seven years, the child led her astonishing animal up the mountain on a silken thread. Above, a battle with the billy goat now broke out, in which both animals died. From the slain ox's horns, however, people made Alphörner, and the German emperor had them bound in silver for the pastoral people. They were sounded in many a battle and were lost only in fighting against the Wälschen.
