People therefore devised means to exterminate the troublesome snakes. But no one dared undertake anything against these venomous creatures.—Then one man offered to hunt the snakes if he were given assurance that there were only black snakes among them and no white ones. Since no one claimed ever to have seen a white snake, the snake destroyer courageously set to work. He took an ointment, a pipe, and a long rod.—Sensing his arrival, the snakes grew very wild; they leaped about in circles, hissed shrilly, and spat venom violently. He would have been done for had he not quickly drawn out the strongly scented ointment, which stupefied the snakes, and blown the pipe, which made them hop toward him without wills of their own. He immediately beat them all to death with the rod or drowned them in the \"weissen Wasser.\"", "The defeat of the snakes was complete.
