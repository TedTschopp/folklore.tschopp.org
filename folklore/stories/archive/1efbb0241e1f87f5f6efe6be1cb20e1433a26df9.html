Chaplain Mooser relates the following:", "When I was still chaplain in Visperterminen in 1847, I retired later than usual one June evening because of business and went out onto the Vorlaube of the old chaplaincy house after ten o’clock to enjoy the fresh air. There, above the village in the meadows of Eggerberg, I saw a great light. I thought someone attending to the irrigation water had a light with him, but it immediately divided into many little lights that floated back and forth at regular distances. I called members of the household, who saw the same thing. At last the lights moved toward the Baltschieder ravine. The first vanished by bursting soundlessly into sparks of fire, while the others went out while still in the meadows of Eggerberg.
