An old firwood cross has been erected here with no ornament other than an age-blackened little crucifix image hanging at the center where the beams meet. The crossbeam is almost rotted through, while the upright, though newer, has likewise been pieced out and patched in several places.", "This partial renewal is the work of a greedy farmer who once dragged away the ownerless cross, began splitting the upright into small pieces at home, and fed it bit by bit into the stove.", "By doing so, however, he brought a series of misfortunes into his house.
