The ruins of his castle lie on the high Jura pass of Wasserfallen in Basel-Landschaft. Near Wasserfallen, people point out the Schelmenloch, a notorious mountain cave with robber legends of its own to tell. Every day Dietrich hunted across the Jura mountains, and no matter how weary he came home at night, even the pleas of his daughter Bertha could not protect the people from his fits of rage and abuse.", "On feast days, when the farmers of Reigoldswil—who at that time had no church of their own—went to divine service in the village of Bretzwil, he only shouted his halloo all the louder, loosed all the dogs, and galloped on his raven-black stallion down the castle slope into the unguarded fields. Horse and rider, stag and hound went trampling through the crops. A startled roe deer fled before him into the local Chapel of Saint Hilary, which stood open because the priest was just then celebrating the Jahrzeitenmesse. At the altar, the monk protected it from the onrushing pack of hounds until Dietrich entered and cried:", "Keep your place in heaven,", "Leave me my chase in the wood;", "If you let your bells blare,", "I’ll let my hunting horn be heard.", "And as terribly as he then blew his horn, so shrilly must he continue to sound it through the countryside to this day whenever a thunderstorm is near. Then young noblewomen are seen riding in six-horse carriages from the ruin down toward the church road from Titerten. In whole companies, in the bright midday of Good Friday, they promenade above, all people in ancient dress, and lay square gold pieces upon immense cloths in the sunlight. This happens beside that great rock from which the Reigoldswil midwife brings forth all newborn children.
