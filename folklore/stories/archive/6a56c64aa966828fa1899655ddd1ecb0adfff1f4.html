Hansli and Bethli were both thoroughly respectable people, and both could eat a thoroughly respectable amount of bread. But there was nothing Hansli liked eating less than the Rouft, and nothing Bethli liked less than the Mutsche. Conversely, Hansli was terribly fond of the Mutsche, and Bethli of the Rouft. For that reason they got along very well. Hansli was happy when Bethli ate plenty of Rouft, because then the Mutsche was always left for him; and Bethli was happy when Hansli ate the Mutsche, because then she got the whole Rouft. And so it went until, at the end of all endings, Hansli went to heaven. After that, however, Bethli suddenly had no one left to eat the Mutsche away for her. What was she to do? She simply took another husband, and his name was Jöri. Jöri and Bethli were both thoroughly respectable people, and both could eat a thoroughly respectable amount of bread. But oh dear! Jöri, too, wanted to eat nothing but the Rouft, and Bethli would not swallow a bit of Mutsche to save her life. Then neither of them would let the other have anything at all to eat, and at last they both died of Vergöustig.
