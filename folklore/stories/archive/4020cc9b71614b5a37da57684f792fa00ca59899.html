Well, the doctor said he should wait until morning; then he would tell him. For now he should go stay overnight in the village. The man set out, but on the way he began calculating what that would cost. The thought of the money troubled him, and he turned back to spend the night in the hayloft. But that night he did not close an eye and could not sleep. Suddenly, toward daylight, he saw a little light coming. It was completely dark, but the light came nearer and nearer and grew larger. He wondered where anyone could be going so late. But the light headed directly toward the house, and he heard someone coming along the path. Downstairs there was a knock at the house door. The door opened, and the doctor told someone to enter the room. Then the man heard them talking below. A man had come yesterday with pain in his stomach; what should the doctor tell him? He had sent the man into the village, and he would return tomorrow. The man had cat hair in his stomach; he should eat white turnips, and then it would improve.
