The Senn grew thirsty and went—the night was moonlit—to the nearby stream to refresh himself with its mirror-clear water. As he bent down, scooped the bright refreshment in his hat, and raised it to his mouth, a dazzling-white, delicate little bone gleamed up at him from the sand and stones of the streambed.
