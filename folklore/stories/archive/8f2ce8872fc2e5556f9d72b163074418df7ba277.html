At Easter, while it was still night, he rose and walked toward the little chapel on the Allmend to pray there. Near the Horw mill he encountered something that seized him by the hair and pulled hard. Although greatly frightened, he continued onward. The thing followed him as far as the Käpeli by the Gatter. There it seized him so that he could scarcely get inside the Käpeli. Then, when he came out, it followed him with a terrible noise, always on his right side, as far as the Bildstöckli on the Allmend. There he saw several little blue lights around him, which rushed and hovered about him as far as the Allmendkäpeli.
