The little man always came in first, then the little woman, and together they stepped up to the hearth. When they left, however, the little woman always went first, and with measured punctuality the little man lingered a moment longer before he too departed after his wife. This man was a Fronfastenkind and therefore could see all spirits and make them speak. He came to the notorious house, negotiated alone and unobserved with the little man, and then explained to the owner that the little man begged him not to have it ecclesiastically banished from the house, because that would delay its eventual redemption most of all.
