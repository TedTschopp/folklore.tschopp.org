After spending the entire day and part of the night trying to recover the lost path, and after resting a little, he intended to resume his way at daybreak. But the uncertain twilight in the ravine, while the day had not yet fully dawned, kept him from noticing a deep pit in the path. He plunged into it, yet because he fell upon soft clay covering the floor of the abyss, the fall caused him no injury or other bodily harm beyond that which usually arises from fear of possible destruction. When he had measured the height of the chasm and become convinced that he must despair of human help, he turned in fervent prayer to the Mother of God, asking her to free him from his distress.", "Deep passages and caves opened in the side walls of the pit. At their sight he was terrified almost to death and again implored the holy Mother of God for help. And behold, a miracle! The dragons not only did him no harm or violence, but even stroked his frightened body with their heads and tails, so that he took new courage and began to grow accustomed to this dreadful and unheard-of company. Yet the unfortunate man spent not one day or seven in that company, but six full months, from the 6th day of Wintermonat until the 10th of April. During this long period he met the necessities of life in the following miraculous way. He had noticed that throughout the winter the dragons took no food other than a salty fluid that dripped from cracks in the rock walls and that the creatures licked up.
