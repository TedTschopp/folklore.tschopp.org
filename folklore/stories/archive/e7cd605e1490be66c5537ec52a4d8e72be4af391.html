It runs as far as the woods, then turns from the highway back into the village and disappears beside the church at the churchyard. Its footprints have often been seen traced through the snow. Its most recent appearance was endured by two brothers. They had to go late at night from their house by the woods to Kölliken to fetch something for their sick mother. The so-called Nachtbuben of that village were considered very quarrelsome. To arm themselves against such attacks, the brothers pulled a pair of stout clubs from a heap of Reiswellen lying beside their path in the meadow. But this shapeless mass of wood began to take form. It stirred and moved beneath their hands and threatened to collapse upon them. At the same time a muffled snorting began; two eyes formed, round as polished pewter plates, and shone toward them out of the darkness. It was the Dorftier. Now they had to flee. Over ditches and fences the two raced back to their rather distant home, but the house door was already locked. Their pursuer gave them no time, so they had to take refuge atop the Holzbeige stacked beside the window up to the eaves. The Dorftier kept them besieged there throughout the long night, and whenever they dared look up again, they met its dreadful fiery eyes staring back at them.
