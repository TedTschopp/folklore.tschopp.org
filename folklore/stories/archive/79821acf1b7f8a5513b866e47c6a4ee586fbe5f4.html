He regarded the little creature with a compassionate look and said, “Despite my prayer, the child was born in an unlucky hour; when it is older, it will hang itself.” The parents were frightened and hurried to a clergyman to ask his advice. He instructed them: “Teach the child to say, of everything it does, ‘In God’s name.’” The parents followed the good advice and taught the child accordingly by word and example. As soon as it was 7 years old, it went into the kitchen and tried to hang itself from the Hähli—that is, the kettle chain. A cat above on the Turner tried to help it take hold of the chain and throw it across. But when the child said, “Then take it, in God’s name!” the cat could do no more. The child tried for a long time until at last it grew tired of the attempt.
