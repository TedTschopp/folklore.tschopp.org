Stoop" ], "english_paragraphs": [ "In the forest below Casella, on the Grossberg at Flums, an unfathomably deep passage begins among the red rocks and runs westward toward the Tobel. Anyone who enters at the middle of the holy night can attain great riches. The farmer from the nearby Girenloch estate dared to do so. After ascending and descending several stairways, he entered a high, mysteriously illuminated chamber. A chest stood there with a fiery dog sitting upon it. A little man approached the farmer and said that his desire for wealth would be fulfilled if he followed every command precisely. First, he was to take hold of the fiery dog and set it upon the ground. The farmer did so. The little man then opened the large chest, which contained many little caskets full of gold. The farmer could take one casket and carry it into his house before one o'clock. If he was not then too tired, the little man would allow him to return once more and fetch a second casket under the same condition. Joyfully, the farmer carried the gold-filled casket toward the exit and set it down there. He believed he could win the second casket more securely if he fetched it at once. He thought, ‘My house is near from there, and the way goes downhill, so I can carry both caskets away together.’ He did indeed receive the second casket and reached the exit with it. But he was now so deathly tired that he had to sit down and fell asleep. When he awoke again in the bright morning, the two caskets full of gold were no longer there.\nJ. B.
