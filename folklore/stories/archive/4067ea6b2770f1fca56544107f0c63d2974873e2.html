This now happened several times without the miller finding a trace of the good-natured culprit. His curiosity troubled him for a long time. The next Saturday he therefore crept into the attic above the Stampfe and peered down through a crack. Before long, a board in the floor below suddenly lifted. Afterward it swept the Stampfe, oiled the wheels, and shut off the water again. Looking itself over from top to bottom and seeing how ragged it appeared, it lamented its condition most pitifully before vanishing. In gratitude, the miller ordered a little suit, shoes, and a small felt hat and laid everything ready.
