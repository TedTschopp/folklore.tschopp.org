It was once the parish church of the surrounding region, both for the Italians and for the German-speaking Valaisans. The two groups had their own priests but only one shared parish church. The priest of the Italians lived in Trasquiera, while the priest of the Valaisans lived in Ruden, present-day Gondo. To prevent misunderstandings, these priests had the exclusive right and obligation, each for one week in alternation, to exercise parish rights in the common church, administer the sacraments, and conduct the service.", "It happened that one Sunday, when it was the Italian priest’s turn, he did not arrive for the morning service. Filled with impatience, the people waited until noon, and when the priest still failed to arrive, men went to Gondo to summon the German priest to the service. The latter followed reluctantly and only under compulsion, because he knew the violent and impetuous character of his fellow priest. He entered the church, vested, and went up to the altar to offer the Holy Sacrifice. — Then the Italian priest appeared at the church door. He had gone hunting on his way and had therefore been delayed, and he saw with anger that the service had begun. Filled with rage, he took his hunting gun from his shoulder, aimed, and shot his rival dead at the altar. — One can imagine the people’s terror and dismay!", "As a result of this treacherous murder, the parish was divided.
