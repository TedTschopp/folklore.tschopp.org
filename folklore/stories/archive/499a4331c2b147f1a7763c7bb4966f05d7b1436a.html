Gamma-Zgraggen, 40 J. alt." ], "english_paragraphs": [ "At the end of the last century, an innkeeper named Tschümperli lived in Steg. He once bought a sow from a pig dealer from Graubünden, but it died the very next night, and he said the Graubünden man should pay for that on the Höllenplatte. The latter traveled over the Furka and spent the night in an Alpine hut, which burned during the night, killing him, his bride, and several other people. The following night, something came and knocked on Tschümperli’s chamber door. He had heard about the disaster and immediately thought it was the spirit of the Graubünden man, so he called, ‘The debt is forgiven you!’ Several years later, something knocked one night on the door of a woman who lived near the innkeeper and called, ‘Go tell Tschümperli that he should repent and prepare himself; his end is drawing near.’ The woman answered resolutely, ‘Go tell him yourself!’ But it replied that it had knocked three times at his door without success and was not permitted to do so more than three times. Three more times, it called to the woman, ‘You will be sure to tell him, won’t you?’ The woman carried out its commission, and Tschümperli put his affairs in order and died a Christian death. People believe that the warning spirit was the Graubünden man whose debt he had forgiven.—That woman was my godmother and told the whole thing herself; I heard it with my own ears.", "Fr.
