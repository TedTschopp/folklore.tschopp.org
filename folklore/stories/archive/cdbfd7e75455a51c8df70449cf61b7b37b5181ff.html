Thus, Baron Roland once saw a lovely maiden at the fountain in Wilderswil, a little village belonging to his domain. Her innocent blue eye and the splendor of her blond braids captivated him. After discovering where the child lived, he rode there, knocked at the door with the pommel of his sword, and bluntly demanded of the startled mother who rushed out that she give him her daughter as a maidservant up at the castle. At the castle, however, things were already in full revelry, for the lord had arranged a feast for his friends.
