Those who watched the irrigation water there, or who themselves watered the surrounding land at night, often saw him walking back and forth across the water conduit like an old-fashioned man. Once, an owner was irrigating there at night. Just as he was about to set the little Teilholzji in place, he felt as though someone behind him gave him a light shove. Thinking it was a person he knew well, he called, ‘Who is there?’ When he looked behind him, his deceased brother-in-law stood bodily before him. Because he had now addressed the dead man, the dead man also had the right to address him. Everything the deceased told him never became known; he related only a few matters from their conversation. Among other things, he said that an irrigation channel had been made unjustly and must be redirected into its rightful course, together with much else.
