When the Knappen were laboring in unproductive rock and the opening of new, richer veins of ore was imminent, they would be sitting unsuspectingly at their meal in the Knappenhaus when loud footsteps rang out from the mine, over the stones, and as far as the wooden stairs, as though thirty or more workers in heavy, iron-shod shoes were approaching.
