One evening, when he was preparing to rest and had undressed to his shirt and one stocking, he suddenly heard a murmur like praying draw closer and closer to his house. He hurried to the window and saw the Nachtvolk walking prayerfully along the road below. The last in the procession, also dressed only in a shirt and one stocking, resembled our Barthli Lutz to the last hair. He understood the warning and said to himself, “Well, Barthli, this concerns you; you can prepare for the journey!” A few days later, he was indeed laid to rest in the Sargans cemetery.\nD.
