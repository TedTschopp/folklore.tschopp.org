As he stepped through the front door, someone entered the other side of the hallway at that very instant: a man black as charcoal. The farmhand did not let himself be disturbed. For supper, he prepared himself a substantial Fänz, set it on the table, and ate with relish. The Black One sat silently on the other side of the table and watched in equal silence.
