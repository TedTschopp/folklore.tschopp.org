It breathes and blows upon those who fear it, after which their heads swell or their eyes become inflamed; yet it cannot harm those who utter curses and threats. It enters people’s vegetable gardens, tramples vegetables and flowers, and tears apart the fences. In rainy weather it even follows them as far as the eaves, but there it vanishes.
