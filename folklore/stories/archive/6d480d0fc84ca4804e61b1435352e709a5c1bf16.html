It is said that this fire arose in the following way, according to what the citizens of the place reported to the authorities in Freiburg. In the upper part of a house, it sounded as though someone with a gentle, whispering voice were calling to another person and signaling him to be silent. The householder thought a thief had hidden there, went upstairs, but found no one. He then heard it again from a higher chamber, went there too, and expected to seize the thief. But when no one was present, he finally heard the voice in the chimney. He then thought it must be a devilish specter and encouraged his frightened family to be confident and unafraid, for God would protect them. Afterward, he asked two priests to come so that they might adjure the spirit. When they asked who he was, he answered, “The devil.” When they further asked what he intended, he answered, “I will utterly destroy the town!” They threatened him, but the devil said, “Your threats mean nothing to me. One of you is a dissolute rogue, and both of you are thieves.” Soon afterward, he carried into the air a woman with whom that clergyman had lived for fourteen years, set her atop the chimney, gave her a cauldron, and ordered her to turn it over and pour it out.
