They wished to keep their arrival concealed and therefore did not take the customary roads through Savièse into the Sanetsch Valley—perhaps those were cut off by the enemy—but climbed behind Mount Prabé across the mountains and, after long detours, descended into the Sanetsch Valley. Battle soon flared with fury and was decided in favor of the municipality of Savièse.
