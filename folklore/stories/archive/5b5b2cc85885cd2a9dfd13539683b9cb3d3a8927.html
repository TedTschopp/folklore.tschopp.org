But was D’heilig Eich at Gondiswil also an object of pagan ritual activity? The narrator, a kind old man who has read much and possesses uncommon knowledge, believes so. It is also possible, however, that an image of a saint stood beneath the tree and gave the oak its name. Yet it is by no means impossible that the name reaches still farther back. There were oaks that were objects of ritual veneration, as shown by the example of Donar’s oak near Geismar, which Saint Boniface felled in the year 725. In many places the Church took a special path to reconcile the pagans with Christianity: it had chapels built near sacred oaks, and beneath the tree dedicated to the pagan god Christian priests had images of saints installed.
