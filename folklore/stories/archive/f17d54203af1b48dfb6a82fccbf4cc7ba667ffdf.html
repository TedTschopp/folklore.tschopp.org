As on the previous evening, it uttered very strange sounds, at times comparable to bright laughter, and once again danced most wondrously about on the hard snow without paying the slightest attention to the carrion. The hunter took up his gun and aimed several times, but the fox would never stand still and simply went on dancing until, weary and dizzy from the dance, it finally had to rest for a moment. The hunter made good use of that moment, aimed again, and fired. But the powder flew out through the priming pan and into his face, while the fox turned toward him and laughed at him.
