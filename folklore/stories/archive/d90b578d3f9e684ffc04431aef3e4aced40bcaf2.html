The following legend is still told about this feat of war:", "A Duke of Savoy lay with his army on the Planta and besieged the city of Sion. The city bravely repelled the attacks and called upon the Upper Valaisans for help; before they arrived, however, it suffered great distress and was in danger at every moment of being overrun by the enemy. When the eagerly awaited warriors from Upper Valais finally reached the Leukerporte, they were not given time to enter the city but were quickly sent across to the Savièseporte to fall upon the enemy's flank as swiftly as possible. At the same time, the men from Savièse and the surrounding area arrived and went into battle with them. It was precisely noon, and the bell rang for prayer. Everyone fell to their knees and loudly prayed the englischen Gruss. — Behold! On the ring wall appeared a wondrously beautiful, shining woman who graciously looked down upon the praying company and then turned solemnly toward the enemy. The Savoyards also saw the apparition and therefore said very meekly: ‘Oh, woe!
