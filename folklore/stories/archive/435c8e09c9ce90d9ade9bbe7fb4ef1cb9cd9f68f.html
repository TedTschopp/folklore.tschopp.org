When she wished to bake, she traveled down the Reuss at night in a breadbasket or kneading trough to fetch onions in Mellingen and scatter them upon the Brodwähen that she knew how to make splendidly from the Scharrete, the dough remnants in the trough. Although she might be seen weeding onions in Mellingen, at the same time she could also be heard in her house in Bremgarten scolding, rasping, and chattering. She never traveled upon a beaten road, but always cross-country over meadows and forests. Once a hunter in her neighborhood shot a hare, but when he reached the spot, he found only an old shoe in its place. He became suspicious and immediately entered her house next door. There she lay in bed and told him with a thousand circumstantial details how she had just dislocated her leg. On another occasion she informed her honorary chaplain that so many hares were sitting in her cabbage garden that he should come shoot himself a roast. He came with his double-barreled gun, fired once and then again, and hit not one animal in the entire group.
