But every abduction carried out in this way was said to have been followed by a devastating flood. In the year 1503, a company of distinguished young gentlemen from Lucerne went hunting on the Fracmont. In the forest above Malters, one of them, who happened to be alone with an ax, saw what he believed to be a rotten tree stump. But the unbearable taste and vapor he perceived soon convinced him that a dragon lay sleeping there. The frightened man soon recovered his courage and struck the monster on the head with his ax, so that it suddenly shot up, soared into the air, and flew away.
