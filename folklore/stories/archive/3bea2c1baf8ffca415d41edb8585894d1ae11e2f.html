This must be done by daylight, however, or the ferryman who lives across the river in the village will certainly not row over. Often, as night was falling, he heard the cry ‘Ferry me across!’ from this high bank and distinctly saw someone descending over the steep edge. But when he arrived, no one could be seen, and only mocking laughter rang down to him from a fir tree. A haunting dwells throughout the area of the Kaisten Bridge. Anyone passing here during the Ember nights feels something sitting so heavily on the back of the neck that it seems intent on riding him. At certain places, it jumps off again and jeers after him from the Hardwald. Or a pedestrian thinks it is walking beside him, keeping pace.
