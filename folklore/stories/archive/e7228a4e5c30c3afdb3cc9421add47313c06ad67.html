The next day the hypocritical woman hurried from house to house among the neighbors, lamenting and wringing her hands as she asked after the girls. The compassion of others did everything possible to find the missing children, but in that weather it was in vain. Meanwhile, in the foreboding breast of many a neighbor, a suspicion of dreadful crime arose against the woman—a suspicion that, sadly, was only too well founded.
