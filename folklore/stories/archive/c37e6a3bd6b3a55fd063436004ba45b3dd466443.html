One evening two young men from Bürglen intended to go z'Stubäda gah—to pay an evening courting visit—together. Near the meadow called ‘Graben’ behind Trudelingen, however, something led both of them astray, out into a dreadful Tobel where they did not know what counsel to take or how to help themselves. In their distress they vowed to remain unmarried all their lives. At once the black clouds in the night sky parted, and the moon illuminated the country, which they now recognized, and the path by which they found the way out. One of the two kept the vow, but Bifang-Hansi broke it.", "At that time the people on the Holzerberg had a remarkably clever dog. It foretold everything that happened far and wide, announcing many a death and many a misfortune in advance. It often happened that this strange animal ran out of the house, all the way up the Holzerberg to the G'hirmi on the ‘Höchi,’ where it barked and carried on as though it were biting a person in the legs. This happened especially in the evening by moonlight and was repeated frequently until the day on which Hansi married his Tryni. On the evening of their wedding day, the newly married couple came up through the Holzerberg to make their home above in Bifang. But both were utterly g'mäntelet—downcast—and up there by the G'hirmi they sat down. The dog now ran up, barking and raging, and would have bitten Hansi in the legs if its owners had not called it back. With a sorrowful heart Hansi revealed to his young wife the secret that weighed upon him. ‘Tryni,’ he said, ‘now you are a poor, unfortunate creature; we shall have no happiness.’ Tryni tried to comfort him.
