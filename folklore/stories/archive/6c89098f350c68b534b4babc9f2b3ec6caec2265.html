Many attempts have already been made to raise it, but magic has attached such conditions to the undertaking that no mortal has yet succeeded in fulfilling them. Once, after midnight, a man went to this tree and dug. Soon a beautiful woman appeared and gave him hope that he would attain the object of his desires, but he would have to kiss her three times. That day she granted him the first kiss. When he returned the following night, a great hideous toad sat beneath the tree, for the beautiful woman had transformed into it. He could not bring himself to kiss it and fled.
