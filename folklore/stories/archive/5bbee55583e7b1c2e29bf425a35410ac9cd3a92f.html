It is not rarely swept by an uncanny, shrill whistling, especially toward the time when people begin to speak of the Vonalpfahrt, but only at night. This always brings bad weather. One evening, when the Vonalpfahrt was to take place soon, the whistling was heard again and especially sharply. Soon they were climbing toward the Alp. When they reached the Zerfinen-platte on Schuhegg, the ringing and lowing could already be heard from Rossmen and Gafortsch in an ear-splitting concert. On Forkels, cattle and owners met, and then they went toward the village in pouring, cold rain. The next day the Senn and farmhands also arrived with the cow chains and other equipment.
