The old headman, however, so the story goes, was not entirely convinced or certain of his case and therefore did not dare affirm his municipality’s claim with an oath. But he knew how to help himself cunningly. He took soil from his garden and put it in his shoes, then concealed a soup ladle — a Schöpfer — in his hat.
