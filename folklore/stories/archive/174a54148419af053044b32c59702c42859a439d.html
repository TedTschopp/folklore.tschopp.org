According to folk tradition, these Jütz were people rich in treasure. They possessed the Läntergen in Morschach, the private Alp properties of Buggi on the Rophaien and Urwängi in Seelisberg, the Riedberg, many cattle, servants of both sexes, and a large dairy. The servants were treated very well, but they always grew tired of the endless veal and colostrum. From this one can infer the size of the family's herd. At the end there were two sons, who knew nothing of work until they were 20 years old.
