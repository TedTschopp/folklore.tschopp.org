Every year the herdsman who spent the summer above brought the leaseholder her rent and a basket full of fresh Ziger and rich Anken, but the insatiable woman was never satisfied. Then a hailstorm broke over the Rätzliberg, and hunger threatened the herd. Lamenting and begging for pity, the Küher brought the leaseholder even less Anken. But the hard-hearted woman cursed the herdsman and laid a curse upon the Alp. Then, all at once, the ice advanced from the wild Strudel, drawing ever closer to the Rätzliberg.
