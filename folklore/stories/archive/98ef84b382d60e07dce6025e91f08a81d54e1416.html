It is also said to be connected with the church tower of Stans by an underground passage. The legend of the treasure runs as follows:", "On a Good Friday, a young maid born on a September day was keeping watch beside an old, sick maiden. It was the very time when the Passion was being read in the church. The girl wished to go into the cellar to fetch something; this cellar is so dark that a light is needed even in broad daylight. She therefore lit her lamp and entered. There she saw a wide circle of burning candles, and within the circle everything shone as brightly as the sun, so that the girl, dazzled and frightened, hurried away. She told the sick woman, who said that she should immediately run back and throw her rosary into the circle; the spell binding the treasure that bloomed on Good Friday would then be broken. The girl hurried back, but found nothing except the dark cellar.
