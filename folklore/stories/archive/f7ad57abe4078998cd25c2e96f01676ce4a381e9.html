But the father and one son happened not to be at home. He explained to the mother that he intended to test himself against her sons. The mother evaded him and said the father and one son were in the forest, while the younger son was in the cellar and ‘tiäg grad abnyddlä’—‘was just skimming the cream.’ The giant demanded that she call him. She did not wish to do so. Meanwhile, however, the young man came out of the cellar on his own. On each open palm he carried a Mutte filled completely with milk and set both upon the table without changing expression. The man from Schwyz watched wide-eyed. Then he offered the Schächentaler his right hand. The Schächentaler looked him firmly in the eyes, seized the hand, and squeezed it so powerfully that blood flowed from beneath every fingernail.
