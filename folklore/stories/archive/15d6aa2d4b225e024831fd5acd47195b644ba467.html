For a long time the man did not notice, because whenever she went out she put a broom in the bed beside him. At last, however, he did notice. He kept watch, but saw her only go out into the kitchen, and then he neither heard nor saw anything more of her. In the morning, when he awoke, she was back beside him in bed. The next time he crept after her and looked through the keyhole into the kitchen. There the woman put on fine clothes and primped and adorned herself like an unmarried, haughty girl.
