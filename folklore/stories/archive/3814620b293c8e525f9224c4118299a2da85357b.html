The people regarded him as a saint.", "In Gurtnellen it is told that Fridli Bücher, whose home was not in Uri but “somewhere out there,” spread the report that during his own legal proceedings he had seen flames of fire burst from the judges' mouths. Once, while he was busy making cheese on his Alp, two cantonal constables came to arrest him. Fridli gave them hospitality. In one hand he brought a Mutte full of Süffi, in the other a Mutte full of milk with the heavy Schweignapf still attached, and he held out both vessels for them to drink without setting them down. The constables stared in amazement and did not dare lay hands on him. Fridli, however, went with them voluntarily. Along the way he tore out a birch as thick as a man's thigh and wound it around his body like a belt. Before the court he was supposed to retract his statement, but he said that a man who was not permitted to stand by the truth was a poor sort of man. He was therefore sentenced to death at the executioner's hand. When they hanged him, the rope broke for the second time, and Fridli said that the hemp must be rotten.
