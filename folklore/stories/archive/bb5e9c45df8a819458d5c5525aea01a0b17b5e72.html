Then the spectral Stiefelreiter rides through mountain and valley upon a snow-white gray horse with its head turned backward, and often he is heard rushing past in two places at once. Once, many, many years ago, the rich Muri Abbey appointed a very wicked Vogt as its guardian. To the abbot, he seemed the most capable man in the world, for year after year he increased the monastery's holdings and prosperity. But the good abbot was struck with blindness, for he did not know what an entirely different face the Vogt showed to the people around the monastery. Toward them, he was in every respect the most malicious person imaginable. No prayers and no complaints could soften his hard heart, and he laughed at the tears of widows and orphans. He wore tremendous Stulpstiefel reaching far above his knees. When the people saw the wicked monastic Vogt approaching upon his white horse in these high boots, they hid fearfully behind houses and barns. The children cried in terror, ‘The Stiefelreiter is coming, the Stiefelreiter is coming!’ and ran away.
