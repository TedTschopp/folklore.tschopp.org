Then they descend from their summer palaces—the inaccessible heights of the Finsteraarhorn, the Jungfrau, and their neighbors—into the lower regions. They gather in the rocky chasms and Tobeln, sport with the fury of the elements, and howl cruel magic songs to which the storm whistles an accompaniment. Often they take their places upon the icy ridges and tauntingly challenge one another, tease each other with ridiculous retorts, and hurl loads of snow—enormous avalanches—at one another. Because they hate everything that shows life, they also hunt readily and often. With loud halloos, they then hunt the beautiful chamois, whistling, clattering, and rattling in pursuit. Their greatest hatred, however, falls upon human beings. Woe, therefore, to the traveler who ventures into their domain at such a time, to the hunter who roams their territory, or to the Strahler who seeks to rob their dwellings—the mountain's interior—of their most beautiful adornment, the Strahlen or crystals!
