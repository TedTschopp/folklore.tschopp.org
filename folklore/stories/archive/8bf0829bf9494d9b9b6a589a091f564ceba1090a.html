A nursemaid served there alongside me. Everyone in the house knew that whenever a person from the household was going to die, a beautiful white woman appeared beforehand. Only the nursemaid knew nothing about it yet. One evening, as the Berger family and I sat in the living room, she watched beside the little bed of a sick child in the adjoining room. Suddenly she came running into the living room, crying out in fear, and shouted, “Come, come!” Everyone quickly ran into the next room, thinking that the little one was dying.
