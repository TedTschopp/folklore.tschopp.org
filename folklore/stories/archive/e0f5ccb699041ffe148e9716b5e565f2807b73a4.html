Equal servings were poured for every community member, and bread and cheese were distributed according to a fixed weight. In the end, one Doppelkanne of wine and two black loaves of rye bread remained. Baschi thought that this was too little to divide among everyone and too much for one person—and to whom could he have given it without the others complaining?
