A girl suffered greatly at the hands of her wicked stepmother and on one occasion was even beaten raw. The child ran away and, in her flight, reached a spring that bubbled powerfully and clearly from the ground. She bent down, drank, and washed her wound. Suddenly, the child felt herself healed. She was heartily glad of this but immediately thought and resolved that she would expressly tell her stepmother nothing about this healing spring. Why did the woman treat her so horribly? She could keep her affliction. The maliciously pleased girl truly said not a syllable about her discovery. When she later received another wound on her body, she went to the place to heal herself by washing, but now she could no longer find the healing spring. Some time passed. Then a hunter was hunting in the same region. He had just wounded a beautiful animal. With its last strength, it ran to a spring, bathed, and was healed. The hunter saw this, and thus the miraculous spring lost through the envious girl was found again.
