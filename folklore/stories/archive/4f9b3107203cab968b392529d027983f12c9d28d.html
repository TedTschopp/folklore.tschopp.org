When the cowherd woman indicated that he should climb with her up a tall ladder into the hay-filled barn at the gable, he firmly refused to go first, however earnestly his hostess demanded it. She had to yield and climb the ladder ahead of him. Halfway up, the following young man saw in horror that the comely woman had rooster’s feet.
