Once, when a terrible earthquake raged and the lake broke far beyond its banks, its level subsequently fell and the city decayed. Its walls sank into the ground, as people say, because humankind had become so godless that the earth could no longer bear them. Nothing of the masonry can be seen today. Instead, a little white woman appears from time to time where it once stood. Old people claim that she was once the city's lady and must now, as punishment for her offenses, guard a great treasure inside the mountain.
