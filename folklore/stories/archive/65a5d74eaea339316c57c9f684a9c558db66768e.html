The following legend is told of this place: on dark and stormy nights, a headless rider is often seen riding a white horse down from the castle, emerging from the forest and racing across the open ground at top speed before suddenly vanishing. Not far away is the Engelreih, and at its foot a hollow road leads to a stream and the footbridge of ‘Frau Escher.’ It is told that a white lady called Frau Escher is often seen at this bridge; if someone wishes to cross at night with evil intent, she comes to meet him from the bridge and turns him back with a threatening hand. Not very far away is a field called Steinmürli because Roman masonry has been found there. At its edge stands an old tree beneath which, according to legend, a treasure rests. Once at midnight a man went there to dig it up. Soon a beautiful woman appeared, encouraged him in his work, and promised that his wish would succeed, but he must come three times and give her a kiss each time. That night he cheerfully gave her the first. When he returned the following night, a large, loathsome toad sat beneath the tree. He was frightened, would not kiss it, and fled. The next night he found an even more loathsome toad at the spot, and when he again could not resolve to kiss it, he fell into madness and was never cured.", "A further legend tells this: another time a man went to this tree and dug.
