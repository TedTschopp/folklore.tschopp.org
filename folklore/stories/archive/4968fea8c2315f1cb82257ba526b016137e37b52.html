To his astonishment, a Jauchzer answered from his cottage at Balmen. He walked on and gave another joyful Jauchzer. The same echo came from the cottage! When he arrived, he found everything ready and prepared for milking. From then on, an invisible hand helped him with all his work throughout the summer. When he vacated the Alpine pasture in September, he heard ‘it’ wailing and crying piteously. This continued for several summers. At last, he consulted his wife and asked, ‘If it cries like that again, should I call to it that it may come with me?’ But she thought he should not. ‘With our parlor already full of children, there would be no room left for that Umghyr.’ The following autumn, it again wept and wailed so pitifully that he felt sorry for it and could not close his heart.
