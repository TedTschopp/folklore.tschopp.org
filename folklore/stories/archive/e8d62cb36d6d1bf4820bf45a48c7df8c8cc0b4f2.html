The village pig has violently sprung between the legs of many a man and pursued him as far as his distant home or a barn; it would surely have followed farther if it were permitted to enter any dwelling of people or livestock. On the other hand, it has sometimes happened that the village pig looked through a parlor window—or, while standing on a ladder, through a barn door—to spy out a bachelor who had fled there. In the Stepfenwiesen, one man was even harassed by the village pig while it had a company of young piglets with it. But he ordered the Kobolde to depart in the Devil’s name, whereupon they quickly withdrew with grunts and squeals and disappeared into the nearby forest.\nJ.
