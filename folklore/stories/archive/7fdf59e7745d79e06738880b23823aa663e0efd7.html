To prevent it, he went to bed in good health. Toward evening, a little mouse climbed up over the bed frame to him. He saw it and tried to fend it off with his foot, but so vigorously that his leg broke. Had he not believed, the mouse would not have caused his broken leg. — A countryman was prophesied that his house would go up in flames that night. Believing this, he allowed no light to be made anywhere in the house all day or during the coming night. After nightfall, a neighbor wished to conduct some business with him but could find neither stairs nor doors in the darkness. He therefore carelessly made a light and set the house ablaze, which he would not have done if the ordinary light had been lit. — Another man dreamed that he saw a body being raised from the water and recognized with horror his own person in it. He therefore feared dying in water and never ventured onto it again. One day, he was invited on a pleasure trip to the opposite shore. Because he would not go on the water, he walked around and, while waiting on the bank for those arriving, stood upon an undercut rock that collapsed with him and killed him. Had he remained with his companions, he would not have fallen into the water with the rock. — It is related of Aeschylus that he was prophesied to be killed by a body falling upon him from above.
