Every one of the Alpknechte faithfully obeyed the command. — But once a Senn conceived the idea of departing from his predecessors’ pious custom because he wished to defy this superstition: on the first evening after the Alpfahrt he omitted the formula. His companions joined him; only the “Bazger” did not, and he recited his formula. — During the night the invisible Alp spirits came, threw everything into confusion, and slew all the Alpknechte in the most horrible manner. The “Bazger” was spared, but the Senn was given the choice of being boiled in Schmalz in the morning or of “büchlen” himself to death (bursting his lungs with the alphorn). He chose the latter, probably in the hope that he would be heard and rescued. He took his alphorn onto a rocky eminence and blew with all his strength. For a long time no one heard his call, until at last his beloved did; the dreadful blasts following rapidly upon one another filled her with foreboding. She recognized the danger and sent help up to the Alp.
