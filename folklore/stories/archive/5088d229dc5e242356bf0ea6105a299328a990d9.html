But as spring approaches, dreadful booming and cracking can be heard in the ice. This comes from the dragon Vuivra, which then tries to break through the thick ice. Suddenly it bursts out and shoots with fiery wings from one lake to another. Its food consists of gold dust said to cover the lakes’ beds. In Vouvry, however, there is another dragon-like monster said to resemble a flying viper more closely. It has a fiery tail, and in its forehead is a great diamond that serves as an eye and can be seen shining from far away when it flies. The flying viper often bathes in streams and ponds, but every time before slipping into the water it sets its diamond eye down upon the bank. Many people have tried to seize the diamond, for it is said to be of immense value, but no one ever succeeded. Once the serpent raised its head as if listening, but then it dived deep beneath the water. At that instant, the man swiftly extended his hand from the bush, seized the diamond, and tried to withdraw very, very quietly.
