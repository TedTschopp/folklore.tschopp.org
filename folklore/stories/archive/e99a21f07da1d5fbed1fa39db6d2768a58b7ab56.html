There the Vispe truly first wells up with a quiet whisper before later emerging below the Alpgletscher: it is the paradise of the animals. Magnificent ibexes and chamois, eagles and vultures, ptarmigans and black grouse, and marmots live there, and none harms another; all dwell peacefully together. Only once every three times seven years may and can a human eye look into this paradise of Alpine animals, where everything is so blissful and beautiful, full of Alpine roses and gentians; of twenty chamois hunters, scarcely a single one succeeds. Ancient pines and maples stand there, and the pines bear cones whose kernels taste sweet like almonds: these are Zirbelnüsse. Whoever succeeds in entering the paradise of animals may take and taste the Zirbelnüsse, but must never catch or kill an animal, or it will cost him his life. Many have cut their names into the ancient sacred Plantanenstämme as proof that they were once there. Elsewhere, an ibex is now rarely seen, and a pine rarely too, and those stand high and are difficult to reach.
