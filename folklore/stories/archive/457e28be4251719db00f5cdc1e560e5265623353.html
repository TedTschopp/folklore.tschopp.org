In the year 1565, this grievous sickness and manifold cruel dying also reached the Libingen mountains and Halden. Of 40-some people there, only two children remained. One other child had been taken away but later died of the plague nonetheless. At Halden, Engelbolgen, Vettigen, auf dem Stein, Libingen, and the surrounding places, this grievous pestilence increased so cruelly that the parish priest and Mesner had to go to the sick with the holy last sacraments for half a day and an entire night without stopping, and several people died during the sacred rite.", "It should now be known that, with the entire municipality of Mosnang's consent and heartfelt solemn vow, the Reverend parish priest Jakob Philipp Stössel promised three Kreuzgänge with outstretched arms, in the truest possible devotion, around the village along all four roads. Henceforth and for all time, every parish priest is obliged to announce them annually from the pulpit and faithfully perform the first upon the Friday before St. Gall's Day. If the weather should be entirely unfavorable on one of the three Fridays, the devotions are to be held inside the church. Every year, the Pfarrkinder are to be earnestly admonished that they too faithfully fulfill the vow and promise, so that the Most Holy Trinity, through the intercession of the Virgin Mother Mary; the holy patrons of church and municipality, George and Theodul; St. Sebastian; and all the beloved saints, may graciously protect, shelter, and preserve the entire municipality and all Christendom, now and at all times, from such sorrowful times and great common misery.
