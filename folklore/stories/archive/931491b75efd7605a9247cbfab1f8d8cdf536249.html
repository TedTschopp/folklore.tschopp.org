He reached the Alp only very late. When he entered the deserted Alphütte, to his great amazement he saw three Sennen around the fire pit, one of them sitting on the very milking stool he was supposed to retrieve. The Käsekessel was suspended over a brightly blazing fire, and they asked whether he would also like to drink “Suffi” (whey from cheese making). He said yes because he thought he must not refuse, though he was filled with dread. They then skimmed it off, and to his further astonishment he saw three kinds of Suffi poured into the vessels: red, white, and black. They asked which kind he wished to drink, and he requested the white. The three Sennen also explained the three colors to him. The red signified milk needlessly used and wasted during the summer; the white meant that they had milked the cows properly and handled the milk properly; and the black meant that they had frequently sworn while on the Alp. At last, they gave him the choice of which of three arts he preferred: to sing very well, to whistle, or to Jauchzen. He asked to be able to Jauchzen very well, and they promised him this gift. The next morning, when he left, he began to Jauchzen and could indeed do it with astonishing beauty, so that when he arrived home, the people marveled at his pleasing yodeling. The Senn who was at home burned with desire to obtain this yodeling too and therefore went to the Alp at once.
