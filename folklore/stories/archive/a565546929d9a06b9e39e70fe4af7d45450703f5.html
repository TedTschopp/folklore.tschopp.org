The noble La-Beaume family came from Savoy, though no one knows how it reached the Alpine country around Charmey. When the family left the Balmis valley, it sold its very considerable estates, which extended as far as Praz de l'Essert, or the Riedmatte, to the wealthy Remy family. Such riches, however, could only have been acquired through magic and with the aid of the Prince of Darkness.
