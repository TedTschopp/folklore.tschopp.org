When they happened to look up through a Teeltschi, they saw a little fox sitting motionless at one spot above them and looking down the little valley. The two hunters entered the little valley and climbed upward. To their astonishment, the animal remained quietly at its post and stared steadily down at them. When they came very near it, the bachelor said to the married man, “You, we are not going to shoot this little creature!” They stepped closer and found that it was tied to a stone with a Niälen. While it looked at the two men and wagged its tail in a friendly way, they untied it and let it run free.
