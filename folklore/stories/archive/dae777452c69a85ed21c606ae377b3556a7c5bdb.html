Natsch.", "***", "On the eastern side of the Seluner Rücken lies a cave called the Wildmannlisloch. Its gateway is so large that one can drive inside with horse and wagon. It leads one and a half hours deep into the mountain, and the passages within are so broad that several men can walk upright side by side. At the front, the Wildmännchen carved tables and benches for themselves. A quarter hour farther inside lies a small lake enclosed all around by blocks of rock; the little dwarfs rolled these there as cool resting seats. Still farther back are the dwellings. The dwarfs lived on roots and milk. They helped the neighboring Sennen ‘schoren’ the stable, feed the livestock, and make hay on the mountain. Once, they fetched the midwife from Starkenbach, who had to assist a Wildweiblein. As payment, she received an entire apron full of clods of earth. On the long way out of the cave, however, she paid no attention to the worthless gift; when she emerged into daylight and examined the last clod, it was pure gold. \nRochholz, Swiss Legends.", "***", "The Wildemannloch leads approximately 170 meters into the mountain; farther progress is impossible because the opening becomes narrow.
