It may have been around 3 o'clock in the morning when they reached the hut at Vorderwaldi, settled down there, and made black coffee, which they duly fortified with schnapps. Their talk turned to the ghost that haunted the place, and Greti-Byni, the strongest of them all, with the black brew gone to his head, boasted, ‘If there is one here, just let it come; I'll soon settle matters with it!’ ‘Just don't spettlä!’ the others warned him. But he tossed another Beckli full down his throat and called loudly, ‘And I say it again: if there is one around, let it come; I will wrestle with it!’ Then the roof shingles began to rumble, and suddenly the shingles flew around Byni's head like snowflakes. His companions said, ‘There you have it now! Settle matters with it yourself!’ and sprang aside. When the dance of the shingles had ended, they returned and found Byni sitting there deathly pale. They poured another swallow of black coffee into him to revive him, but this time without schnapps. It did no good. They had to turn back and carry Byni home.
