The people of Unterwalden still speak of “Lisi Bossi” and the outrage she is said to have committed against the Engelberg house of God. It was on the 29th day of the month of August 1729 when, according to the legend, students there were playing with rockets. Unfortunately, some flew into the building and set it aflame. At the first sign of fire, people rushed to the bells, but alas, the ropes would not pull; half scorched, they fell down, except for the rope of the great bell, which alone still hung firmly and opened the bell’s mouth to call for help. Yet it was not the alarm peal it should have been, and the valley residents recognized the danger too late. Lisi Bossi was to blame. She was nearby and, with her black magic, bewitched the rockets into their disastrous course, kindled the fire, and silenced the bells.
