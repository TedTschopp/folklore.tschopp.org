At last her tricks were discovered. A gentleman from Andermatt was once riding past a certain garden in the city of Milan and saw Schneidergret pulling onions and leeks there. But he was in such a hurry and traveled home so quickly that Gret could not possibly have arrived before him by natural means. Yet she had long since returned to her cottage — or rather, she had never been away for any length of time. When the man realized this, he understandably grew suspicious and, with several confidants to whom he related his experience, began to investigate Gret closely. It then emerged that at noon during Carnival, while butter melted in a pan over the fire, Gret would use the pretext of fetching onions from the garden before her cottage to transport herself suddenly to Milan. She would steal onions from a garden there and return before the butter had reached precisely the right melting temperature. She did this almost every day. It further emerged that she prayed entirely backward; the Our Father, for example, she began at the end and finished at the beginning. She was also found to pray: \"Brigita, Brigita, hedge stakes,\" which she muttered rapidly over and over. At the new church in Andermatt she prayed: \"Little nails, little nails, up and down, may the Devil take it from the grave.\" She repeated this quickly. It was further proven that Schneidergret had already caused much harm. As soon as these deeds were beyond doubt, the people considered putting her in confinement, but she could not be captured; as soon as one of her feet touched the earth, no human being could hold her any longer, and she vanished. At last, a very strong man from Göschenen undertook to seize her.
