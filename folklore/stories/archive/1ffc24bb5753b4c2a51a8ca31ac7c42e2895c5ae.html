The face is painted in vivid colors and varnished so heavily that the skin appears wet with sweat and the large black eyes moist with tears. This is ‘St. Kümmernis,’ about whom stories are often told in the upper districts of German-speaking Valais and whom young women, if possible before marrying, are expected to see out of curiosity—just as wealthy English people supposedly could neither live contentedly nor die peacefully without having looked upon Mont Blanc or the Matterhorn at least once.", "One legend relates that St. Kümmernis was a beautiful king’s daughter whom her royal father wished to betroth to a man who displeased her. She was heard: her mouth, nose, and eyes grew large and became hideously misshapen; coal-black eyebrows and a mighty cropped beard completed the disfigurement of her once so beautiful face. When her father realized what had happened, he angrily had his daughter nailed to a cross.
