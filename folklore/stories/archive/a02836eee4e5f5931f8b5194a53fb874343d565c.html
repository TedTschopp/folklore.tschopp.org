They found what they were seeking. A great number of foxes were dancing about on the Pardenner-Boden. Two of them especially drew the hunters' attention: one violently beat, bit, and scratched the other, then tied it to a tree stump with a rope. Suddenly all the foxes were gone as though blown away; only the bound one remained. The two hunters approached the prisoner, and Xander said to his brother, “If all the others are gone, let us set this one free as well.” They meant to untie the fox, but it began to speak like a human being and said, “Cut the rope very close to the stump!” Xander did so and said, “Then run, in God's name.” In a flash, no fox was left to be seen.
