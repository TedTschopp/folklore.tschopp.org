While thinking about him, as she approached the Rüti that belonged to her beloved, she saw a man standing within the field. His entire body was a bare skeleton, but terrible bright fire blazed through every gap. Only the apparition's head was still whole, and with horror she recognized in it the living facial features of her beloved. She fled and had reached her house before she quite knew it. But her sweetheart was already lying inside on the Ofenbank, calmly awaiting her arrival. ‘Jesus, Mary!’ she cried, ‘are you here?’—‘For a long time already,’ he answered. ‘And yet I just saw you outside on your Rüti!’—‘Then,’ the lad replied, ‘you must have brought fourfold eyes home from the fair, for I have been waiting here bored for you for an hour already.’—‘Oh, sweetheart,’ cried the girl, ‘I will not be dissuaded. I saw it with my healthy eyes. What have you done?’ The lad continued to deny it, and the girl resolved to avoid him from then on. She confessed it to the priest, who told her she could thank God on her knees for revealing such a secret to her in this life. He then summoned the lad and explained to him what it meant to move Marchsteine, and what dreadful punishment must follow a crime that the all-knowing God sought to expose even in this life. Such words shook the young man to his core, and sobbing he confessed how he had taken half his neighbor's Rüti by moving the Marchen. He left the priest in gratitude and put everything back into proper order as he had vowed.
