That village lies even farther from Zurzach, and the dwarfs’ cave there is high above the last Kadelburg vineyards. After climbing down those rocks covered in snow and black ice in winter and making it safely across the water, one still had to cross the woods and fields of the entire long Almende before finally catching sight of the market town’s towers.
