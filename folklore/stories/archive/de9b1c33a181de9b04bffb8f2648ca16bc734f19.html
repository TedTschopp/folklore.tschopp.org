One winter, a young man from Saas was feeding his father’s livestock on the mountain above the village. He found his son at work in the Sennerei and was astonished at the rich store of milk, butter, and cheese. He also noticed the cattle’s fine appearance, and in addition the fodder supply was far greater than he had expected. Had I remained unrecognized, your son and I would have fed the cattle here until spring, when they go out to pasture.
