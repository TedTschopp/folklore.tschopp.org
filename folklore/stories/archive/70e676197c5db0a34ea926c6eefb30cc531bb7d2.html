Arnold u.a.m." ], "english_paragraphs": [ "According to legend, Landesstatthalter Hans Kuon lived at Törelen in the municipality of Spiringen and built the beautiful house that still stands today on the rear Mühlebach above the road. During its construction, he and his servant rode toward Schwanden, a hamlet east of Unterschächen. Along the way, both were surprised by a rockslide at the Rübi. The servant rode onward toward Äsch and saved his life; the master turned his horse and galloped back, straight into death’s open arms. The little prayer house by the Rübi is said to commemorate the accident. Several years ago, a horseshoe is said to have emerged from deep rubble near the site of the accident, a little west of the new government work shed behind Eistersmatt.
