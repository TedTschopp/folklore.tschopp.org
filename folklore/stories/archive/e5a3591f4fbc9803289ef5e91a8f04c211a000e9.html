One must cross it when taking the path to Ruchenberg through the dark forest where the Alaräunchen lives. This Tobel is called Scalära. The Scaläratobel begins far up in the mountains at Montelin, the highest peak of the Hochwang, winds outward through dreadful gorges, and empties into the Rhine valley not far from Chur. In this Tobel, whose very name fills the surrounding inhabitants with dread and horror, one hears a ghastly din. Between bare rock walls as high as the heavens, thunder and crashing sound without cease, and by day one sees nothing but rubble and stone. At night, however, a terrible howl can be heard as far away as Trimmis. When midnight comes, human figures emerge from a thousand clefts and cracks in the rock. These are the deceased mayors, councilors, bailiffs, and other citizens of the city of Chur. They make all sorts of strange gestures. The distinguished ones ride white horses, and once they are all assembled—an immense multitude—the procession begins to move, the riders in front and the most recently deceased mayor at the head of the cavalry. It descends through the Tobel, crosses the highway, and passes through the brush as far as the Rhine. There they water the horses; the procession turns around and silently returns to the gorges. So it happens every night, but not everyone can see the spirits who dwell here forever.
