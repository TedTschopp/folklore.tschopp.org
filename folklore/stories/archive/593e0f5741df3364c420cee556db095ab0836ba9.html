You know the mistress is always scolding us.” Then the Güggel said, “She knows nothing about it. Just come along; she never gives us anything good anyway.” Again the Hüendli said, “No, no, it is out of the question; we are not going up.” But the Güggel gave them no peace until at last they went, got onto the table, and gathered the bread crumbs with great zeal. Just then the mistress came in, quickly took a stick, swept them down, and treated them very harshly. When they were outside the house below, the Hüendli said to the Güggel, “See, see, see, see, see, see—do you see now?” The Güggel laughed and merely said, “Ha, ha, had I not known it?” And off they went.
