She soon had an opportunity to prove her purity. Once, when she wished to carry wood from the castle attic to the kitchen, she felt herself quite tangibly held back above by an arm. Frightened, she hurried down the long stairway and tried to persuade herself she had not seen it. But it happened again. She now declared to her employers her firm resolve never again to enter that uncanny place. The Landvogt reassured her and represented it as a work demanded by piety if, the next time, she stood her ground before the apparition and questioned it. In mortal fear the girl returned to the dangerous place. The arm did not fail to appear. When she now asked her question, an upper body also took shape and a voice said, “Pray for me, and then I shall be redeemed!” She wished to promise, but the apparition had already vanished again. Lisette soon believed that through her long, fervent prayer she had redeemed a sinner, when a figure dressed in green in an old-fashioned cut stepped before her. He asked her to pull the fiery splinter at the front from between his teeth and throw it down the chimney. Without really looking closely, she did so. The figure disappeared with profuse thanks and a promise not to return.", "The maid did with the splinter as she had been commanded. It flew noisily crackling down the chimney.
