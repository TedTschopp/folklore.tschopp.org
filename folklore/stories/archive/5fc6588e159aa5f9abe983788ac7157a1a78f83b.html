He had a large iron cage made and carried into the middle of the forest. He also demanded a syringe, petroleum, and firewood. He shut himself inside the cage and waited until the bears came. Whenever one appeared, he sprayed it with petroleum and set it alight, whereupon the beast ran away and burned to death. When all of them were dead, he left the cage, had the dead animals carried to the king, and demanded his daughter as his wife. ‘I made the work too easy for you,’ said the king. ‘You must perform one more little trial for me. There are many murderers in the neighboring forest. If you go and kill them, you shall most certainly have my daughter!’ The tailor pulled a sour face but declared himself willing. He put a little piece of Ziger in his pocket and entered the forest. The twelve murderers appeared and laughed at the scrawny little man. The tailor reached into his pocket and said: ‘Look here.
