From the outside, it is very beautiful to behold; within, however, it is full of corners and angles, though only in the side wings. The house is said to be uncanny, and especially the maids employed there know many stories about the haunting supposed to occur within it. An old woman who lives nearby and often visited the house because she knew it well claims, however, that matters were worse in former times. The spirits have now lost their power, and only on the stairs does something still appear from time to time. That is of little importance: at most, it might brush icy cold across someone’s face like a corpse’s hand or blow out the candle while a person lights his way on the stairs, whereas whole swarms of ghosts formerly raged through the house. The haunting was wildest in the chimneys and kitchen flues. Sometimes it behaved very gently and whispered a soft ‘Pst! Pst!’ inside them, so that maids still working in the kitchen at night often thought it was a signal from a sweetheart in the street. But it would then always break into such horrifying laughter that terror shot through every limb of the girls, who certainly resigned their positions the following day. According to the woman mentioned above, this continued until approximately the nineties. A Swabian cook then served in the house and wrote about the matter to a Catholic priest whose household she had formerly managed. He answered that the haunting in the flue came from none other than the devil himself. He knew only one means of driving it out, but that remedy was certain to work. The Swabian cook applied it. The old woman thought she must have made some mistake, however, or the ghosts would have departed completely. A door through which the spirits commonly entered in former times is still shown in the house today." ], "notes": [] }, "legacy_urls": [] } ---
