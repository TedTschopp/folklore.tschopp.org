Several men from the Amsteg area, whose names are still given, resolved to dig together for metals or Strahlen—in short, for valuables—in the ruined mine gallery by the ‘Höhe’ in the Ried at the foot of the Bristenstock. First, however, they went to Zurich and received instruction from an experienced man for several weeks. At last he told them that throughout the undertaking they must be sincere and honest with one another, otherwise their work would be in vain. They now set to work. Fortune seemed ready to favor them: they found the right trace and were well on their way to extracting a great treasure. But when they went to begin work with great joy one morning, every trace had vanished. All their searching and labor were now useless. They sent one of their number back to their teacher in Zurich to ask what was responsible and what they should do. He answered that they themselves were to blame: they had not been honest with one another, everything was now ruined, and they should simply give up the undertaking. The messenger brought them this news. They questioned one another, and it now emerged that one man had appropriated a small tool and another had used the drill to blast stone on his own estate.
