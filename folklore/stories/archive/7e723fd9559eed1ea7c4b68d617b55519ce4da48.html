The inhabitants of Gsteig implored him to free them from their dangerous, uncanny neighbors. After long hesitation, the foreign Capuchin consented to the daring undertaking and went to the place from which he was to banish the spirits forever. From atop a limestone rock, he began to hurl his Bannformeln, holy words, and holy signs against the spirits. But these were not powerful enough; the spirits stormed wildly against the rock on which the conjurer stood and tried with all their strength to tear it away beneath his feet. The pious father could scarcely withstand this struggle, but his foot pressed down ever more firmly, boring deep into the rock, which had already begun to sway beneath him—then one more formula of conjuration, the most powerful of all, and behold, the spirits withdrew forever.
