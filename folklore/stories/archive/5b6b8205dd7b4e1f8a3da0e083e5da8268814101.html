This tower, however, was also frequently visited by a certain household spirit that so terrified the castle’s inhabitants that no one but the lord dared enter. The baron did not fear the demon in the least and studied there without ceasing. The spirit usually appeared to him several times by day and night in the dress of a secular priest, sat beside him, and often conversed with him at great length while instructing itself about the nature and method of his studies.
