Two of them, however, were thoroughly sly and secretly conspired to cheat the third, who was a simple-minded fellow, out of his share. After they had traveled together for several days, they entered a lonely region and lost the road. They suffered great hardship there. All their food was gone, and only a little flour remained, from which they decided to bake a cake. While the simpleton lit the fire for it, the two rogues discussed how they might divide the cake between themselves alone and cheat him of his share. One said, “Do you know what, dear brother? We will propose that all three of us sleep until the cake is baked. When we wake, each man shall tell what he dreamed, and whoever can tell the most wondrous dream shall have the cake.” No sooner said than done. The two immediately fell asleep, while hunger kept the simpleton awake. As soon as he saw that the cake was baked, he went to it and ate it up without leaving a single crumb.
