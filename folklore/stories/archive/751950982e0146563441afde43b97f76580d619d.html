In old castles, specters have always enjoyed the privilege, because in former times pride, arrogance, money, and injustice were trumps there. People are still afraid of them even now, and I would wager that no one would dare sleep alone for a night in the Armsündersaal, even if he had the finest bed imaginable. At twelve, chains rattle; or the Statthalter comes padding up the long, hollow-sounding stairs with his little stick and old wig; or an old Landvogt comes in at the rear through the Ringmurtürli and searches the wall for the Urner-, Schwizer- oder Glarner-Woupä. Angry that everything looks so neglected, he may come and give you a rough shaking in bed.\nAlbrecht.\n(Erinnerungen an das St. Galler Oberland in Sarganser-Mundart by Frater Hilarius. A.
