Up to the right on a rocky headland there are still remains of walls, called ‘die brocha Burg’—the broken-down castle—in local speech. Northward, a footpath runs through the beautiful beech woods, through the so-called Hölzli toward Sevelen. An older hunter was once waiting here because he expected game. Then seven foxes came in a row from the direction of the Magletschwand. Alarmed, the hunter lowered his gun and let them pass. Suddenly the last called to the one ahead, ‘Wait, Kathrinali!’ The next morning the hunter’s head was enormously swollen, and from then on the man never hunted in this grove again.\nCh.
