When the latter died before him, the living man remembered the vow and went with the vessel of wine to the appointed place. The dead man’s soul or spirit appeared there with the bread, though it did not let itself be seen and allowed only its voice to be heard. The living man then asked how matters stood with the dead man’s soul—whether he was saved or damned. He received the answer that the spirit was not yet permitted to know, because the final judgment had not yet been delivered: he had first been required to keep or discharge the promised vow made in life, as a matter of justice.
