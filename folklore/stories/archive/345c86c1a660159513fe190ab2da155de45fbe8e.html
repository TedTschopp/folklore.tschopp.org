A surveyor named Trog was supposed to measure it for its owners, the Binz family, but he cheated them. When he was discovered and feared the court, he hanged himself farther up in the Gönhard. Now, whenever bad weather is approaching or a misfortune is to occur in the surrounding country, he emerges from the forest on the Distelberg at night and steps onto the country road. A pig dealer once passed that way at night with a wagon full of animals and wanted to apply the drag chain for the descent from the height. Although he pulled and adjusted his chain, it now remained too short. He ran to the nearest house along the road. A tailor who lived there gave him cord and rope with which to lengthen the drag chain, but on the very first attempt these ropes also broke. The pig dealer immediately thought of good Trog, whose haunting he already knew from experience here, where his road had led him past very often. He therefore asked the tailor to grasp his hand as firmly as possible, and together they performed the function of the drag chain down the slope. It went quite well as far as the boundary stone below. There, however, the horses absolutely refused to go farther. At the same moment the clock in Aarau struck twelve, and behold: Trog stepped out onto the road from the footpath that leads to the Binzenhof, a white pointed cap on his head and a measuring rod in his hand. Without paying them any attention, he began surveying the nearest fields; when he had finished, he disappeared into the forest again with equal unconcern. Immediately afterward the stubborn horses moved on of their own accord.", "Whenever Trog is on the footbridge near the forest directly above the Binzenhof barn, the dogs in the farmyard below can no longer be restrained.
