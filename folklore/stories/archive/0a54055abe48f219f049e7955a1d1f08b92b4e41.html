In return, he offered to give them a site for a chapel and even to arrange for its construction, so that they would have to provide only the materials. He also gave some capital for the chapel’s upkeep. The Bauernsame fulfilled all the conditions on its part, but Lord Landammann continually delayed the matter and died before he had fulfilled his promise. Strange things immediately began to occur there. Several honest men testified that they had seen Lord Landammann Reding riding back and forth on a white horse. This helped. His heirs built the chapel on Jberg in the year 1650. But it was not until 1734 that Ferdinand Geist, auxiliary bishop of Constance, consecrated it in honor of the divine Mother, the holy bishop Conrad, George the Martyr, Roch the Confessor, and the holy virgins Barbara and Catherine.
