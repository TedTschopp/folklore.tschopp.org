Satan and evil spirits can do nothing against it; Bozen and haunting spirits are scarcely to be feared when an innocent child keeps one company. Deceivers who know this popular belief often ask for the hands of innocent children in order to discover a promised treasure or perform some other illusion. A mother believes she can pray far more powerfully and pierce the clouds if she holds an innocent child in her arms, nestling against her and clasping her neck. There are also places where an innocent child is brought as close as possible to a dying person, so that it may drive away the evil enemy and receive the departing soul in innocence. Innocent children are therefore gladly seen and well tolerated among the people everywhere.", "This esteem and respect for the innocence of baptized children reaches its height among believing people when one of them has the good fortune to die in that state. Many a legend attests the popular belief that innocent children do not go to heaven alone, but are accompanied there by another armen Seele released from the torments of purification for that purpose. Thus the death of an innocent child is believed always to free, if possible from among the relatives, a suffering soul from the pains of purgatory; it is therefore regarded as a happy event for the entire family, one often requested from heaven in fervent prayers. The good father and tender mother do, of course, shed a few quiet tears for the beloved child; but the joyful awareness of having a beautiful angel in heaven who prays for them at the throne of the Almighty immediately dries those tears and transforms all sorrow into joy. The day of the child's burial becomes a festival of joy for the whole family; parents and relatives put on their wedding clothes, accompany the body to the grave in this attire, and celebrate the happy day within the intimate family circle.
