At last the Älpler summoned all the people of the surrounding area, and they came from every side and formed a ring on the valley floor in order to catch the witch inside it. An entire world of people was gathered together. But the witch did not show herself. They encountered only a Ronen, that is, a tree stump, lying on the ground. No one suspected it of anything evil. They rested; some brought out cheese and bread and began to eat. One lad sat down on the stump and, from time to time, stuck his pocketknife into it, playfully boring and poking into the stump’s rotten wood. No witch appeared, and that day the people dispersed without accomplishing anything. Later the witch was caught after all and, before she was burned, confessed that on that day, when she had conjured the semblance of a Ronen, she had been in the greatest danger of her life.
