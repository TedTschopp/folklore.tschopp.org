While little Melkli slept in the cradle inside the hut, the grandfather sat outside it on the Bänkli with the other children at play, comfortably smoking his pipe. Then the little screamer in the hut raised his powerful voice as a sign of his lively appetite. The children said that they had gone berry-picking. ‘Hansseppli verschwundä, niänä meh!’—‘Hansseppli has vanished, nowhere anymore!’ In vain the old man asked at the neighboring huts. No one knew anything. At last the mother returned home; her anguish over the lost child was heartbreaking. With her sympathetic neighbors she searched the Alp up to the crags and down to the wild Kärschelenbach. There was no trace anywhere.
