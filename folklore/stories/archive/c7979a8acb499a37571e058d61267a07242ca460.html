It is said to have been dug at the command of a Landvogt named Wiladnig at the time when Landvögte still ruled the Bernese Oberamt of Aarwangen, and indeed during a terrible period of scarcity in the land.", "Instead of supporting the people afflicted by hunger with counsel and action, the same Landvogt is said to have forced them to dig this small canal, which carries water from the little Riedsee. Although the poor people exerted themselves almost beyond their powers, they did not manage to complete the work. When the Landvogt learned this, he is said to have cursed terribly and tormented the people, for he remembered his dreadful oath. For a long time afterward, he did not dare go outdoors, for he trembled before every black cloud. Once, when the sky was clear and not a breath of wind could be detected, he rode in a carriage, accompanied only by the coachman, to a neighboring village.
