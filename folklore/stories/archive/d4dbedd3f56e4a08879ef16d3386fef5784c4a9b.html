In Jngenbol there was a young lad whose beard was just beginning to sprout. What happens in such days of youth? In short, young Niderist went z' Licht. But he stayed much too long, and because he had a strict father, he did not dare knock at home so late at night; instead, he slipped into a barn and onto the hay. At that time there were still many vagrants and beggars who customarily spent the night in barns. It was Sunday. Niderist had not lain there long when he heard several voices below in the Gaden, some of them familiar. ‘Eh lueget, die alt Gret chunt au!’ they said—look, old Gret is coming too! Old Gret was a witch. Because she happened to have her pan with her, the beggars would not relent until she stirred in her Häfeli an ointment suitable for air travel. She stirred and stirred, then murmured several words to the travel-hungry company. Niderist heard them above on the hay and, out of Wunderfitz, spoke them too.
