The spring sun shone warmly down and put the washerwomen in a fine mood. Not only were their hands active; their fluent organs of speech, as could hardly be otherwise, likewise found no rest but clattered on without interruption in the liveliest murmuring and hissing. Naturally, the washerwomen’s talk this time fell upon the military expedition and the enemies being fought. ‘I wonder,’ one said, ‘whether those stupid, square, loutish heads up there might not become somewhat rounder and more pliable this time?’ ‘Oh, certainly!’ laughed another. ‘Round and spherical as polished Rhône gravel! Oh, how good that makes my heart feel! My husband promised to bring me home something truly fine from that cow country above, where calves dance and bulls make music. Oh, how I look forward to that happy day! Ah, look how the water is turning red! The proud ringed cows they are slaughtering up along the Rhône today must have beautiful horse blood!
