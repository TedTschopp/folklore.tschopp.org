The villains went away to try their luck with the girl. After spending several hours at an Abendsitz with their beloved, they returned to the scene of the crime and found him dead. To hide their atrocity from punitive justice, they threw the body into the abyss. The legend is silent as to whether their murder received its deserved punishment in this world.
