It appears as a dog and as a Schwarzwälder-Hotzenmann—that is, in the beautifully archaic costume of the farmers from the neighboring County of Hauenstein on the Upper Rhine in Baden near Waldshut. Throughout the Zurzach district, moreover, large dogs are regarded as able to see spirits and even as evil spirits themselves. In former times, the Wälderma usually appeared by the customs house at the Rhine bridge, where old Babeli lived. She knew him so well that whenever he knocked, she would say from inside her room, ‘Why knock? You can come in through any keyhole.’ Usually, an emaciated man with a fixed stare then entered, went straight to the stove, and warmed himself. He never answered. During the war with the French, Appenzell soldiers were stationed here to guard the Rhine frontier, and a few men were also billeted with Babeli. When the Rhima happened to knock at the door on one of those occasions, she sent her Appenzell men to open it. But they all ran away together, and from then on she was spared all soldiers.
