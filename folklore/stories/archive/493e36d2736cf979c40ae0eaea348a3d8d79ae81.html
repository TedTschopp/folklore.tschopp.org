He had already drunk heavily during his arduous day’s march when, on the narrow rocky path near Guttannen, at the steep cliff of the Zuben where passing is dangerous, he encountered the dwarf Selbthan. In his wild drunkenness, the coarse fellow considered it too much trouble to walk forward to the lead horse and carefully guide it aside. From behind, he immediately swung his long twisted leather whip and struck the poor dwarf, raising the thickest welts. The little man pressed himself against the mountainside and cried so pitifully that all the dwarfs from the entire valley came running. Seeing all his property lost, he threw himself down after them in despair.
