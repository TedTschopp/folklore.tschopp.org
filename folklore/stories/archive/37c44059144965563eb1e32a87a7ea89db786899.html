The Senn hurried down to Gersau in utter distress and reported that, up on the Alp beside the three huts where the disaster occurred, two black and shaggy giants had been seen at the same time, towering into the clouds. In each monster's savage head sat a pair of eyes as large as a hundredweight cheese. Even in the morning, the most repulsive smell of sulfur could still be clearly detected at the place where they had remained. To be spared such disaster in the future, the Sennen founded a brotherhood. To this day, a day of penance and supplication is observed on the Feast of Saint James in July at the chapel on the Käppeliberg.
