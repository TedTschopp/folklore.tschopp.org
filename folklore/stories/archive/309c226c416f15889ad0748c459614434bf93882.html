After various conversations, the old man revealed himself to the traveler as a man of Oberhasli whom sorrow had driven from his homeland into foreign lands. His three beautiful daughters had been cursed; and to this day all three, still undelivered from the enchantment, remain on the high mountains of Hasli, where they often show themselves with ghostly manifestations. First, the Gauliweiblein dwells in the Gauli Glacier and, accompanied by a little dog, often appears to the Sennen in the rear Urbachtal. Second, the Engstlenfräulein wanders on the Engstlenalp at the far end of the Genteltal and has been seen many times by the herders. Third, the Geissmaidlein remains on the heights of the beautiful Hasliberg and has often lured solitary lads into lovemaking. Yet when it recently wished to climb into the hayloft of a barn with a handsome, quietly disposed youth, it revealed a pair of goat’s feet. The deeply frightened young man slipped away to the side because at that sight he no longer felt safe.
