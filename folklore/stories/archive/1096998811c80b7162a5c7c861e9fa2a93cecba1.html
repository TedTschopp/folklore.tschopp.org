Along the banks of these pretty little islands, which the swiftly flowing waters everywhere spray with foam, snow-white and golden water lilies bloom. Yet no one comes to pick them. The silence of death always reigns around the islands. Only at certain times can strange tones and noises be heard, which always seem to haunt back and forth among the little islets. Often it sounds as though a mysterious murmuring, a plaintive sigh, or a heavy groan were heard, and then it suddenly roars and surges like a waterfall. The people of Noville consider these islands ill-famed and anxiously avoid them. They claim that the place is far from safe and that those sighs and that terrible crying and roaring are the voice of Fenetta, the Island Maiden, who dwells among the islands and appears from time to time. This Fenetta is said to be a small, pretty creature with green eyes, long reed-green hair, and a delicate face. She always wears a long, richly folded garment and reed sandals. Yet no Noville resident now living has seen her in the flesh. For as soon as the fishermen who pursue lake trout there notice anything of the Island Maiden, or as soon as they hear a sigh and gurgle beneath an overhanging bank or a rustle in an alder bush, they flee headlong from the realm of the uncanny islands. They know well that everyone who beholds Fenetta must die within that same year.
