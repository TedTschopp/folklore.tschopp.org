By the time he reached the area below Balzers, it was already deeply dark. Without warning, smartly dressed people called him aside from the road, and he encountered a splendid company. They told him to sit upon a platform, where he found choice food and drink. One gentleman merely instructed him not to let anything disturb him, to pay attention to nothing, and especially not to drink anyone's health. This struck him as odd, but he remained silent, played, and enjoyed the fare. The dancing became wild and colorful. But no one paid any attention to him, so in the end he grew bored after all. Forgetting the warning, he said inwardly, as was his custom, “Gsundheit Hans! Gseg' ders Gott, Hans! Furcht der nüt, so gschieht der nüt.—Health, Hans! God bless you, Hans!
