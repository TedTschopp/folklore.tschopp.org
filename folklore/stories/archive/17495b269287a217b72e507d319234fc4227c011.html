In the old wooden cottage beside the new schoolhouse in the Wyler at Gurtnellen once lived the wealthy farmer and hunter Sebastian Daxli. The whole Wyler from the Gornerbach to the Märchlital was his.[1] One day, as he came from Fellenen with a chamois on his back, a fahrender Schüler met him on the Fellibrücke and asked whether he was well supplied with lead. “No, I do not always have enough of it,” Daxli replied. The stranger said that he would show him some. He went with Daxli across the “ghäcklet Plattä” and showed him a vein of lead in the Fellitobel. Daxli could fetch a quantity of lead there for himself and his friends, but was not permitted to show anyone the place or sell any of it. Others could watch where he went to obtain his lead, but if they followed the same route, they found nothing. Daxli was rich, but in the end became so poor that he had to sell his estates. For that reason people in Gurtnellen still say of him today:", "Hunter Daxli, unwearied,\nShot chamois by the hundred,\nAnd many foxes and hares besides,\nAnd in the end ate up his own land.", "2.
