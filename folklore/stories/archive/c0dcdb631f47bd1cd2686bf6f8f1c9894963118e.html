Then she bent down toward him, singing a song from which he could not awaken at all. She beckoned to him. He had to leap up and follow her. They went along the rock face on a path only a handbreadth wide until the rock opened like an arched gateway. They penetrated deep into the darkness. Only the woman's eyes competed in brilliance with a wondrous gemstone she wore upon her head. At last, the shepherd boy had to hold onto her golden belt, which gathered her broad, white, pleated dress. Now he shivered as the underground path crossed glaciers; now heat ran up his back. Then a new cave shone so brightly that the radiance blinded his eyes. He felt as though he wished to lie down and sleep, for his limbs were like lead. His companion handed him a golden cup, and when he drank from it in deep draughts, a river of fire passed through his body. Yet his longing for the outside world had not been extinguished.
