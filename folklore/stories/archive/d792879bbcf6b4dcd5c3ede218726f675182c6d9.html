A spectral fox was said to haunt the place. A fearless hunter reportedly said: ‘If I load blessed powder into my rifle, I shall try whether I cannot lay this fox down to sleep on the Beize too.’ Thus, on a winter night, he went expressly to a stable where the haunting was said to occur and waited at the Fuchs-Passi. Around twelve o'clock at night, a great fox truly came onto the Beize. The hunter raised his gun and fired. The weapon was flung from his hands all the way to the farthest wall of the stable. Frightened yet courageous, he wanted to look out through the Glotz—the stable window—to see whether he had hit it. There, his face struck an ice-cold human face, as though it too wished to peer inside. Despite the terror this Zusammenturnen caused him, he hurried home.
