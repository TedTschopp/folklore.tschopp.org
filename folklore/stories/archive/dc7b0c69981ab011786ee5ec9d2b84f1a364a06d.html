As they were carried away, their companions are said to have called after them that whatever might happen to them among the humans, they must never reveal where the ‘Salzbrunnen im Sortel’ was located or what purpose the ‘Beikörnlein,’ or secondary grain, in the oats served. But at last, they may have grown tired of this performer's life, in which a great number of curious eyes were always directed upon them, or they may have longed for their own people and become homesick. In short, they asked that each be given a ball of thread and then that a window be opened, saying they would show something truly interesting.
