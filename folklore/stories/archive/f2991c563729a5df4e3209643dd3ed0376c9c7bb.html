The rumbling and clattering were so loud that one might have believed everything would lie broken to pieces together on the floor in the morning. But when people looked in the morning, not only was nothing broken, every utensil stood in the same place where it had been put the evening before. Often, especially on the eves of holy days, the stove bench was heard creaking exactly as though a heavy person sat down upon it; indeed, people believed they could hear the person panting and groaning. The spirit was good-natured and harmed or hurt no one. Since the house was demolished, nothing more has been perceived of the spirit.
