Not far away, a gorge deepens through which the so-called Bala-Bach flows down. Before reaching the gorge, however, one comes to a small chapel where the Mother of God is venerated. When the mountain people wish to go to Bodmen, they customarily say several Aves in the church in honor of Mary, all the more because the gorge is notorious for the uncanny Gratzug that travels here from the heights into the depths. A certain Fortnaz, a pious and upright man, never failed to pray before the image of the Mother of God when passing this chapel. No matter how violently it stormed and rained, as soon as Fortnaz reached this chapel, he always greeted Mary with an Englischen Grusse. Once it was already deep into the night when he had to pass there, and urgent business put him in a great hurry; yet even this time, he did not omit his customary devotion. And see: as he was about to step onto the bridge over the Bala-Bach, he heard from afar a terrible tumult approaching him with the speed of lightning. When he looked up into the gorge, he saw before him a long procession led by a tremendous beast. Yawning, the animal opened its jaws toward him to devour him, but a higher power prevented it; then, with dreadful grunting, it plunged through the depths into the deep gorge. Behind the beast, men and women, children and old people—people of every station and region—followed in frantic haste, all raising such clamor and din that he had never heard its like. He thought he recognized some of them. He would gladly have spoken with them, but the rushing speed with which they swept past did not permit it; he could only gaze after them in horror. The last man, however, stopped by the bridge before Fortnaz and said: ‘Know that this is the Gratzug, which year in and year out, by day and night, without rest or repose, travels over mountain and valley and through every land, seeking to destroy humankind. The beast would have swallowed you too if you had not prayed and Mary had not helped you. But I must hurry, for my company is already a thousand miles ahead. Oh, woe is me that I must run with such a procession!’ And the man vanished.
