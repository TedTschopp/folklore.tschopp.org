Legend says that whenever the Flühluft blows across the mountains and the snow threatens to melt, he is condemned to leave this resting place. Wherever he sees loose stakes in the river barriers, he drives them firm again with powerful blows or replaces them with new ones when necessary. The blows of his battle-axe sound dull and dreadful through the night, but to nearby residents they are a warning to protect their property in good time from the swelling waters of the Emme. The Baron of Brandis must perform this watchman's duty until the Emme, grown tame, no longer needs barriers.
