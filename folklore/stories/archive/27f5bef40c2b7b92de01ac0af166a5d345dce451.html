A hired hand never had any rest at night, and although he was not burdened with too much work and the food was good, he visibly grew thinner and thinner. Every night, a witch came, lifted a floorboard beside his bed, and threw a horse's halter over him. He then became a horse, and the witch mounted him and rode away. At a certain place, she always dismounted, tied the horse to a fence, and left him. Before the prayer bell rang, she returned and rode him home. Once, however, as the horse stood tied to the fence again, it occurred to him to pull off the halter; it seems that a human brain still worked inside the horse's head. He became a man again. As soon as he caught sight of the witch, he threw the halter over her with lightning speed. Now she was the horse, and the hired hand rode her home, put her in the stable, fetched the master, and showed him the unusual little horse, saying, ‘Look here, Master, what a fine Mäni I have bought!’ ‘Well, why ever not!’ the master replied.
