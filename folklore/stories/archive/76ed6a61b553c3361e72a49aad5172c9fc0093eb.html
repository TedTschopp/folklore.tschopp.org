It looked exactly like a small, shaggy black bear with eyes that sprayed fire. Yet there were reckless people bold enough to challenge the creature they called the Ungeheuerlein with insults and wrestle with it in the darkness. Among these brawling heroes were the Brügger brothers of Zur March. Whenever they went home from Plaffeien in a drunken haze, they never failed to call out in the Sagenboden: “Hey, Ungeheuerlein, where are you hiding?
