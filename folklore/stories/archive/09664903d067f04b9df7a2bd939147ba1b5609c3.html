Later he stayed in the district of Rotenburg near Lucerne and stood at the head of an entire society of women who, like the witch-mother in Küssnacht, practiced necromancy. It was around the year 1577. Hans Riss conjured spirits as follows. In the house where the specter was, he first measured all the doors, and even the entire house, with a thread. Afterward a circle was drawn in the parlor. Seated at the table, the conjurer then spoke fifteen words, all concerning the Passion of Our Lord, and thereby summoned the specter to appear within the circle. Holy water and a palm branch lay on the table. If the spirit bound in this way was not evil but capable of redemption, it appeared inside the circle in a white form and gave a sign. But if it was an evil specter, it came only as far as the circle and raged mightily without becoming visible. For such accursed spirits, no entreaty or prayer should be made.", "He claimed to have learned this art from the woman in Küssnacht around 1561.
