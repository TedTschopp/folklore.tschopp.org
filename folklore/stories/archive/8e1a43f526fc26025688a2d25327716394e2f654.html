After a long, arduous wade through the snow, he reached the end of the gorge, which opened into a dark passage. There he noticed a gleaming light and made his way toward it. As he drew nearer, he was startled anew. An ugly dragon dwelled there, pressed against the wall. It fixed its glowing eyes upon him and greedily lapped up, with its long outstretched tongue, the liquid dripping down the walls. When the beast made no move to kill him and he began to feel hunger and thirst, he too put his mouth to the wall and began licking up the yellowish-red, sticky liquid that seeped over the stone. It tasted sweet, like wild bees' honey, and satisfied him well. After the meal he sat down on the ground and soon fell asleep. When he awoke, the dragon was still there and behaved as though it had not noticed him at all. The cave was as warm as the parlor at home.
