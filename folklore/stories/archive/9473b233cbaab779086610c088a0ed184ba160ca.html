The Vuivra, it is told, is a flying monster that wears a crown upon its head, has fire for wings, and resembles a dragon in body. It feeds upon gold sand, which it alternately stirs up and devours from the bottoms of the three larger mountain lakes. When the bottom of one lake has been exhausted, it rises from the water into the air and hastens in dreadful flight to another lake, there to graze again upon the gold sand newly accumulated by the waters during its absence. It may happen that the gluttonous monster, enclosed beneath solid ice, finds the winter too long and sometimes has only a lean Fassnacht left to it. It then delivers such mighty blows against the hard crust of ice that mountain and valley thunder powerfully all around.", "Furthermore, a wild rider—a Cavalier—gallops through the air in the Eringertal; the clash of weapons and the pounding of hooves can be distinctly heard. On land, meanwhile, a red bull followed by a little black dog disturbs the valley dwellers' peace. It bellows so terribly that even the kitchen utensils begin to shake. A great sow followed by twelve piglets also wanders through the valley. Red tracks are often found in the snow.
