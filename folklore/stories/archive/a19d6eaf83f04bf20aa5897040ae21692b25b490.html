The animal crept softly toward his bed and was just about to sniff his face when he sprang up to seize it by the neck. But the cat was quicker than he was. It would not allow itself to be caught and fought with teeth and claws to fend off its attacker. At last, he managed to draw his knife. Gripping the cat by the neck with his left hand, he was about to deal it a fatal thrust with his right when it began to speak and begged for its life in moving words. ‘Great will be my brothers' vengeance if you kill me,’ it finally said, ‘and misfortune upon misfortune will change your evil disposition.’ The farmer could not resist the plea and let it go.
