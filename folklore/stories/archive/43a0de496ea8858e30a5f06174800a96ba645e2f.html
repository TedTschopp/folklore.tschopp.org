In the middle of the hall stood a beautiful maiden dressed in white. She approached him and offered him three gifts, explaining that she was enchanted and had awaited deliverance for many hundreds of years. It now lay in his hands to free her; everything depended only on which gift he chose. The gifts were a pot full of gold, a golden cowbell, and the maiden herself. The herder remembered his dearest beloved at home and chose the cowbell. The maiden rose in anger. At the same time, a dreadful crash of thunder broke around him. As if by an invisible hand, he was thrust from the underground hall and fell stunned beside the rock portal.
