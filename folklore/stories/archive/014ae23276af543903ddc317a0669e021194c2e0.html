Here is a sorrowful legend:", "In the high alpine pasture of ‘Pointet,’ the highest in the Sanetsch Valley and the subject of the preceding legend, the large alpine cauldron was regularly stolen every winter. This was a serious loss for the Senntumsgeteilen. Since every other precaution had failed, they therefore ordered the newly elected Alpenvogt to carry the newly purchased cauldron away from the hut and secure it somewhere else.", "The task weighed heavily on the appointee. He was therefore very pleased when an old soldier, who had traveled far and wide, promised to tell him of a means that would not only secure the alpine cauldron but would also unfailingly deliver the thief into his hands. He need only check frequently so that the rogue would not have to wait too long for him. Our Alpenvogt used the prescribed means and looked often to see whether there was any sign of the thief. For a long time his searches had no success. Then bad weather suddenly set in, and snow fathoms deep cut off all access to the alpine pasture for months.", "When the snow had receded far enough for the ascent to the alpine pasture to be possible again, our Alpenvogt hurried up at once to learn whether the alpine cauldron had been stolen again. — And behold! He found the thief there indeed, with the alpine cauldron on his back, just on the point of stepping across the threshold—but dead and half decayed. The man sank to the ground as soon as the Alpenvogt touched the alpine cauldron with his hand.
