She must guard her treasure forever in the Schrattenhöhle unless someone is bold enough to risk his life solving the enchanted maiden's riddle. But that requires courage. She appears only on hohen Donnerstag, when the bells are rung in church for the last time at the Gloria. Whoever is then at the cave entrance can reach her, for only at that hour does she sit at its mouth, combing her golden hair and counting her money. Anyone who now wishes to raise her hidden treasure must pass beneath a millstone hanging from a thread of spun yarn while the maiden, or a spirit, threatens to cut the thread in two with scissors. Even after a person has survived this adventure, an equally dangerous trial still awaits: he must solve the difficult riddle the maiden sets him while seated beside the spring at the rear of the cave. If he cannot, he is lost.
