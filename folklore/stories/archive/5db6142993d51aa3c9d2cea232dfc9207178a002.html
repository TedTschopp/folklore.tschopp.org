The following tale, which also has siblings in the Netherlands, is included here as a small contribution to this question, which in part still awaits investigation.", "In Ingenbohl, a Kiltgänger’s route took him across the Gottesacker every evening when he made his visits. He had a cheerful disposition and, above all, a noble and compassionate soul. He could not bring himself to walk among the graves without good-naturedly saying a few Lord’s Prayers for the “dear souls.” He remembered especially the schamrote Seelen, meaning the poor executed people. When he had prayed, he stood and went on his way, crying out joyfully. The local pastor heard with displeasure this young man’s peace-disturbing whoops so close to a sacred place and ordered the sacristan to keep watch the next evening and cure this “night rogue” of his noise when he came. At the usual hour, the young man again approached with his customary display. When the watcher tried to get at him, he saw two men of gigantic size walking beside him, so he dared do nothing. The second time, the sacristan kept watch with four others. The young man again approached with joyful cries, this time guarded by six large, strong figures, three walking ahead and three behind. All had red stripes around their necks. A third time the Sigrist tried and lay in wait. This time he took eight men with him and hoped infallibly to master the young shouter. But what terror seized them all! At nine o’clock, his customary hour, the cheerful crier came surrounded by a great host of men carrying severed heads in their hands. The sacristan hurried to the pastor and related what had happened. The next morning the pastor had the young man summoned and asked who accompanied him every evening. Astonished, the man replied that he had never had another person with him, but whenever he went home in the evening he said a short prayer and then strode cheerfully onward. The pastor was satisfied with this account. He laid a blessing hand on the crier’s head and said, “May the Lord’s protection against every enemy always grow within you.
