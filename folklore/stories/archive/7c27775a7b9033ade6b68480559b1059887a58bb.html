There he encountered a horse that repeatedly tumbled before his feet but vanished as soon as he began to curse. After he had slept for a while that night, someone knocked outside his window. Thinking the hired threshers had arrived, he rose; but as soon as he opened the chamber door, a glowing furnace stood on the threshold, so that the farmer fell over it and burned his legs.
