On the ‘Egg,’ night and fog overtook him, and he found himself forced to spend the night at the Wallhalb. He put the cow in the stable, found the abandoned Küherlager in the firehouse, and soon slept like a prince. During the night, however, the Erdmännchen came, took the cow from the stable, slaughtered it, and then cooked the meat in the Käskessi before the eyes of the frightened little farmer, who had awakened at the noise and was forced to watch it all. They prepared a meal and lived splendidly and joyfully. They also offered some to the unwilling spectator in a friendly manner, but he took only a very small piece of meat and, instead of thanking the giver, would have preferred to send him to the land of pepper. After the meal had ended, the dwarfs departed, taking the remains with them. When the farmer rose in the morning and was about to set out gloomily and sadly for home without his cow, he was surprised to find no traces of blood before the hut. He entered the stable to see whether the cow might have been slaughtered there. How astonished he was to find it unharmed at the Barren!
