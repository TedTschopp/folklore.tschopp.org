Saddest of all is that such quarrels and disputes very often occur within one and the same family, among relatives and brothers, and are inherited across generations.", "Among the many examples that everyone may have seen, and may still see, with their own eyes, a legend from St. Niklaus tells of one. In the previous century, one of two brothers was extremely quarrelsome and loved and practiced brawls and litigation. Thus he brought a very long and costly lawsuit against his brother over a Hotzdecke—pieced and woven together from waste tow. Two thousand pounds had already been spent in court costs over the miserable article, and the affair remained so tangled and confused that the judges were at a loss to pronounce judgment. They therefore summoned the feuding brothers once more to reconcile and asked them to settle the affair amicably. One brother declared himself ready to contribute everything possible toward peace, but the other stated firmly and resolutely that he would continue the lawsuit even if he had to see his brother die upon the gallows because of it.
