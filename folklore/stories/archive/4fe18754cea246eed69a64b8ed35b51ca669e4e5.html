Every ten years, an unknown woman came onto an Alp in the canton of Glarus, wandered among the cows, and then patted the finest cow on the muzzle, saying, ‘That really is a lovely little cow!’ This animal was then always doomed to destruction. At last they acquired a farmhand from Uri, who ordered, ‘The next time the witch pats a cow, tell me at once; I will know what to do.’ They obeyed him, and when they fetched him, he immediately milked the cow, poured the milk into a small iron kettle, placed it over the fire, and stirred the milk with a cow chain into which he had woven a blessed hazel twig. They heard terrible screaming and wailing, and an invisible voice cried, ‘Stop, for God’s sake! I certainly will never come again.’ But the farmhand did not relent until all the milk had evaporated.
