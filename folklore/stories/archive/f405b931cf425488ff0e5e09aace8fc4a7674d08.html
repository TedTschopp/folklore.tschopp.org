The site where this occurred, a small elevation, is still called the Büni. Shaken by the outcome of the Battle of Kappel, popular tradition relates, Zurich instructed its envoy that if no other way were possible and the victorious Catholics insisted upon it, they would accommodate themselves once again to the old faith; but he should try to determine whether remaining with the new confession could be obtained with money. Catholic envoys truly allowed themselves to be bribed, and Zurich remained Reformed. For this, however, they forfeited the peace of their souls for all time and must still reveal guilt and punishment to people as specters. At the Büni stands a house whose inhabitants often witnessed the nocturnal haunting enacted by mounted spirits called Schimmelreiter. They do not tolerate their customary circuit being blocked.
