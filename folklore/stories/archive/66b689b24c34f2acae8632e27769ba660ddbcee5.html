Only two years ago, in broad daylight, a girl bringing food to workers in the Biffigwald is said to have met a fox that would not leave the path. When it came closer and had scarcely passed her, she saw that the fox had two heads, and soon afterward it vanished in the forest. Often something has reportedly been heard grunting like a great pig; it has often been seen as a horse and also as a bull where nothing of the kind was anywhere nearby. People also met a hare with three legs there, heard a rooster crowing loudly, and are said to have encountered a wind that suddenly rose so strongly that the forest trees swayed back and forth with a dreadful roaring; yet when they stepped out of the woods, other people claimed to have felt nothing of it. At night, a rare and hideous cry was also often heard, like the call of an owl or eagle owl. For that reason, certain people say that even in broad daylight they are afraid in this forest." ], "notes": [ "Swiss term “Bozo”: a Valais regional name for a frightening supernatural presence or bogey; here it is not given one fixed body but manifests through malformed or misplaced animals, uncanny cries, and a wind confined to the forest." ] }, "legacy_urls": [] } ---
