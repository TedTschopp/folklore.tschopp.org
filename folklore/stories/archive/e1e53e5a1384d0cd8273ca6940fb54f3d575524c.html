In former times, a peasant girl serving in Zug is said to have hurled her child into the depths of the Mühlibach. After her death, the murderess often appeared at the midnight hour as a finely adorned female figure in rural dress with a double-rosette hairpin, especially to young fellows whose late-night visits brought them near the Geisboden. She was confined within a certain circuit extending almost to Oberwil. At the Mühlibach stood a large, flat stone where people claimed sometimes to have seen her washing even in broad daylight. One day, an insolent young fellow from Oberwil took it into his head to soil this stone. The following night, while he lay in bed uneasy over his deed, there came a knock on his house door at the stroke of twelve. When the frightened man looked through the window, he saw the Mühlibach-Dame herself, who threateningly ordered him to go immediately to the place in question and clean the stone. She promised him safety if he obeyed, so the terrified man set out, cleaned the lady's washing stone with pounding heart, and returned home without seeing the ghost again.", "Even in very recent times, two men from the Walchwilerberg claimed to have seen her.
