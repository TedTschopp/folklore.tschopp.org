At around midnight one came along—a gruesome one! I took aim, and the shot went off properly. But it roared outrageously. By God, I felt something touch me on the back from behind. The devil has it, I thought, and I got up to look. But I could find absolutely nothing. There were only tracks in the snow like those of two finches, and they went down through the ravine toward the Reuss. I did not follow them far. I thought, You go home, took the gun in my hands, and left. But that thing did not look pretty! The whole barrel was bent, and at the outermost edge it was finely split and bent back like a Wychwadel. The next day I was able to take it to the smith Bümä at Steg and have it shortened.
