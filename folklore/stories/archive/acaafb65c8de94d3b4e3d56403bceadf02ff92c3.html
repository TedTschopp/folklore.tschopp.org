The Hubel of this mountain is much higher than the summit visible in every direction, and just there lies such a strange hole in the earth. Some boys once threw a cat into it, but it emerged alive from a spring at the foot of the Fluh. Another boy was held by his companions while he looked into the abyss. At first he saw black, pitch-dark night. Suddenly the darkness moved from its place, for it was a gigantic black bird that had hung underground in the air with wings spread and that, as it flew away, revealed the empty blue beyond. They had to pull the boy away by his feet, or in his dizziness he would have sunk after the bird.
