When they searched, the child was found drowned in the nearby brook. This child had been the maiden's liberator and therefore was likewise not to remain long without the reward of blessedness. The maiden's fate had been bound to one of the fir trees on the ruin. That tree had been felled and fashioned into the cradle of this very child, who was lost to the parents so early. When the castle tree fell and was made into a cradle in which a little child had slept until learning to walk, the destined condition was fulfilled.
