Someone to whom he complained pointed out that there was probably a hole in the floor or wall through which the monster entered. He searched and indeed found a hole in one corner, rubbed completely smooth by the constant slipping in and out. As advised, the following night, as soon as he felt the pressure, he got up, took a well-fitting wooden plug, and drove it firmly into the suspicious hole. When he awoke in the morning, a naked woman sat on the bench and implored him for clothing. He gave her some.
