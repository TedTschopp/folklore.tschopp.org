During the so-called blood vengeance carried out against the murderers of Albrecht of Austria, it was destroyed along with other castles in these lands. Nearby lies the little Flueren wood, and within it the Mumeli, a lonely spot surrounded by ancient fir trees. People say that many centuries ago, on this little plot, a man of Fahrwangen buried two knights whom he had stabbed during a drinking bout at his castle. Now he must still leave his family tomb and, every night, run here to this place in great bounds across the fields. Sometimes he is seen in white, sometimes in black. He cuts the nose and ears from a wood thief and carries him to the lake in a sack. He also appears in the form of a dwarf. He can be recognized most of all by his cries. Between the villages of Meisterschwanden and Sarmensdorf, he whoops like someone setting out to hunt; on the lakeshore, he calls plaintively. The people call this wehweln and wehvern, and call him himself the Mumelimann or the Runelimâ.", "On the footpath that leads from Meisterschwanden down over the high lakeshore to the ferry crossing to the village of Birrwil on the opposite side stands a single steep hill, the Kapf. There, a dead man makes his call for passage so often and so distinctly that, for a long time, the Birrwil boatmen would no longer leave the opposite shore merely when someone called. Instead, they appeared only after a blast of a horn, to take aboard those waiting on this side and ferry them across. Fearless people, including lakeside residents themselves, have often tried to discover where this voice, audible to everyone with its uniform, mournful tones, might come from. When they stood on the Kapf, they heard the call from the Schleiferhübel, a brush-covered hill of stone about a thousand paces farther north; while people standing there at the same time believed that those on the Kapf had called to them. Thus even unbelievers think that something supernatural lies behind it. The country people assert that this call is heard whenever rainy weather is about to set in or a violent thunderstorm is about to break.
