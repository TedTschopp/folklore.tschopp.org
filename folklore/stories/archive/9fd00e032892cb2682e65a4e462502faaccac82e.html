It rolled along boulders as high as houses, several of which can still be seen today in the streambed and the adjoining Spillmattli. Two witches came riding upon the erupting debris and brought a mighty stone with them; one pulled and the other pushed. A Meitli in the Hostet saw it. As quickly as she could, she ran to St. Anthony’s Chapel in the middle of the hamlet and rang the little bell with all her strength. And behold! The storm subsided, the Rübi stood still, and the boulder remained where it was; all the old witches’ tugging and kicking could no longer move it. ‘Push, Lunni, push!’ cried one. ‘Rageeri (or Grageeri), pull!
