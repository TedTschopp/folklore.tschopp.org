At that time, the river, which forms the boundary between the cantons of Solothurn and Aargau here, still had no bridge. Dutifully, the old man rowed across but found no one. When his call for the passenger to hurry received no answer, he finally turned back. In the middle of the river, however, he thought he heard the same call from high upon the Bornberg in a woman’s voice. A moment later, it truly called from the bank below, and the boatman believed he saw something white moving there. He therefore crossed again. But when for the second time he could find no one on the bank, the matter began to seem strange, and he quickly turned his Weidling homeward. On his own side, he heard the same voice for the third time, but at that moment so tremendous a storm broke loose in the air that the Fehr would inevitably have been lost had he followed the call once more.", "In relating this, he did not wish to claim outright that it had been the Bornjungfrau herself.
