When he glanced back, he saw three men following him. He quickened his steps, and the men did the same. He resumed his usual pace, and they too walked more slowly. At last, he stopped and waited for them. He wanted to know whom he was dealing with, for they were continually speaking to one another about boundaries and Marchen. He addressed them, and they now identified themselves as former Landsmarcher and confessed that during their lives they had falsified the boundary markers for the benefit of their relatives. They also implored him to put the matter right. The priest promised to do so. It seems that he delayed a little, but he had no peace in his house. There was noise and pounding—it was one great clatter—until he earnestly set about fulfilling his promise. He went to the relatives of the three Landsmarcher and urged them to return the property obtained unjustly. At first, they refused to believe him. He visited them again and told them that the matter gave him no peace. At last, they resolved to set things right.
