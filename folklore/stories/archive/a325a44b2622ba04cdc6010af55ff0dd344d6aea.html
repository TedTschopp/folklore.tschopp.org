Nevertheless, he continued his free way of life, enlarged his cell, and justified everything by invoking a patent issued to him for this purpose by the Prince-Abbot of Einsiedeln. Even after the diocese of Constance expelled him from the country, he appealed to the Landvogt and clergy. He insisted that only the gravest sacrilege could cast doubt upon the authenticity of the body of Saint Firmanus that he had brought into the land, and that God now wished graciously to confirm it through a miracle.
