Weary of the world, they bequeathed their homestead to the Monastery of St. Johann in the Thur valley and verpfründeten themselves there, exchanging their property for lifelong support. Their first and only meal from the monastery kitchen was milk soup. After enjoying it, they went to bed, where they died peaceful deaths. Popular speech therefore says that the beautiful farmstead was sold for a bowl of milk soup.\nA.
