He left his old mother and his sweetheart below. He told his sweetheart, ‘Come up sometime for a Stubäti,’ but said nothing to his mother. One beautiful Sunday, he looked down and saw a woman approaching. The fellow thought it was his sweetheart and went to meet her.", "But when he came nearer, he saw that it was his mother.
