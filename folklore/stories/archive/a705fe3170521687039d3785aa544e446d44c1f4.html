Michel's sister still had a stony field there. When the clocks in the towers of Ganterschwil, Bütschwil, and Lütisburg struck 12, a dull sound was heard in the Hagenau. At the edge of the stony field, the earth opened, and out came Michel, pale and haggard, with whip in hand. The white horse immediately followed him and threw itself upon its former tormentor. Michel fled in terror. He ran back and forth along the edge of the field, continually pursued by his white horse. After each circuit, he moved one foot nearer the field's center, thus traversing the entire field. Everything had to happen within an hour; then the man, dripping with sweat and exhausted nearly to death, sank upon the field, which swallowed him and the white horse again with the first stroke of the bell.", "Thus Michel's breach of trust and cruelty to animals were terribly punished, and the threat of his wife, Anna Babeli, who had often warned him, came true.
