Suddenly a beautiful maiden dressed in white stood before him. To the great relief of the frightened man, she told him that he had nothing to fear from her. During his lifetime, her father had often acquired great riches unjustly and buried them in nearby Fracstein Castle. She, his innocent daughter, had to guard the treasure in atonement for her father’s crimes until she was redeemed and the treasure raised. If he wished to redeem her, he should return to this spot at midnight that very night. In her place, however, a hideous serpent would come, threatening him with danger and destruction and bearing a ring of keys around its neck. If he succeeded in pulling this key ring from it, she would be redeemed and he would become the owner of all the treasures she had guarded until then. If he failed, she could not be redeemed for another hundred years, and he too would be lost. Captivated and driven by desire for wealth as well as by the lovely apparition, he promised to appear and accomplish the task.", "Near midnight he duly stood at the appointed spot and waited for whatever was to come. He summoned all his courage to fulfill his promise and had already grasped the bunch of keys three times with his hands, but each time he let it go again. With a muffled roar of fury, the monster now plunged down toward the stream, and soon everything was still and quiet once more.
