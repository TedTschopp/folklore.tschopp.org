A red woolen shirt reached below his hips; he wore red knee breeches and red stockings, buckle shoes with glittering brass buckles a finger’s breadth wide, and on his head a so-called Dreischnörrihut. Such a hat, which could be tied with cords into a three-pointed shape but spread broadly into a floppy hat against the rain, had indeed once been customary in this region in ancient times.
