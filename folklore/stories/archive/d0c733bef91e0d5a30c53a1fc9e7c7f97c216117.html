It rises at the roadside bank where the footpath by the mill rejoins the carriage road, flows across the road there, and enters the nearby Surb. Half a hundred paces before the mouth of this little field stream is the setting for the legend of the golden maiden called the Boddemaidschi and the Boddebächlijumpfere. Dressed in white, she comes to this Boddebächli, combs her hair there, and at the same time guards treasures for which people often dug in former times. The maiden is encountered not infrequently, nor is she in any way regarded as an evil spirit; nevertheless, people do not particularly like to pass that place.
