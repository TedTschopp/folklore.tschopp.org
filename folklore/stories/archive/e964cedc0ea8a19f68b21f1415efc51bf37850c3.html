Now, to be sure, birds sing in the wild-rose bushes. But even today, people do not like to pass at night between those yews and dense red firs, and one need not be a Sonntagskind to notice traces of mighty earthworks and deep underground structures. People believe a castle stood here on the height in olden times, of which only the name Adlisberg remains. Nothing is known of its inhabitants or the cause of its destruction. Nevertheless, my great-grandfather and other men already dug here for treasure. On a new-moon night, they succeeded in finding a shaft. At the moment they intended to enter it, the ground beneath their feet began to sway with a great rushing. Dismayed, they looked out toward the sky and down into the little valley, where they saw a burning Viertel rolling out over the Oelbach, growing ever larger and more aflame. It fluttered and crackled as though the entire region stood in fire, and by its reflection they could clearly see the church windows flashing in the village. Then, with roaring and cracking behind them, the shaft collapsed, and a voice called:", "‘Oh, I wanted far too much;", "‘My torment has no end.’", "It is still unknown whether this was the miller on the Oelbach who took too much from the people, or the knights of the castle.
