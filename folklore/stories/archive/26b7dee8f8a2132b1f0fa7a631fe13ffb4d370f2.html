All at once, as though it had grown from the ground, a monastery gate stood before them that had never been seen before or afterward. They did not dare knock and ran away. But they regretted it, gathered fresh courage, went back, and knocked. A woman’s voice called from within that the keys lay on the lintel above the door; they need only reach up and open it. When the two hesitated, half frightened, the voice called again that they should reach up and open the door. Now they did so and found inside a maiden, who bade them sit at a table set with food and drink, saying: ‘Eat and drink, and do not be frightened when, after one hour, twelve black men come and beat me into little pieces upon the anvil. When they were gone, the fellows filled their sacks and meant to leave. Out of greed, one took so much that he could not properly move onward beneath the burden and asked the other to help carry it, promising gratitude.
