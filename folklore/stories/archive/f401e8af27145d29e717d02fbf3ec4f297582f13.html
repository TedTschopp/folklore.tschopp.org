The people of the area wanted to show him their gratitude but did not know how. One day they set a Schöppli of the best Veltliner wine on the stone for him. The Mannli regarded the wine for a long time and considered almost anxiously whether he wished to drink it. At last he put it to his lips with extreme caution. The wine visibly pleased him, and he drank the entire Schöppli. Another time they set a pair of shoes on the stone for him. The Mannli looked thoroughly astonished and tried to put the shoes on over his head. Little by little he nevertheless became clever enough to put them on his feet.
