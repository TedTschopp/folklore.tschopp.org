Imhof-Tresch, 52 Jahre alt." ], "english_paragraphs": [ "Our father († 1923, 84 years old)—he often told this story—was looking out for the neighbor's kids one evening and was standing at the Riggäport at Gufern in the Maderanertal. Some distance away, in the so-called ‘hählä Schachä,’ he saw by a stone the figure of a cowherd. The figure held his staff behind his back with both hands, wore gray woolen clothes and a gray hat, and had his trousers laced up to the knees. He saw everything quite distinctly. The cowherd went around the stone three times and then continued on, springing over the Sydenbach—it was as though he were flying. Father followed him for some distance; then the apparition turned back and came toward Father. Father now went home and noticed that the stranger followed him as far as the bridge. After Father had eaten supper, he went to his customary bed and closed the hut door. He awoke in the night. Someone came and lifted the door latch, then went three times around the hut and threw something against its door that sounded like a dry cowhide. He entered the hut, climbed the ladder to the Ruossdiele, and noisily threw himself down there. Father then fell asleep. The next morning he had forgotten everything and went to work; when he returned home that evening, it came back to him. He made his bed and found in it—two Tuolen im Nätsch! That spirit must therefore have slept beside him, for the two Tuolen lay side by side and were clearly separated from one another.", "Fr.
