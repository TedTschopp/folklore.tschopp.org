To restore them according to the old boundary descriptions that are still preserved, both communities appeared at the boundary one day and jointly set fresh stones so that each ran toward the next in a perfectly straight line pointing to Wittnau's church tower. The present course of Wölflinswil's boundary, however, has become entirely different and wholly contradicts the documentary provisions. The Bowäldler is to blame, a man who was then Sigrist and Gemeindeammann of Wittnau. On the very next night, for the benefit of his own community, he dug up the newly set markers and placed them so that the Wölflinswil people lost many Jucharten of their communal territory. When they complained and requested a judicial Augenschein, a large pear tree was already growing at the site of their first reference stone. The deceitful enemy had planted it there, thereby interrupting the reliable line for all the boundary stones that followed. The Wölflinswil people retained the loss, while the deceitful Ammann from then on bore the insulting nickname Bowäldler. When his conscience awakened over it, he was found one day hanged from the ropes of the church clock. A person from Wölflinswil therefore still teases a Wittnau resident he meets with the greeting: ‘Is the Bowäldler still hanging up by the Zeitstein?’ They also insult one another as Oerkensünder and Balmerainsünder because the Ammann led the false boundary across the Wölflinswil communal land called Oerkenhalde and Balmenrain.
