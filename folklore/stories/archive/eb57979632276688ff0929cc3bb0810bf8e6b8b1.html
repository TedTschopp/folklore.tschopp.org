Nor was it necessary: a dwarf's daughter had settled down domestically with him, and her father supplied them both abundantly with Wildbret; they had milk in plenty. At that time, skim milk was made into low-fat cheese, but Ziegern was still unknown. The farmer's son learned it from the dwarf woman. She further explained that the real gold was contained in the Schotten. She also wished to show how to extract it and was working in the Käskessi when the hut door opened and the young man's father appeared. In his anger, he threw her out. The son now related everything and caused his father no small astonishment. But regret came too late. The dwarf woman could no longer be found, nor could the gold in the Schotten.\nL.
