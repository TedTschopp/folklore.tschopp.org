I know you and trust you; for you must know that I am not living, but the spirit of a dead man.’ An ice-cold shudder passed over our good-natured questioner; yet he remained standing and compassionately listened to the softly sighing dead man. — The latter went on to relate that in life he had been a foreign cattle dealer who had often allowed his cattle to run and graze at this very place. He had not thought this a serious offense; yet he could not be released until sufficient amends had been made. He therefore turned to this man and asked him to help. His heirs ought indeed to do it, but it would be useless to tell them anything because they would not believe or fulfill it and would only burden their consciences. He should therefore tell them nothing but help from his own charity, which the spirit would strive to repay; for his release he lacked alms and a pair of Holy Masses.
