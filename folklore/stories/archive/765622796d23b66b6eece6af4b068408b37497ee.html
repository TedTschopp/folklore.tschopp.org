For the convenience of the house's former inhabitants, a round pane of glass had been set into the parlor door. A mother also had a child at this dance. She took a small, innocent child in her arms and went to the house to fetch her dancing child. When she reached the dancing room, she secretly looked in through the round pane. There, in the middle of the circle of merry people, she saw a little green man with a long tail. As soon as the musicians finished their dance tune, the Devil, who had been leaping in their midst, also stood still. As soon as the musicians struck up another dance, the spirit leaped up as well; and when the dance began, the little green man danced too, but entirely alone. Greatly frightened, the mother entered the room with the innocent child and openly told what she had seen. Everyone sprang up and fled in terror. Each person wanted to be first.
