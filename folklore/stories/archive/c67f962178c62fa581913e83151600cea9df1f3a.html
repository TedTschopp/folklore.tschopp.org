They did so, and they also let the strangers live in the Gaden for as long as they wished. At last the family moved away. Some years later, when the councilman was walking up through the Baumgarten toward the little village one morning to attend divine service, a lovely, snow-white child came toward him by the holly tree, gave him its little hand in a friendly way, thanked him for its baptism, and vanished again. It was that godchild. ‘Praise God!’ he thought to himself, ‘that you performed this service; how glad you are now!’ A few days later he learned that the child had died that morning.
