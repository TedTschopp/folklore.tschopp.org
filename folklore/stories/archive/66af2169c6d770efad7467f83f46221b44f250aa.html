Nevertheless, the wicked band lay in wait for a long time to catch him whenever he went out to preach the Gospel. Thus the saint was once on a missionary journey from Amides (Ems) to Val Tomiliasca (Domleschg) and was riding along the path through the dark Brühl-Wald when he suddenly recognized behind him his long-feared adversaries galloping up on horseback. — He spurred his little horse, and the good animal, considerably smaller than the villains’ steeds, was strengthened as though by an invisible hand and remained ahead of the others for a long time, until at last the pursuers drew very near. — At the place where today, opposite the Rhine from the chapel of St. George near Räzüns, a sheer wall of rock rises toward heaven, St. George’s little animal gathered itself and—with one leap carried its master and itself safely across to the other bank.
