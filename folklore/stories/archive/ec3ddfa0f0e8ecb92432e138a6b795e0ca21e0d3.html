This is Steinach Castle.", "The last lord of Steinach lived alone in his castle as a harsh, unfeeling ruler. His subjects trembled whenever he emerged from his fortress, for without mercy he punished those who displeased him or disobeyed his commands with the utmost severity. His heart closed entirely when a bitter feud broke out between him and the lord of Wartensee. In cold blood, he burned villages and farms and killed his enemy's serfs and servants, together with their wives and children. The lord of Wartensee tried in vain to get at his adversary. By day, the latter was always well armed when he rode to hunt; at night, he raised the drawbridge, slid mighty bolts across the castle gate, and set bloodthirsty dogs to watch behind the walls. At last, the lord of Wartensee won over a maiden who lived with the lord of Steinach. When her lord sat down to dine at the window facing Wartensee, she was to hang out a white cloth. She did so, and at once an arrow flew through the window and pierced the tyrant's back and chest with such force that its point lodged in the table.
