Not everyone sees it, but those who do must step aside to avoid misfortune. This dog makes the following circuit: it comes from the little town of Willisau and runs through the Tälebach ravine as far as the Bauwiler footbridge. Anyone who does not yield to it on the bridge is thrown into the brook. From the Bauwiler footbridge it goes up through the gorge toward Schürhubel; from there into the Kanzelwald and down the Bachtalen; along the Buchwigger; in through the lower gate to the fountains of the Hintergass; out through the upper gate to the brickworks in the Willbergwald; from there down past the Grundmühle to the Hirseren; and from this wood along the road toward Eimatt, onward to Hergiswil and into the Änziwald, where it disappears. It is said to be a former Schultheis who, through his influence, managed to have the extensive forests assigned to the people of Willisau to the detriment of the rural municipality and Hergiswil, and who must wander as punishment. The legend even claims to identify the family to which the Schultheis belonged and which is therefore said to have fallen into decline.", "b) The town beast is a great black dog that allows itself to be seen during the Fronfastennächten. The town beast is especially recognizable by its dreadful, repulsive cry when it sounds through the solemn stillness of night and pierces marrow and bone; it can be compared with no other sound or tone. This creature has wandered for several centuries and is actually Willisau’s old master builder, whose home was the old coppersmith’s shop in Spitalgasse. By contract he was supposed to build the other houses like his own, but built them smaller and worse. He now atones for that.
