As he entered the Kornmatt and jumped over a fence, something crashed close behind his back, just as if a second man had also leaped across it. Kaspar paid little attention, however, and walked fearlessly down through the Kornmatt. At the bottom, he opened the little gate and entered the rough mountain lane. He had taken scarcely a few steps along it when a second man slammed the rickety little gate behind him, exactly as he himself had done. That did strike Kaspar as strange. He peered around and waited, but nothing moved and no one appeared. Yet it was pitch dark; perhaps his pursuer had hidden. Kaspar reached the place where he had to leave the lane and turn toward Holzerberg. He grasped the top fence rail, vaulted the fence, and directed his steps across the meadow.
