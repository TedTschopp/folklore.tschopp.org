On his way home he saw a black man who seemed to be watering the meadow at the stroke of midnight. Our Kiltgänger at once courageously confronted this unusual “G’schaffigen” and managed to drive him as far as the place between Oberflachs and Schinznach called Kaibenrain. There the waterer suddenly vanished, while at that same instant the pursuer received three such hard slaps that he fell over. When he regained consciousness and tried to leave, he found that one side of his body was completely paralyzed. This happened at the place called Kaibenrain or Rossberg, a depression in the meadow; nearby is the Rössligrube.
