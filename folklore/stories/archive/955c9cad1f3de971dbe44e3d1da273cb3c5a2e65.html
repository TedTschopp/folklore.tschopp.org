The herdsman shouted for joy.", "Many years later, a charcoal burner was making charcoal in the forest below. When the weather changed, he heard shouting in the night, then groaning and moaning. He called into the darkness to ask what it meant. Someone approached and told him that he was the Küher and had to carry the “erfällte” cow back up to the Stollen with groans and moans. There, the cow again stepped onto the fir bark, slipped, and fell below. Then he had to shout for joy. Each time he carried the cow upward, he could atone for one Heller of the harm he had caused the poor woman. This had been granted to him as a special mercy because he had been only seventeen years old at the time of the punishable act; otherwise, he would have been lost eternally.\nJ. B.
