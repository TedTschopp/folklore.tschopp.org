The Schwinger now challenged them, and the one able to drink two fingers deeper into the kettle of boiled Anken accepted the challenge. When they went at each other, the man of Grächen seized the Schwinger around the middle and pressed him against his body with such force that the stranger fell flat-compressed and dead to the ground.
