There, a poor girl, a Temperkind, is said to have glimpsed something beautiful while passing just as the sun was about to set golden in grace. Beside several open, heavy chests full of red gold sat a distinguished gentleman dressed in black with a beautiful daughter. The girl said it was impossible for her to describe all the splendor of these treasures and the woman's strange clothing. She wore a marvelous little black hat with beautiful feathers that hung so low over her face that the girl could not see it properly. Splendid black curls played around her cheeks and neck. A high pointed collar bent back from the edge of her bodice and revealed her dazzling white neck and shoulders. A dress of dark satin gleamed over the white underdress. Richly folded white sleeves enveloped her arms, and fine embroidery adorned the skirt. Of the precious jewelry at her neck and breast, the girl would say nothing at all. Twice, the woman beckoned to her with her little hand—and the girl already took several steps toward her, but the woman's beauty and the treasure's wealth had dazzled and intimidated her—so that, dear God, she stood undecided for several minutes—and missed her good fortune. The sun was just setting; then she heard a loud rushing. On both sides of the hill upon which the treasure lay, a great torrent suddenly came down that she could not have leaped across; she therefore had to run back.
