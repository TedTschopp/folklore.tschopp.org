The appearance of these enchantresses was frightful. Long black garments enveloped their powerful bodies. Their withered arms were bare, their faces pale and gloomy and hissed around by venomous vipers. In their left hands they carried golden magic wands. Their chief work was questioning the dead, who willingly presented themselves at the women's conjurations, during which their voices swelled from quiet murmuring to hurricane-like howling. But spirits from distant worlds also appeared at their call and answered their questions. The women bound these spirits by circling their own heads three times with their magic wands and tracing a triple ring around the moon's disk.
