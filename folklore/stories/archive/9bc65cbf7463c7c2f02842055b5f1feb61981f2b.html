At the appointed time, the male and female dancers from the surrounding area were to gather there. As they passed through the forest separating the two Voralpen, they began to sing and whoop. From high in the forest, they were answered in the same tone. They marveled at this greatly, because they could expect no people in this uninhabited region at night.
