He was consequently in a very good mood whenever, during late hours of the evening gathering, he noticed a bright light in his house on the Bort while returning from the village of St. Niklaus, for he then supposed that his daughters had the company he desired. But if everything on the Bort was dark, the judge likewise returned home, usually at a late hour, very sullen and despondent.", "To make his daughters richer and therefore more sought after, he lived very miserly and treated the accused with great severity in order to acquire a respectable fortune. Once, in bad weather, an otherwise blameless woman faced a severe shortage of fodder for her small herd of cattle. Her pleas for assistance were rejected everywhere. In desperation, she therefore took a Vorschos of hay from a neighbor to save her starving cattle from death. Accused before our harsh judge, she was immediately condemned to die, and as blood-money the judge then inherited a beautiful meadow beside his house on the Bort, which had long been his ardent wish.", "But the harsh sentence seemed to have weighed heavily upon the judge in the next world.
