My earliest childhood memory is connected with one located closest to the village of Suhr, for on a walk that took me past it, I was told that a crocodile lived inside and devoured anyone who ventured too close. When I asked how it had come there, I received the following answer: Not long before, all the nearby inhabitants had been startled from their sleep at night by an inexplicable screaming that generally fell silent only toward morning. From then on, however, this nightly bellowing was repeated and quite clearly came only from the swamp mentioned above. People flocked there from every direction when word of it spread through the region, and no one interpreted it in any other way than that a strange monster lay hidden there in the marsh. It was thought that a crocodile had escaped from a menagerie traveling this road from Bern to Zürich. When the noise gradually ceased to be heard, people claimed to know that the animal had once leapt onto land at night and tried in vain to return to its pool. A farmer had come upon it in his field, killed it, and stripped off its armor of scales. Everyone wished to see the skin of this newest dragon, but no one knew where the farmer lived. Finally, it was said that he had gone out into the world and was exhibiting the skin for money.
