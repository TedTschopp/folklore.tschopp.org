He could not explain it and finally persuaded himself that the light (a Dochtlicht, the kind they had then) had gone out by itself. On another night he still lay awake in bed when he heard a noise and clinking, a sliding, as though his boxes and Guttern were being moved and knocked about on the shelves. He thought it was the cat making a mess for him, so he got up, opened the door, and struck about with a stocking to drive the cat away, then went back to bed. But he found all the Trucken and Guttern in order, yet no cat anywhere; then he grew uneasy.
