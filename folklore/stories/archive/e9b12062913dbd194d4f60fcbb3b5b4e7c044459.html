Year in, year out, it is half filled with water. In popular speech, it is called the ‘ughürig Brunnä.’ Far below lie immeasurable treasures of gold and other precious goods, guarded by the envious Satan in the form of an enormous serpent. It rages and spits poison and fire at those who try to raise the treasure and redeem the penitent soul.
