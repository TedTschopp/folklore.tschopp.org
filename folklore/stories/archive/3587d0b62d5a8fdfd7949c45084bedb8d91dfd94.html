The men of Selzach agreed; every throw was to be worth one Maß of wine. As soon as the three strangers had warmed to the game, they seemed to rise to supernatural size and strength. They sent ball after ball away with such force that people in the valley thought they heard thunder rolling. The balls shot far beyond the village playing ground and up the Jura; some came rolling back, while others flew onward over the mountain and through the fir woods into the valley on the far side. Even today, the long, ruler-straight fissures that the skittles balls cut for themselves can be seen along the mountainside from Betlach to Grenchen. The men of Selzach had to pay for the game. Once all the available wine had been won at skittles and drunk, the three long-bearded champion players also retired to rest. No one knows where they came from or where they went.
