It was a bridge built of beams and planks, such as one finds everywhere in Valais, even over deep gorges where it is dreadful to look down. “Dreams are only dreams after all,” he said. Then a man came from the other side and asked whether he had lost something on the bridge. Niggi laughed and said no, but that he had dreamed something quite foolish about this bridge: namely, that he would find his fortune there.
