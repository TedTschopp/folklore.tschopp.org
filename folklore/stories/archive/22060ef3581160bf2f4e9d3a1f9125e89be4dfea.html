Formerly, the storks regularly appeared and nested at Veltheim on Petri Stuhlfeier (February 22). Then it happened that one of them was walking about on the nearby castle meadows at Wildenstein and was shot by the tenant farmer there. The village felt great abhorrence at this outrage, and so the people remembered all the more readily the day and year of the event. Petri Stuhlfeier came again, and a whole summer passed; ten summers passed in this way, and since that day the familiar storks had never again shown themselves there. In the tenth winter, that spiteful castle farmer died, and the old village storks returned the following year. It is said that from then on, every evening the children went to the churchyard to hear the animals pray at night.
