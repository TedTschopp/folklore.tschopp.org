The name suited him especially well for another reason: he lived in constant discord with his wife and always knew how to find new causes for quarreling with her. At last, returning from Baden in an angry mood one day and again being received badly by his wife's sharp tongue, he killed her and hanged himself. As soon as his body was carried out of the house, a great black bird shot out with it and flew toward the nearby ponds between Dätwil and the city of Baden.
