The wealth of the Dialen is explained by the many smelting furnaces and mines in the region, now abandoned but formerly worked for gold and silver. Thus, in the Münsterthal, an Ofenberg is named where dwarfs live, and near the Cierfer-Alp the Dialenlöcher, las foras dal las Dialas, are pointed out.
