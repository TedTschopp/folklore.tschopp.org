This must have been very many years ago, for no family of that name is now found anywhere along the whole lakeshore. He was an industrious, upright man who stood at his forge from morning until night. His hands grew hard, but his heart remained gentle. With a son's love, he supported and cared for his mother, and he never left the house to seek any pleasure for himself that he could not have shared with the old woman.", "But whenever the Fasnacht season approached, Peter regularly grew restless. ‘Why, he sings Hopser and waltzes for hours,’ his mother would say, ‘and his hammer and anvil have to help make his dance music!’ Whenever music was played for dancing in the village, he was indeed the first to arrive, the lightest on his feet, the most courteous to the women dancers, and the most generous to the musicians. On one such Fasnacht occasion, Peter had disguised himself with others of his kind. When they entered the dance hall together, he wore such a countless number of little bells and Schellen on his cap and Wamms that everyone immediately and unanimously gave him the joking name Schellenpeter. This time, the girls would not leave him alone. He had to dance like a whirlwind until, amid the heat of the overcrowded hall and the weight of his costume, he could scarcely breathe. He opened a window and cooled himself in the wintry air that cut sharply inside. After only a few minutes, he felt very unwell, and when his mask was removed, his face was as white as the snow outside the window. Nevertheless, he did not wish to be counted among the weak that day and once more flung himself into the dance with all his strength. He had scarcely leaped twenty paces when dizziness felled him; he was dead on the spot.
