To test the idle question in reality, they closed both doors before going to bed. When the eleventh hour had struck in the valley, a dreadful roaring suddenly arose on the mountain heights as though a thunderstorm with a hot Föhn were breaking loose above. Immediately afterward, a mighty voice called before the gates of the Melkhaus: ‘Open the gates; the Friesen are crossing the mountain!’ The call was repeated a second and third time. But when the servants inside gave no answer, a thunderous crash passed through the air. The hut's roof was torn away so that from inside, the stars could be seen in the sky. Outside sounded heavy, muffled trotting, as if many riders were riding into the Grindelwald valley.
