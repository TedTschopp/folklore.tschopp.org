Nothing now points to it except a cave in which a treasure lies hidden. Crawling through this cave, one reaches a stone staircase leading steeply into the depths. The farther one descends the stairs, the louder a roaring becomes, and a sharp, howling wind blows against the visitor; little stones and hailstones fall in masses upon the treasure seeker. The storm rages ever more fiercely and develops into a veritable hurricane, so that one can scarcely hear one’s own words. At the bottom of the staircase, one encounters a strong iron door that is firmly locked. Furious barking answers any knocking, and a female voice responds to questions posed in the three highest names. A maiden is said to await redemption while sitting upon an iron chest in which the treasure lies hidden.", "Various young men from Flums have already tried to raise the treasure, and they claim to have advanced as far as the iron door. All, however, had to turn back there because of the storm that threatened to suffocate them, including a certain painter named Bless and his brother. They had set out very early one morning with their coats pulled over their heads so that only their eyes remained free. They were permitted to return no person’s greeting and to speak no word to each other along the entire route, but had to pray without interruption. Only in this way can the treasure be raised.\nRob.
