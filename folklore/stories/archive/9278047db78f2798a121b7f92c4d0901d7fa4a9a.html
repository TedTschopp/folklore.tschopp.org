A woman from Einsiedeln who was also employed in the hospital, a Hörige of the abbey, became his wife, and from this marriage the famous Paracelsus was born around 1493. Beside the Teufelsbrücke on the Sihl stood an old farmhouse, only recently rebuilt, and this was where he, a true child of nature, spent his earliest youth and several later years. A painted image representing the famous doctor hung upon it, and his name still lives among the surrounding people as that of a magician. He obtained his education not from books but by traveling throughout Europe. Nevertheless, he wrote many books, or dictated them. His temperament, as he himself said, was not woven subtly by nature, but fashioned in the rough Schweizerlandesart.
