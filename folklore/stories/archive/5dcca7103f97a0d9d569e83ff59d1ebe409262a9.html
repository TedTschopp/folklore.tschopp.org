She stopped, looked over the wall, and called, as custom requires, ‘Is it mowing well?’ Melk replied irritably, ‘Hardly anything to boast about! Today it is as though bewitched. After a while, however, he no longer liked this; it seemed uncanny to him. He threw the root away.
