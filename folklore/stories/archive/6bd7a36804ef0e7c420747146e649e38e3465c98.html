It is said to have been covered by a terrible rockslide when a great peak of the jagged Ralligstöcke broke loose and plunged toward the lake. Great blocks of solid limestone still lie there today. A castle-like building called Ralligschloss, situated a few minutes before the field of rubble, is also said to be a surviving remnant. Not long ago iron tools were found beneath a great piece of rock. The following legend about it is current among the people:", "One evening, as the inhabitants of that city were preparing to retire, a little dwarf—a Bergmännlein—came down from the mountain and requested lodging for the night. The inhospitable people turned it away, and it found a friendly reception nowhere except among the residents of Ralligschloss. The next morning the little dwarf stood on the summit of the pointed cliff, the Gibelfluh, and proclaimed the tidings of disaster over the city in the following words:", "City of Roll, go forth with your people!", "The pointed cliff has split;", "Schlegel und Wegge stand ready,", "Go forth toward the Stampach!", "But no one heeded this warning, and disaster broke in. The greater part of the pointed cliff tore loose and crushed the city together with its inhabitants. No one was saved except the hospitable residents of Ralligschloss, which still stands.
