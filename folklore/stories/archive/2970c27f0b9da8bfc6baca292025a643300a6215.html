Behold! The old and pious ringing for Vespers from the church tower at Naters met his ear. He quickly turned to discover the source of the hated peal. The church tower at Naters, from which the lovely notes escaped, could not elude his eagle eye. His face glowed with anger; he quickly reached for his bow — gunpowder had not yet been invented — loaded a ruinous projectile from his quiver, and shot it down into the valley. Naturally, it was aimed at the bells in the church tower at Naters. — But the shot missed completely; in his hasty rage he had not aimed well. It is also possible that the good Jesuits, who must answer for so many missed shots by the enlightened, dazzled his aiming eye, for he had to shoot over their residence in Brig.
