Without stopping, they pounded away upon the wobbly table. When another round had ended, they stopped and went out in front of the house. At that very moment, a roaring began. Shingles flew from the roofs, and branches were twisted from the trees. The Süidürscht passed overhead. One can imagine what a fright that caused. They ran into the room and scarcely dared move. Only one refused to let fear master him. He remained standing, mocked the Dürscht, and bellowed as loudly as his throat allowed. Afterward, he joined the others in the room, laughed, and sat down at the table. The others sat there pale and haggard.
