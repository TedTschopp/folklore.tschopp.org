I had already erwellt—that is, had already made the cheese and Zieger—when we heard someone approaching our hut from far away. He came up the valley, and we thought it was a chamois hunter who might wish to spend the night with us. When he was already close to our hut, Waldi-Peter went out to see who it was. The supposed hunter walked past the hut without giving any sign that he wished to stop with us. Peter then addressed him: ‘Little hunter, little hunter!’ and I called outside as well: ‘Let him go. If he wants to come in, he will.’ Yet as soon as the wanderer was addressed, he was nowhere to be seen. Peter said the man had been wearing a grayish Tschoope and that he had seen no head upon him.
