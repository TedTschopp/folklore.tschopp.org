The maiden thanked the young man and said, “I am an enchanted girl and would now be redeemed if you could fulfill one more request for me.” “And what would that be?” the fellow asked. “Come with me to the Tobel,” she said. The young man was to allow it to coil around his neck without letting a sigh be heard, no matter how hard the animal squeezed him.
