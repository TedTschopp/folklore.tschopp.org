An old married couple lived there peaceably together. They earned their daily bread from the inn they had established there in that lonely place. Although they lived there in peace and quiet, the rumor spread that these two spouses were “monsters,” sometimes committed dreadful crimes, and killed travelers. Their son, who had been away in foreign lands for several years, heard this as well. He refused to believe it and, to convince himself whether it was really true, returned to his homeland. On the evening when he arrived at his parents’ house, he did not reveal himself to his family; naturally, they did not recognize him. His companion, however, took lodging elsewhere for the night. Sure enough—in the night, while the stranger slept, the woman entered his bedchamber and poured boiling hot rendered fat down his throat. In the morning the companion came into the house and told the two old people whom they had taken in.
