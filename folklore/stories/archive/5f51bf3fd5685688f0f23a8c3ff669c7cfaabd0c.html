In olden times a beautiful city is said to have stood there, to which the eternal or laufund Jud also came. But because the people there now recognized who he was, not a single person wished to shelter him overnight. Because of this mercilessness, the laufund Jud cursed the city together with its people and said: “Now there is still a city here. When I come again, grass will grow here, trees will stand here, and great stones will lie here, and no houses, streets, walls, or towers will be seen anymore. But when I come for the third time, no herb, fir tree, hut, wall, or street will be found anymore, only snow and ice or glacier—and that shall remain lying here as long, as long as I must wander the whole world without peace or rest.” And everything happened to the smallest detail just as the laufund Jud had once prophesied. A glacier three hours in breadth now covers this region where a city once stood. The deathly stillness is interrupted only by the thunder of avalanches, the cracking of glacier crevasses, and the raging and roaring of the Winter-Gugfa.
