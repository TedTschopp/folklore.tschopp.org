G. once went to Oberschan ‘zur Spinni.’ As he returned home through the Sevelen wood early in the morning—it was not yet daylight—he saw a little woman in the beech forest near the path and asked in surprise what she was doing there so early. Without turning around, she answered that she was gathering leaves for the pigs. Only then did he notice that she wore a red stocking on one foot and a black one on the other. It was the well-known Plattenwibli, who died soon afterward. When the coffin was carried away from the house, a girl asked her mother, who had come into the house, whom they were burying. When the mother answered, the girl said, ‘No, the Plattenwibli is sitting in the kitchen on the hearthstone. Just look!’ The mother told the child to be silent and followed the funeral procession.
