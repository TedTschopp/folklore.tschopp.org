She was given to him, and the two became a happy couple who received the mill as their own. Once, as they stood by the stream watching the waterwheel together, three ravens flew over the house. When the widow's two daughters had grown up, a Mahlknecht came to the house and found employment. His face was fine, but his heart was full of malice. The ravens flew over the house again.
