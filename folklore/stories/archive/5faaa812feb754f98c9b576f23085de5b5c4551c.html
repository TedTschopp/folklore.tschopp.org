This brother worked the fields and took no part in politics. He was diligently occupied in the so-called Triesten when the Angelus rang at eleven o’clock and he was about to say his prayer devoutly. He looked toward the monastery church in Brig and, to his great astonishment, saw neither the boarding school nor the monastery, though he did see the church. At first he did not trust his eyes and thought it a temptation of the Evil One, who wished to disturb his prayer. He therefore turned his eyes away and said the prayer, yet could not keep from glancing toward the monastery from time to time. The same apparition remained. After praying the Angelic Salutation, he again took up the spade briskly and continued his work. But the strange vision he had just seen gave him no peace. He looked up again and now, to his utmost terror, saw neither boarding school, monastery, nor church. As often as he looked across from this spot, he saw the same desolate sight. After one or two hours, however, the monastery and church are said to have appeared again. What was laughed at then has become sad reality: the Jesuit boarding school and monastery have disappeared.
