Stoop" ], "english_paragraphs": [ "A Zusenn had taken to Fursch his fiddle, which he passionately loved and played beautifully; there, he liked to sit on the Stollen to play. When the livestock had been driven away in autumn, the Zusenn had to remain behind alone and aussennen. Then, Greller Jöri came to him with the Sufetuse, or Molken-Tanse, to collect the customary alms. During the night, they heard all the familiar melodies being played on a fiddle outside on the Stollen, as though the Zusenn himself were there. Great fear seized them. The invisible fiddler had announced snowfall. In the morning, the entire Alp lay buried in snow.\nJ. B.
