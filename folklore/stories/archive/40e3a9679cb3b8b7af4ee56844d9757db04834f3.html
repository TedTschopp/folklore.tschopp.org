There I found the entire company in the hayloft, gathered around a crackling fire. Above it hung a cauldron fastened to a beam by a chain. The Roma were preparing their meal. One can imagine how frightened I was! Yet after their departure, no trace of the fire remained; indeed, nothing scorched could be seen at all. In other barns the Roma did exactly the same.
