There, in the area where the old Gisli church once stood, I saw a calf wandering loose. I caught it, tied it to my cloth, and pulled it homeward with me. But it soon tore itself free again, and when I tried to tie it securely once more, it grew upward and outward in every direction. It now seemed advisable to leave the bewitched animal alone and make my escape. I had already forgotten this incident when I took the same path again, carrying a Hutte on my back. At the bottom of the forest, a pretty foal ran after me and finally laid its head, sniffing, upon my carrying basket. But scarcely had I turned to look at it when it swelled into a mighty white horse, blew its hot breath upon me, and, when I tried to escape it, I found the road blocked by high walls and ramparts. To my greatest horror, I then saw quite clearly a rider on a white horse come charging down from the steep Fluh along the narrowest forest path. Since then, it has never again occurred to me to take this road in the evening." ], "notes": [ "Swiss term “Hutte”: a large traditional back-basket or carrying frame used for loads; forms and materials vary by region." ] }, "legacy_urls": [] } ---
