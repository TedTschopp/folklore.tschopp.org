Suddenly, a message arrived that a wild horde was gathering at the foot of the Theodul Pass in the Aosta Valley, intending to invade Zermatt and abuse its defenseless inhabitants. Nameless terror was now added to the people's great grief. But our Karl did not lose his head. He quickly assembled the women and strong daughters, had them put on the clothing left behind by the men and brothers, and led them, armed with all manner of implements, up the mountain toward the enemy.
