Thus she often appeared in the form of a bear on the left bank of the Poschiavino. The hunters hurried over to kill the supposed enemy of their herds. Their otherwise reliable guns always failed, however; then the uncanny bear reared up on its hind legs and mocked the inept marksmen with all manner of comical gestures. At last, however, a hunter was found who was reputed to know somewhat more than all other mortal men. With a mysterious air, he placed a few “Brodbrosmen” on the priming pan. — The shot went off, and the bear limped back into the forest, leaving a trail of blood behind it.
