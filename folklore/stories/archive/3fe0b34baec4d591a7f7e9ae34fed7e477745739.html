One evening near Gallows Hill, he found himself amid a swarm of cats that crowded around him insistently and followed him. One of them was especially forward and was constantly under his feet. He struck at them with his stick, but in vain. At last a kick he dealt the brazen animal made them all disappear. He then continued on; he walked and walked and had to walk without rest or respite through the entire night, never knowing where he was. When the prayer bell rang in Attinghausen in the morning, he found himself drenched in sweat, exhausted and worn out, three hours above Attinghausen at the farthest end of the Waldnachter Alp. But he was cured of his sinful visits. He himself told his neighbors, and one of them told me.
