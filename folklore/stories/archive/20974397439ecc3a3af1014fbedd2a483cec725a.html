Among the inhabitants around that mountain massif, perhaps no man remained on people’s lips as often long after his death as this popular prophet. What is called the Bruder Klausen Weissagung is not infrequently attributed to the Rigelitomme. He lived at the mill on the Fontanen and prophesied that after his death eight owners of this estate would come to nothing. Only the ninth would prosper there again. But since then more than eight have fared badly on the property, for nature has arranged the place for ruin. Beside the same mill stood a hollow tree into which Tomme would stand at night and pray. It has now rotted down to a short stump. Tomme did not go to the parish church like other Christians; he had his own form of devotion. When the farmer from Girishof at Menzberg met Tomme on his way to church, Tomme invited the other man to come along to his church. The farmer agreed out of curiosity and was led into a mountain cave, where a magnificent hall enclosed them.
