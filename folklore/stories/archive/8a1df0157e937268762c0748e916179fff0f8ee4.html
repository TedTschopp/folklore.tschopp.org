The people of Wangs placed little value on this pasture, however, because grass grows there only sparsely, and they had never seriously claimed it as their property. Many people claim to have seen the Valeishund at one place or another along this ancient route. Night watchman Alexander Buel of Wangs is said often to have opened for the Valeishund the gate that stood on the Rosen until a few years ago.
