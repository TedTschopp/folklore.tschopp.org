Keller, Lehrer in Unter-Siggingen.) ", "Sage aus Unter-Siggingen" ], "english_paragraphs": [ "Between the villages of Ober-Siggingen and Unter-Siggingen, on the slope of the Siggingerberg, lies the little wood called Lirenhölzli, surrounded on the east and west by vineyards. Each year before every holy season, the crying of a child is heard here in the Liren. This name is given to various fields and is derived from the Latin lirare, denoting the third turning and plowing of the soil. The crying is interpreted as the soul of a child born here to a girl while she was working in the vineyard and murdered immediately after birth. The road connecting the two villages passes this place, and the Kiltgänger returning home from their nighttime visits therefore know many tales of the Lirenkindli. The night watchman too became so frightened by this pitiful crying that he sought help from the priest at Kirchdorf. On the priest’s advice, he has ever since passed the spot with his saber drawn, for bare iron keeps spirits away.", "(F. J.
