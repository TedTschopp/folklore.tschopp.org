On very hot summer days, a great snake appears there with eyes like walnuts and a tinder-red crest; if it is seen, one can be certain that a thunderstorm is very near. It was already there in the days when the Bernese bailiffs still lived at Schenkenberg Castle. It can be redeemed only by driving a nail through its head and stripping off its skin like an eel—by that means and no other. The boys who tell this add that looking at it gives one diseased eyes, after which one must go to the healing baths at Schinznach.\nThis snake was once the daughter of a Junker of Schenkenberg. When her father went to war with the emperor, she buried all the treasures beneath triple walls and vaults deep in the ground of the castle hill, and she died before the knight returned home from the campaign. Since the Schenkenberg line died out, many treasure hunters have sought the riches hidden here. The castle maiden too is said to have appeared to several people and asked them for a kiss.
