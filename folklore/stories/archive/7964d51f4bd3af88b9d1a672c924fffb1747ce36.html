Geninazzi, 22 J. alt, Erstfeld" ], "english_paragraphs": [ "A little shepherd boy was tending sheep on the Blüemlisalp. He sat beside a willow bush. All at once he heard magnificent singing. He listened, looked around, followed the song, and soon found a radiant maiden sitting upon a chest of money with a little frog in her lap. She saw the boy and beckoned kindly for him to approach. He stepped before her, and she said, “Break off a willow branch and strike the frog in my lap three times!” The boy did not dare and ran away to his sheep. That evening he told his father everything. His father encouraged him to fulfill the maiden’s wish the next time. Surely no harm would come to him. The following day, the lovely singing sounded anew, and again the maiden beckoned to the boy and asked him to give the animal in her lap three blows with a little willow switch. He obeyed, broke off a branch, and drew it back, but he did not dare to strike. That evening he told his father what had happened to him. His father went with him to a monastery and had him blessed. Both his father and the monastery’s abbot encouraged the boy, whom the abbot blessed, to act according to the maiden’s wish; he had nothing to fear. The next time he struck the animal twice, but when the frog suddenly swelled up and spat fire, he threw the switch away in fright and ran off. The maiden wept and cried exceedingly loudly and heartrendingly, “Lost for 101 years!” and vanished. The 101 years are now drawing to their end, and soon the maiden will appear again. That is how I heard soldiers from this region tell it while I was in the military.", "N.
