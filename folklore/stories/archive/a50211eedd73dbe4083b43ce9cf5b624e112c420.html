Aarburg.)" ], "english_paragraphs": [ "The Bornhund, a black dog with a collar, is named Bruno. Whenever the weather changes, he descends from the upper rocks of the Bornberg to the Aare. Some claim that he has wings and flies from the Bornberg on the left bank of the Aare across to the two Wartburg castles on the right. He is regarded as a weather prophet and a messenger of death. One part of the Aarburg suburb lying at the foot of the fortress is called the Säge after a sawmill there. Here, too, a black poodle called the Sägehund runs about at night. On New Year’s night, however, he extends his wanderings farther and, with a Namensbüchlein—an old ABC primer—in his mouth, visits all the districts around the little town. He is said to be the spirit of a man who died many years ago while serving as overseer of the local cotton-spinning mills and had the reputation of a dishonest administrator.
