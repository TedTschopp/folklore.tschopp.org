As early as ten years ago, an Emd man saw the Bozo clearly there beneath the chapel in the morning, while it was still dark. Since then, either in the darkness of morning or late in the evening, it has more or less continually molested and plagued people there. Once several people were walking up there late. At first they saw it like a tangle of black wool, like balls rolling over one another. One of them, the boldest, wanted to go on ahead; when it came close to him, it looked like a small lamb speckled black and white. But he did not get away unscathed, for he soon became deathly ill and afterward suffered a full-blown sickness. Some have seen it there as a black cat, others as a human being. Several times already it has led people astray. One man fell so far down that he injured himself badly, and a doctor had to be summoned for him. The parish priest of the time also nearly fell down there and lost his breviary, which they did not find until the next day. Certain facetious people accused him of having been drunk and said the Bozo was not at fault at all—but God preserve us from imputing such a thing to so thoroughly upright a gentleman. This parish priest is said afterward to have declared: “I will not dare pass there at night again. That time it very nearly made a fool of me.
