It therefore granted him the Ruttenwald to clear. There he could gradually have established a home for himself. Instead, he begrudged everyone below in the valley possession of their green meadows. One day, when he was once again fretting over his work on the heights, a little man suddenly stood beside him and led him to a high rock wall. With astonishment, the envious man saw within it a great gate from which bright light shone. The little dwarf said: «This door, which opens only once every hundred years, leads to all the treasures of the world. Go inside and choose, but choose wisely and do not miss the moment, or you must remain within. In one hour, the door closes again for an entire century.» There the idler stood and stared at the immeasurable treasures in the earth’s womb. He wished to seize everything and carry everything away. Now he picked up a precious stone, now a chain or golden crown, then an unshaped lump of gold, only to set everything down indecisively and search for something still more valuable. In his heart he thought only of how he might outdo his neighbors. Thus he forgot the brevity of his allotted hour. It ended before he could reach a decision.
