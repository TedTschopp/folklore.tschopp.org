No one passes through this Tobel at night unless absolutely necessary.—Once a young man had to go by night from Schmitten to the baths to fetch the doctor. In the Tobel he heard magnificent music and listened to it for a long time, but soon perceived with horror that a great host of dark figures was approaching, led by a musician.—He tried to hide, but one member of the procession pulled him from his hiding place, and he had to go with them. Downhill, uphill, back and forth they went throughout the night until toward morning, when they halted. The young man had to step into the middle, and all the others danced around him; he suffered not the slightest harm. When the morning bell sounded, the nocturnal company suddenly scattered, and with difficulty the young man found his way through the unfamiliar undergrowth.—He reached the doctor, presented his request, and related his experiences during the night.—The doctor went with him to Schmitten, where the young man's suffering mother had passed the crisis and was on the road to recovery.
