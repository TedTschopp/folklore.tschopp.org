But he had not remembered the saying correctly and ventured, “Oben us und überall a!—Out above and strike everywhere!” It worked. He was immediately carried up the chimney but beaten so miserably back and forth there that at last he fell down badly battered. Yet he immediately mounted the hobbyhorse again.
