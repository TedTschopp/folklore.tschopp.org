While holding this post, he grew so visibly rich that people believed honest means could not be involved. The village watchman also claimed to have seen him repeatedly at night transferring whole Tausen (half-buckets) of wine from the Zehntenkeller to his own cellar. Since the watchman did not wish to risk anything against him alone, he arranged with several strong men to lie in wait together for the community thief. Thus they positioned themselves at night behind the house he had to pass. It did not take long before he duly appeared. The men immediately stepped forward to show him that he had now been discovered. But he quickly smelled trouble and, as if nothing had happened, walked with his full Tause on his back toward his nearby orchard. There he emptied the wine beside a tree as though he had merely been carrying Gülle and wished to fertilize it there. Then he walked calmly toward his house without looking around again. Our farmers, however, were not fools either. Instead of following the cooper, they ran straight to that tree, pulled off their shoes, and used them to scoop up the spilled liquid. They had guessed entirely correctly: it was excellent old wine. But what could be proved? Thus nothing further happened to the Wildi-Küfer, and he only grew richer. Yet when he died, his entire body turned black, and four weeks after his death he was already seen running through the village and heard making a commotion in the house. My cousin also has a story to tell of this, from the time when she once worked for his family on Taglohn. She cannot be persuaded to return. At that time, the old cooper’s wife had gone to the baths at Schinznach to enjoy herself like rich people, and no one remained in the house except her daughter. My cousin was to sleep there with her for the duration. This went perfectly well until midnight. Then she was suddenly awakened by a tremendous din. At first she thought the servant was dragging the heavy draft chains across the wooden floor. But when the cooper’s daughter also awoke and told my cousin in a hushed voice, “Pst! Just be quiet; he will do nothing to you,” she suddenly remembered the haunting cooper. Only then did she become terribly afraid.
