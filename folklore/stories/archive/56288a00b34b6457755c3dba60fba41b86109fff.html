Every morning the wild goatherd came close to the houses to collect the animals, and every evening he led them back to the same spot and then returned to the forest. The young men of Conters often tried in vain to catch him. At last they devised a peculiar plan and filled two fountain troughs from which he was accustomed to drink, one with wine and the other with brandy. The goatherd first tasted the red drink—the wine—and cried, “Little Red, you will not lead me astray,” then refreshed himself with the white drink—the brandy. In the intoxication that followed, he was bound. His tormentors knew an old legend that the Fänggen could make gold or the elixir of life from whey after its whey-cheese had been removed, and they would not release him until he revealed an arcanum to them.
