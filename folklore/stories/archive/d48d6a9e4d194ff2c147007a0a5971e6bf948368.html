The little valley is called Walebach after the flowing water that accompanies it. Following the stream upward, one reaches the Grauflue, in which a Balm has hollowed itself out where ‘an entire Senten would have room.’ The floor of this Balm—fresh sand crumbles readily from the loose sandstone—is always as if swept. No impurity is tolerated there; invisible spirit hands maintain order. In the Lutherthal, the people know quite definitely which of those gentlemen in the city who did not mean well by the common people had to walk after death and were afterward banished to the Graufluh and tied to a rope. One such spirit was once carried there by the conjurer in a Kratten. Along the way, someone wished to see the spirit and therefore had to look over the carrier's left shoulder, whereupon on that occasion he truly acquired the power to see spirits. When bold Milzjoggi once went into the cave and challenged the spirits, such a strange rushing began that he had no desire to wait for what came next.
