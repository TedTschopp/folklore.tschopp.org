As the hunter followed the chamois, such a little man suddenly stood before him and earnestly begged him not to shoot its animals; in return, it would show him a great treasure. It pointed out the treasure in the nearby rock, the Giswilerstock. He need only go and take it, on the condition that upon entering the rock he must on no account look back, or the treasure would remain beyond his reach. Full of greed, the hunter felt his way into the opening in the rock. Someone cried out joyfully behind him; without thinking he looked back, and at once everything vanished with a thunderous crash.
