He went closer and came to the old castle, which was illuminated all around. There he saw three knights using golden balls to knock down golden bowling pins on the grass, making a sound that rang far and wide. The farmer watched the game for a while, and the curious, profit-seeking man felt the desire to try it himself while the knights rested a little. He boldly crept nearer, and because none of the knights moved, he seized a gleaming ball and threw it so that all the pins fell upon the grass. Scarcely had this happened when a great noise arose, and the ball and pins sank into the earth together with all three knights.
