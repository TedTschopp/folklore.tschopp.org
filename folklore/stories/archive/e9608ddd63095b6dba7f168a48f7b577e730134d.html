Concerned for her welfare, he hurried to a cave in the nearby forest where a Holzmütterchen famous as a midwife lived and asked it to come personally to help his wife. It came, and so as also to appear as a rescuing angel to the starving family—for it was just then a time of scarcity—it gave the man an old Fürtuch full of gold coins to carry. Believing them to be pieces of tile, he let several fall here and there so that he would not carry such a heavy load for nothing. The little mother, noticing this, said to him, ‘Je mehr du zatterst, desto minder hast du’—the more you scatter, the less you have. He then stopped zattern, and behold, when he emptied his Fürtuch at home, it contained nothing but gold coins.
