Over their meal they spoke of their beloved Switzerland and the friendly peninsula town where they had been born, and they expressed the heartfelt wish to be there and to avoid the months-long journey so that they could greet their old parents sooner. Their hostess thought that something could be done and enchanted the three young men into sleep. When they awoke, day was just about to dawn, and they heard a little bell ringing. “Why,” one cried in astonishment, “if it were not so many miles away, I would wager that this was the Kapuzinerglöckchen in Rapperswil ringing for Morgenmette!” Scarcely had he said it when they discovered that they were in an open field near Hürden, a little village in the canton of Schwyz, and saw Rapperswil across the lake. Astonished, they crossed themselves, thanked God, returned to their families, and then erected three wooden crosses at the place where they had awakened; the crosses still stand today.\nH. They did not awaken even when the Mother of God, hearing their fervent prayer, carried all three in swift flight toward Hürden, where they found themselves at sunrise. Naturally, they could scarcely trust their eyes.
