On Anton vom Turm's command, the baron's servants burst in, seized the bishop, and hurled him and his companion out of the window into the abyss. The unfortunate bishop struck a sloping rock, tumbled farther down into a level meadow, and lay there. People working in the fields found him still alive. They therefore lifted him and intended to carry him to Chandolin. At the end of the meadow, they set him down again so that they could wash his bloodied head at a little spring. Here the bishop died. In memory of his death, everyone who drinks from this little spring now plants a small wooden cross in the ground, and many hundreds always stand there.
