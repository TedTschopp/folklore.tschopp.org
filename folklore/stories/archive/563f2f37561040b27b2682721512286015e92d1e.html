Many, many years ago, a chapel of St. Catherine is said to have stood there. A second St. Kathrinenbrünneli flows from the Fluh near the Hundsbühl or Husbühl quarter, where a little garden now nestles upon the shore and the eye looks freely across the lake toward the Rütli meadow opposite. Anyone suffering from intermittent fever drank this water and became well. At this place too, legend has dedicated a little sanctuary to St. Catherine, that favorite saint of the people. Indeed, folk legend is devout and generous: it builds houses of God in great numbers, and no one can erect them so easily or cheaply. Wherever it miraculously possesses a lovely chapel, it also hangs up a Glöcklein, likewise miraculous and bright in tone, a little bell able to ring by itself. Legend truly bestowed such a bell upon the little St. Catherine's chapel on the shore at Brunnen.
