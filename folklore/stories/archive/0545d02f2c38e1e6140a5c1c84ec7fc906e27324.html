Zurfluh-Jauch, 49 J. alt" ], "english_paragraphs": [ "Well, I certainly do not believe that absolutely none of it is real. A man from Gurtnellen once told me something that surely was supernatural. He said that he and another boy had once been out with the goats. Then, all at once, they noticed something in a thicket. It kept wriggling and rustling about. They picked up stones and began throwing them at it. It came out of the brush toward them, and they kept pelting it with stones as hard as they possibly could. If it had been a natural human being, he said, they would have struck it ten times over. At last one of them said he did not like this, and they ran away. That evening both had swollen heads and had to go to bed. It nearly drove them to death! It came within a hair of finishing them off! “Yes, and what did the one that came out of the brush look like?” He could not say much about that, except that it did not even have a head and was half white and half black. Later they told the story to the priest, or to a Capuchin—at any rate, to a clergyman—and he said it had been a poor soul. They should merely have addressed it in the three highest Names; then they could have redeemed it. It had very little suffering left. — N. N. told me this.
