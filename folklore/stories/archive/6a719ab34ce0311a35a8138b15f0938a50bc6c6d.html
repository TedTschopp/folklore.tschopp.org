He sat persistently at a spot where no carriage could swerve quickly without risking a plunge into the depths and where no pedestrian could spring away so nimbly that the lurker could not immediately cut him off again. Like a beggar he began to fiddle a Schlemperlied and treacherously awaited the traveler’s charity; but as soon as the traveler made the first motion to offer him alms, he had already been stabbed with a knife.
