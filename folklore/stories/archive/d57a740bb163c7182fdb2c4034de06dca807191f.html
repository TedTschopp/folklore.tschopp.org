People very much wished to know what the unfathomed depths concealed. A man condemned to death was therefore offered freedom from punishment if he would descend and bring back a report from the mysterious interior. The criminal undertook the venture. He slid far down on a rope until at last a great, broad field lay before him. For a long time he wandered across the meadow without finding a dwelling or food, so that he ate his shoes completely down to the soles. At last he spotted a house and entered it. Look—three sleeping men sat at a table. One of them raised his head and asked the newcomer, ‘What year is now counted?’ The man told him. The underground figure then led the offender to a window and said, ‘Do you see those people there?’ He answered yes. He saw a great host of soldiers, all lying quietly on their stomachs on the ground with arms crossed beneath their foreheads. They wore Unterwaldner Landestracht and colors. Now the underground man put a fateful question to the visitor from the upper world; it was simply, ‘Do you know any of these warriors?’—‘No!’ came the answer, whereupon the other replied: ‘Then we shall have to remain here until 1800 und nu meh, nu meh!
