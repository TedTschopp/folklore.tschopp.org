One man threw around himself a fresh cowhide with the horns still attached and waited outside beneath the uppermost storm-beaten fir to confront the approaching man. After a long time, Brunnenhans finally entered the hut, set down his Tragref, threw his bloody mountain staff into the corner, and sat by the fire to dry his clothing.
