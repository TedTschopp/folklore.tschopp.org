There are certain persons, mostly female, also called Temper-(Quatember-) Kinder, who possess a special gift for seeing such processions. These often consist of long ranks of unknown dead, joined by the figures of some familiar people who are deceased or still alive and who, it is suspected, will be the first to cross into the spirit realm. What astonishes us is that this vision of the dead is not infrequently fulfilled. Living people are often drawn in as well and must walk along. This happened to a man in Sion when, late one evening, he crossed the churchyard. Just as he meant to pass the great church door, a long procession of veiled worshipers wrapped in white garments emerged and made its way toward St. Theodul’s Church. All carried burning candles in their hands, and it was as if a mighty wind were rushing through the treetops. When the last person stepped out of the church, he offered the silent observer a burning candle too. As the man grasped it, a cold shudder ran through his limbs. He wanted to flee, yet something drew him on to join the mysterious procession at this unusual hour. He took courage and went with it. Just then the clock struck twelve, and the muffled strokes mingled strangely with the worshipers’ incomprehensible chorus. The whole of St. Theodul’s Church stood in brilliant flames and seemed densely filled. Our narrator alone was among strangers there, although several figures looked entirely familiar to him. An hour passed—he did not know how long or short it was, so thoroughly had the strange spectacle confused his senses. And at the stroke of one—whisk!—all the candles went out.
