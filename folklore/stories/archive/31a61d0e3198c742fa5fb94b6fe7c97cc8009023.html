When he was in danger of being discovered, he fled to Italy, where robbers attacked him in the open countryside. He fell into such mortal danger that he vowed to atone for his offenses in the strictest religious order if he remained alive. And behold! Very close by arose the bright clatter of many horseshoes, as though a troop of mounted Landjäger were trotting toward them. Terrified, the robbers fled in all haste and let their victim go. He now found himself rescued and utterly alone in the open field, without perceiving the slightest further trace of cavalry or any kind of human aid. Before long, however, he regretted having made so burdensome a vow and therefore traveled to Rome to be released from it. The pope refused to grant a dispensation because the rescue from Above was too plainly evident.
