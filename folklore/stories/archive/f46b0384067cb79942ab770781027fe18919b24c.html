It extends an hour and a half into the mountain, and the passages within are wide enough for several men to walk upright side by side. At the front, the Wildmännchen have hewn out tables and benches. A quarter-hour farther inside lies a small lake encircled by blocks of rock they rolled into place as cool seats. Still farther toward the back come their dwellings. At the end, she received an entire apronful of clods of earth.
