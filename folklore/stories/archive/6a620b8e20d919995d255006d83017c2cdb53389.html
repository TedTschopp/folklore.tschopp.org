Formerly used as the Oberstafel of the Felli Alp and stocked with cattle, today it is only mown or grazed by goats and sheep. Once, several Walker siblings from Ried were there cutting Wildheu when they saw Meieri-Franz, their farmhand, coming up through the valley and approaching them. He passed out of their sight behind several rocky projections and, contrary to every expectation, did not reappear. The following day, one of the siblings went down to the mountain holding and confronted Franz there. He denied it with the utmost firmness. Hardly eight days had passed when he fell victim to fatal pneumonia.", "Jos.
