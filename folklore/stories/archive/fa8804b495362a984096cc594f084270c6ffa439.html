In court, he appeared as a false witness. The court was held on the disputed common land itself. Before riding onto the disputed ground, he put earth from Zug territory into his shoes, placed a comb and a spoon beneath his hat upon his head, and at the decisive moment swore: ‘As surely as I have the Creator and the Judge above me, so surely do I stand upon the ground and soil of Zug.’ As a result of this false oath, judgment went against the people of Walchwil.", "Soon after that day—perhaps already on the way home—he met with disaster at the boundary between the two communities; people said the devil had taken him. A man named Krummenacher from the Entlebuch then confined him in the Flue, where three holes can still be seen today. The kobold had to remain here as long as no one called him out. If he was called wantonly, he emerged but had power only on the Allmend, not on private land.
