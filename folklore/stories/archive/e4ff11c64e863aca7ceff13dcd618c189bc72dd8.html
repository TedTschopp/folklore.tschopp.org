A warm breath of the Föhn chittelet—gently shook—the Droslen scrub on the Planggen and the old windows of the mountain cottages on the lower mountain farms, drying the well-ripened fodder on the warm, dry ground within hours. That same evening, while the sun was still setting, they were able to stack it. Our Regli was the first to carry a substantial load onto the Tristbett prepared for it.
