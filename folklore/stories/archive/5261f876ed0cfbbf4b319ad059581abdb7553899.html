The man of Weissenfels fell into deepest grief. He bade father and daughter farewell by letter and departed that very night. The young lady’s tears had not escaped Brandis’s notice, however. He grew jealous, rode after the departing man, overpowered him, and brought him wounded to his castle to die in the dungeon.
