Formerly one of the most fertile Alps, the Blüemlisalp, lay here, and not very long ago a pass led from here, though not without danger, to Valais on the other side. The people of this part of the country would have had a happy lot had they not at that time labored under the arbitrary rule of cruel lords. No one’s property was safe, and even the valley’s women and maidens did not escape these monsters’ persecutions. Their godless deeds could not go unpunished, however. Heaven’s wrath awoke, and once, when one of them—the most wicked of all those under whom the land had languished—pursued a young herder girl with his savage lust, a large black billy goat never before seen on the Alp suddenly came leaping to the fleeing maiden’s aid and hurled her pursuer from the steep rock wall into the abyss with a powerful blow. At the same time, the Firnen of the icy mountains all around began to tremble. Amid tumbling pieces of rock and masses of ice, the once-blooming and fertile valley was transformed into the desolate glacier waste it is today. From that moment, rarely entered by human beings, it became the abode of all those villains who once ruled here and abused their power to oppress their neighbors and satisfy their abhorrent passions. Condemned to eternal penance, they now often travel from here through the country, lamenting their fate in dull, peculiar tones.
