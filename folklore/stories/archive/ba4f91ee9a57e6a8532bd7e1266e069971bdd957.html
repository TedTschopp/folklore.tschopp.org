According to legend, a giant of tremendous strength and size once lived among them. Because he had committed incest with his sister, he was summoned before the Landvogt at Sargans Castle. In anger, he is said to have torn up a fir tree by the roots, knocked off the branches with his hand, and used the trunk as a walking stick. At the castle entrance, he lifted the iron gate from its hinges as though it were made of paper and twisted one of the hinge pins upon which it hung as though it were wax. Indeed, that pin still points downward today. On the return journey, the giant is said to have driven a crowbar into a millstone at Mels, lifted the stone onto his back, and carried it to Gunis, five minutes above the village of Vättis.
