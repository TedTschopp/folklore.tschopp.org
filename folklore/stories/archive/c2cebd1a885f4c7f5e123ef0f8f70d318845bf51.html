It was dismantled and hauled down into the valley to be rebuilt here. But near the present building site, the transport suddenly came to a stop in a meadow, and the harnessed animals would pull no farther. The farmer then noticed that an unknown man had meanwhile seated himself at the rear upon a projecting board of the load. So as not to ruin his building project through thoughtless speech, he silently pushed that board off the wagon, and at once the load began moving again. But from this spot in the meadow where the board was thrown off, the Wild Huntsman now begins his procession up to the Homberg, where the Mutt-Schüerli formerly stood.
