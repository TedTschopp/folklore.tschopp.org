After some time the daughter asked, ‘What shall we do this evening?’ The widow said that she intended to kill the neighbor's child—the child of the very father who was now with her. The daughter also said something, but I do not know what. The two women smeared their sticks with something from a little pot, sat upon them, and said, ‘Üff und drüß und fort!’ And away they went, out through the chimney. The father was terrified and lamented fearfully over his child. But his unmarried companion thought that the father should imitate the two Wybervölcher. The father followed his advice, smeared his stick with ointment from the little pot, and said, ‘Up and out and away, but much faster!’ One, two, and he was gone and home. ‘Quick, bring me my saber!’ he called to his wife, who fetched it after some protest. Soon his own cat came to the window; he opened it and struck off one Taapen with the saber. The next morning it was the hand of the neighbor woman, with its gold finger ring.
