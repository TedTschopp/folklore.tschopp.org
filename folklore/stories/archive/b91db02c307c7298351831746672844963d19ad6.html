It is maintained by a maid who lives in an outbuilding; all the shutters are and remain closed. Nevertheless, from time immemorial, the same man has looked down from a certain window whenever the weather is about to change. He wears a gray hat on his head. This is the Günnä, of whom nothing else is known except that he was an immigrant who bought property here and is said to have committed many injustices. Every morning, the maid, whom he never encounters, must make his bed afresh; in return, each time she finds three Batzen lying beside it on the little bedside table.
