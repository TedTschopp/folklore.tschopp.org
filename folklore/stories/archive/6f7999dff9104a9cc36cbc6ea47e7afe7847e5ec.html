Thirty years later, after a great flood, a human skull washed white was found in the village stream. People suspected an unatoned murder, and at a municipal assembly it was proposed that everyone present should attest his innocence by laying his right hand on the skull. All did so except an old man, who hesitated. When he was finally compelled to do it, three drops of blood appeared on the skull. The murderer confessed his guilt and was executed.\nJ. B. Stoop", "***", "This legend still embodies the ancient Bahrprobe, or Bahrrecht. Before the public people’s court, the murdered person was laid upon a bier, and those under suspicion had to step forward and touch the body and its wounds with their hands. Certain formulas were spoken as they did so. God was then expected to give a sign: the wounds should begin to tremble or even bleed, and the dead person’s facial color should change.
