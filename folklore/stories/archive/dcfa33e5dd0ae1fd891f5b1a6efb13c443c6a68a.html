At last, no one wished to work the Alp any longer, and it lay deserted. Then, a man came to the owner and said: ‘I shall go onto the Alp; I am not afraid.’ The owner replied: ‘If you can live there, I shall give you the Alp.’ The other man moved up. The spirit soon came in black form and opened the Gatter for him so the livestock could enter. From then on, the spirit was always with him in the stable, during milking, in the barn, on the Alp, at the table without eating with him, and even in bed without touching him and without speaking a word. At first, the Senn did feel dread. But little by little, his fear passed, and he grew accustomed to the spirit. At the same time, he noticed that the black specter gradually began to ‘weissen’—turn white—first at the head and then farther and farther down until, at last, it was entirely white. Then, it collapsed and became ashes. The man struck the ashes, and soon, a white dove flew up. Later, the spirit came once more and thanked the man for finally redeeming it after his predecessors had been unable to do so and had quickly died of fear and terror before it.
