To wonderfully beautiful music, wild figures whirled across the grass in full dance, and at a long, richly laden table he saw a row of pale-faced figures dressed in black sitting and repeatedly inviting him to eat. ‘Before eating,’ he thought, ‘one must pray,’ and he did so.
