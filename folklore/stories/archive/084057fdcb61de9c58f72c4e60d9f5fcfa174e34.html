At last the stranger manages to persuade Johannes that it is improper for him to be beside a princess and that he must not tolerate even the mere appearance of doing evil. Matters went so far that in the end Johannes no longer recoiled from a plan to murder her. During a walk, he cut deeply into the princess’s throat and then buried her. When the forest hermit returned to his cell, he no longer found the other man there and soon realized who his companion had been. Full of remorse, Guarin went to the Pope and confessed his crime.
