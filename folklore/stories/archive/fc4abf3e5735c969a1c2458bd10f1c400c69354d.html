It came every night and pressed him. He did not see it arrive, but he saw it leave in the shape of a cat. When it lay upon him, he could not move or call out, although he was awake. At last he meant to put an end to it. He put on a scapular and took a Gertel—a billhook—to bed with him. When the cat came pattering up over the bedcover at midnight, he swung the Gertel and, in the drei höchsten Namen, the three highest names, cut the fleeing cat’s tail off. It uttered a terrible scream. He now tried to find the tail but could not find it at any price; only traces of blood were there. The following morning his wife had her head bound with a rag.
