Nevertheless, they visibly lost weight and wasted away like goats. One day their master therefore asked what was wrong that they were growing so thin; if they lacked anything, they need only say so. They now told him, ‘Every night a large black cat comes into our bedchamber, jumps onto the bed, sits upon our hearts, and torments and presses us terribly. We suspect it is your own wife, for once, when she probably thought us asleep, she entered the chamber in her true form and left again when she noticed that we were awake. But this evening we will keep a gun ready, loaded with blessed ammunition, and shoot at the cat.’ The master was frightened, for he too did not quite trust his wife and suspected her of witchcraft. But what could he do to avert the impending misfortune? The farmhands gave him good advice: ‘At night, soon after 11 o’clock, pay attention! If you see that your wife is sleeping without breathing, turn her over and lay her on her face.’ The master followed this advice, and in the morning his wife lay dead in bed upon her face, just as he had placed her.
