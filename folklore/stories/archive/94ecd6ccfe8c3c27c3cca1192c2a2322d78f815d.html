Gräggi means the screamer. At night, it sometimes lies in the road as a tree trunk, a dog, a pig, or a calf, or screams down at a person from a tree. At other times, it rolls toward one as a sack of leaves, cries in the most varied voices, and sweeps over vineyards and forests with a noise like the Wild Hunt elsewhere. One would think that not a single Rebenstickel or tree could remain unbroken, although in the morning no trace of any of it can be seen.", "It was known of one family, consisting mostly of Fronfastenkindern, that they were on friendly terms with the “Gräggi.” At night, while the sons slept, it watched their horses, and when the animals had grazed one circle bare, it drove the stake—the “Stumpen”—farther on.
