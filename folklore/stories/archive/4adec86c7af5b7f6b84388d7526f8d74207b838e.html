When the little boy tore the cloth from around his eyes, he still could see nothing, for it was a pitch-black night. He began to howl as if a knife were stuck in him. But the bear could speak, and it called, ‘Give me bread and milk for the little boy, or I shall eat you!’ That frightened her terribly. Without delay, she lowered the food to him over the gallery in a Chrättli at the end of a long pole, sighing in bitter earnest as she did so.
