Natsch" ], "english_paragraphs": [ "Two girls who had a reputation for witchcraft lived on the Murgerberg. Nevertheless, a young lad often visited them, though he was never welcome. They always played their mischievous games with him and made a fool of him. Once, as they looked out of the window and saw him coming again, one of them said crossly: \"If only that tiresome, stupid boy would turn into a donkey at once!\" And indeed, the wicked wish had been fulfilled to the letter, and the faithful suitor was transformed into a gray, long-eared donkey. As may readily be imagined, he was dreadfully frightened by his misfortune and scarcely knew what to do. He could not return to his father's house in this form.", "At last, he resolved to trot down to the mill by the lake and await his further fate there. The miller found him there and took him in because no one else wanted him. The bewitched boy then patiently and honestly did his duty as a mill donkey; only, he often slipped out of the stable or left the pasture at night and went up the mountain to the house of the sorceresses, hoping that they might finally release him from his sorrowful condition after all.", "There, he once heard one of them say: \"One can almost feel sorry for our former suitor. On the feast day in question, he managed to get into the church after the service despite every effort by the churchgoers standing around to fend him off. As soon as he had eaten the foliage there, he regained his human form.", "Because no one happened to have witnessed this last event, he wisely kept his experiences secret until shortly before his death.
