Senn, Chronik.", "***", "The legend occurs in many forms throughout the Alpine region. It illustrates the folk belief that no sin is punished so harshly as a child’s ingratitude toward his mother. Whoever strikes his mother will even have the offending hand grow out of his grave one day. The wickedness of the Senn on the Sardona-Alp is intensified by the fact that through his gift he also treats milk and butter with contempt. Buried Alps are found in great number. The deterioration of the high-mountain climate through thoughtless logging may explain some of them; rockfalls and avalanches also faithfully contribute.
