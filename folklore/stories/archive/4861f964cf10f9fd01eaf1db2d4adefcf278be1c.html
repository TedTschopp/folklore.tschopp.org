As recently as fifty years ago, a maiden with a fine and delicate face, dressed in white, was often seen strolling beside this spring in bright sunshine. After doing so for a while, she sat beside the water, loosened and combed her hair, which fell in many curls over her shoulders and neck and shone in the sun like the purest gold. She often sat this way for hours as though longingly awaiting someone. But as soon as the sun inclined toward its setting, she rose and, sadly braiding her golden hair again, returned to Bischofsstein Castle, from which she had come. This maiden is said to have awaited redemption.
