When they reached the path by the new drainage ditches, they came upon a fairly well-made bed spread out at the forest's edge only a short distance from the trail. Out of curiosity and high spirits, they uncovered it and found a bottle inside. They immediately took it home and, like a bottle of vinegar, set it upon the stove for the time being. But curiosity gave the others no peace; they wished to know what was in the bottle. So they took it down again and pulled out the stopper. With a great crash, a little man sprang from it. Eyewitnesses now relate that this was the spirit of that legal agent from Brugg who was once found dead at home. So that he would not be heard haunting the house forever, his relatives had arranged to have him gebannt—bound by magic—inside that bottle and carried up to the Habsburg.
