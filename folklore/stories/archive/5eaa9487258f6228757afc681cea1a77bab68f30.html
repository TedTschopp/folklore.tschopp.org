At first he did not dare agree. But the Bergmännchen came several times and pressed him more and more, until at last he thought that if he did not oblige it he would have all the less good fortune with his cattle afterward, and that in the end he could bear the loss even if he never saw his little cow again. He resolved to select the poorest animal among his cows and entrust it to the Männchen, and dismissed it with the promise that he would give it one when the herd departed. At the Kühscheid, the Männchen came and timidly took charge of the little cow that the man handed to it on a rope.
