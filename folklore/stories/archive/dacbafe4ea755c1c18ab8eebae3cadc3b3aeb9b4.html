When conducting his Besegnungs- and magical business, he always locked himself inside his chamber. His servant, who had long grown curious, made a borehole through the room's wooden wall and watched from outside as the doctor seized a white-headed snake with his hand and began boiling it in a water cauldron. Soon, white foam rose at the rim and gathered into a snow-like mass. Meanwhile, the doctor seemed still to lack a vessel or some other boiling implement, for he suddenly went next door into the kitchen without locking the door. The servant used this moment and slipped into the mysterious chamber. There, he wiped the white foam, which he took for boiling milk, from the rim with his finger, hastily licked it down, and ran outside into the Matte as though nothing had happened, in order to mow there.
