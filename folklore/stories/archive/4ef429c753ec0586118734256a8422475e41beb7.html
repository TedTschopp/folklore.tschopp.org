It is said that 20 years ago the Älpler found implements belonging to those original inhabitants, including trivets, cooking spoons, ladles, arrows, and the like. Even now, one can see at the findspot a block of rock or hill of strangely hard stone called the Heidenkilchli. The heathens are said to have held their worship here. The rock, as I saw it, stands completely isolated on a beautiful little pasture after one has climbed a steep bank. It contains strange Dusen, or incisions, some shaped exactly like deeply impressed footprints. Several are as much as an inch deep; others form actual hollows roughly two to three inches deep and of such length and width that a well-grown man can lie in them.
