The man hid himself and watched their activity for a long time. He now heard the most beautiful music he had ever heard in his life, and immediately the figures began to dance; then the music fell silent, and the company began such a commotion that his hearing and sight failed him. As he lay there, one of the nocturnal spirits leaping about discovered him, seized him rather roughly by the collar, and directed him onto the road home. — Although scarcely in command of his senses, he was still able to notice that the figures had no definite form but bore triangular heads with fire-spraying eyes, and that their voices were only croaking, not human speech.
