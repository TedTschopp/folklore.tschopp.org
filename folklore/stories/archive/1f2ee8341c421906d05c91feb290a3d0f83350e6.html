She was suspected of being able to make weather. Once, on a magnificent summer day, when much cut hay lay waiting to be brought in and the diligent housewives hung their featherbeds out of the windows to air in the sun, the people of Silenen instructed an innocent little boy to go test the witch. He was to say that he would like to know what people would do if a sudden storm came in: would they first bring the featherbeds or the hay under cover? The little boy did so, and the witch asked, “So, so, would that please you?” The boy said it would please him greatly. She gave him a little pot containing water and small beans and said: “I am going up beneath the roof now. You must stir the little pot, and when lightning comes, pour out a few little beans and some water.” The witch went. Rain soon came when the boy stirred the pot; but as soon as lightning flashed, the little boy was frightened and in his terror threw the pot and everything in it into the air.
