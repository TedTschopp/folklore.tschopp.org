They had peculiar customs. They preferred to choose their sleeping places on the haylofts. They could keep a fire burning there without setting the hay alight. Wood they worked could not be burned. People were supposedly able to verify this in the timberwork of the old town hall at Flums, since popular belief held that the building dated from the time of the Heiden.\nFerd.
