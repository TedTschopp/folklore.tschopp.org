The gracious fairies were later replaced by a strange specter, the Donna di Vanüglia, a white female figure who emerges from the valley of Nüglia and goes about there by day and by night. This interesting personage was once the estate steward at the castle in Zernetz and embezzled much property. After her death she haunted the castle, rattling her mighty ring of keys, until the castle’s owners had a skilled conjurer banish her into the desolate little valley of Nüglia. There she now often goes about with the ring of keys on her arm; what makes her appearance even more horrifying is that she has no nose.
