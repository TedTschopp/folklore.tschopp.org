On the Valais side is the so-called verlorene Berg, a dreadful wasteland where nothing is seen but rubble and scree, with scarcely a blade of grass. The following legend is told of this mountain:", "Several hundred years ago, this mountain held grass-rich pastures, and in summer it was very lively where death now reigns. Below in the valley lived a very wealthy farmer in a stately house. From that house to his Staffel upon the highest Alp, he could have paved and covered a broad road with cheese, so abundantly were his cellars supplied. For all his prosperity, however, he was also terribly stingy and miserly. He clothed his wife and children poorly and scarcely allowed his family, farmhands, and maids even the worst food. If a beggar came asking for alms, he harshly turned the person away with hard words. Once, an old, sick little mother tottered up and pleaded for a piece of bread and a drink of milk because she was faint with hunger and thirst. But the rich man, railing and thundering against the beggar rabble, slammed the house door behind him. The poor woman fainted from fright. A beggar passing by took pity on her; poor himself, he knew the need of others. He shared with her a piece of black bread that revived and strengthened her and fetched her a cooling drink from the nearby spring. Refreshed and heartened by the humane aid of one of her own kind, she slowly climbed the steep mountain, leaning upon a staff and accompanied by her benefactor. At the top, they parted, but first, she cast one more sorrowful glance toward the hardhearted rich man's home in the valley.", "A short time afterward, dreadful storms accompanied by thunder, lightning, and earthquakes seemed to announce the approaching end of the world. Trembling, everyone hid inside, but the rich man watched the fury of the elements with pride and scorn. Yet behold! With a thunderous crash and roar, an immense mass of rock and ice broke loose from the highest summit of the Saanetsch and shot into the depths with lightning speed, devastating, sweeping away, and destroying everything.
