Wild knights are said to have committed outrages in this castle and tormented the surrounding people. For that reason, they supposedly appear at certain times of the year, especially when it will soon rain. Thus, several men relate that once, while they were in the fields in broad daylight, they heard a dreadful rattling from the little forest and suddenly saw several magnificent wagons driving out. Richly dressed lords and ladies sat in the wagons and urged the horses to run quickly by calling out. Suddenly, everything vanished.
