It has now been destroyed down to its foundations. Around the year 1480, its last owner was Junker Albin von Silinen, a brave and benevolent lord. Many farms in the surrounding country had to pay annual ground rents and other dues to the castle. The farm in Wiesthal also owed such payments to the castle lord. At that time, however, a widow with seven children lived on this farm. She was an industrious and upright woman. One summer, a hailstorm struck the region and destroyed the honest widow's entire harvest. The woman took two of her children and went sadly with them to Rüssegg. Weeping, she told the Junker of her misfortune and asked him to forgive the dues she owed for that year. He comforted her and had milk and bread set before her and the children.
