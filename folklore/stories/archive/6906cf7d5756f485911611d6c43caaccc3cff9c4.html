One night they deliberately went through this meadow late around midnight in order to meet him. When he truly stood there, they called to him with laughter, “Chum, brünniger Ma, zünd is!” He instantly came within a single step of them, and the frightened girls fled into the nearest house. There he now hovered back and forth so close before the folding window shutters that they threatened to catch fire. The girls began to plead with him to go away, saying that they would rather pray something for him. “Very well,” he said, “but a rosary!” They began one, going through all the Our Fathers and Hail Marys. By the last Our Father their hearts had grown lighter again, and they laughed irreverently in the middle of the prayer. At once the Brünnling swept up and down before the windows with renewed heat and ordered them to begin again from the start and pray better, or else he would not leave.
