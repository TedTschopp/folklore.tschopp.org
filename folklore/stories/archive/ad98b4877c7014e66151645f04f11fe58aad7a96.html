Zgraggen" ], "english_paragraphs": [ "About 60 years ago, the Bernese brought a tremendous giant on a chain up through the land of Glarus as far as Linthal and challenged the people of Glarus to a Schwinget. At that time there were very many young, strapping, immensely strong unmarried men in the canton of Glarus. My 80-year-old master, who told me the story, was also such a young bachelor then. But not one of them dared defend the honor of Glarus and grapple with the giant. So they sent for old Fridli Dürst, a small Mandli who was no longer young but possessed an unreasonable strength. They begged him to come wrestle with the Bernese man and save the honor of the land of Glarus. At first he refused and excused himself on account of his age. Little by little, however, he yielded to their pleas and went with them to Linthal. The Bernese were lodging there at the Raben. The giant came out in front of the house. Fridli looked at him, tossed his little leather cap onto the open ground, and said, ‘There—onto that very spot you must fly.’ The giant and the Bernese laughed. But without warning the Durst got beneath his opponent, threw him backward over his head, and broke his neck. Then the people of Glarus rejoiced greatly, drinking and celebrating far into the night.", "Joh. Jos.
