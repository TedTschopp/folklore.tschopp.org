Often, when she passed them early in the morning or in the twilight of evening, or traveled in a boat on Lake Lucerne from Burgweidli to Angelfluh, she said she had seen stately knights, sometimes dressed like courtiers in silk doublets, and at other times clad in steel with helmets and swords. Once, after such a friendly invitation, she had dared to enter the small meadow situated between the lake and the castle. There, to no small astonishment and delight, she saw the knights amusing themselves with a golden game of skittles.
