Once, when he was somewhat delayed, his companion Saint Achatus had taken his place. The church was already completely filled with devout listeners when Saint Beatus appeared. But because it was a very warm day and Saint Achatus was no exceptional orator, to his great sorrow he had to watch one member of the congregation after another fall into sweet sleep. Saint Beatus listened all the more eagerly and devoutly to his pupil’s words. Then he suddenly perceived the Devil beneath the pulpit: sitting there with one leg crossed over the other and a crow’s feather in his hand, he wrote with all speed on a great goatskin the names of the sleepers who had fallen to him. Saint Beatus was in despair. He would gladly have woken the sleepers, but he could not interrupt the sermon without committing a mortal sin. Meanwhile the Devil wrote diligently on, and his infernal writing tablet was already full although not all the sleepers’ names had been entered. The Devil, unwilling to let an opportunity to seize as many souls as possible pass unused, thought to stretch the goatskin farther in length and breadth. He took one end in his teeth and, bracing himself against the baptismal font, grasped the other with his claws. In this truly devilish purpose he strained so hard that the goatskin suddenly tore and his head struck the foot of the pulpit with all its force. This made such a comical sight that Saint Beatus had to laugh aloud. The whole congregation awoke a few moments before the Amen, and the Devil lost the prey he had already imagined safely lodged in hell. Naturally this was very unpleasant for him. Angry and ashamed, he rushed away and plunged into the Thunersee, whose waves surged high and closed over him. Saint Beatus, however, did not go unpunished for the sin he had committed when his gleeful laughter interrupted the sermon. When he reached the lake, which he customarily crossed on his cloak to his dwelling in the Beatus Cave, the cloak had forever lost the God-given power to carry the saint." ], "notes": [] }, "legacy_urls": [] } ---
