Once, toward midnight, the schoolmaster at Flums heard a peculiar murmuring down in the street, like the prayer of a mighty host of people. He leapt up, pulled on only one trouser leg, and sprang to the window—and what did he see? A long, long procession of dead people from Flums, with here and there one of the still living among them! The murmuring came from the sorrowful rosary recited by the entire funeral procession. Among those near the front, he recognized many old acquaintances. The farther back in the procession, the more unfamiliar and unknown they became to him. At the front, he saw the old black banner of Flums, the cross, the clergyman, and beside him the sacristan himself, wearing only one trouser leg and with the Pfisterläuferli around his neck. Everyone prayed without looking around. Nothing of the prayer could be understood except: \"...
