The forest stream Schliera above Alpnach once concealed a dreadful “Hüwel” among its firs and bushes. It was an enchanted human being. When hunters aimed at it, it behaved so fearsomely that they did not dare shoot. Several young hunters even mixed Malefitzpulver into their live ammunition, but even then they could not pull the trigger—or the cock and flint failed, or the bullet missed its target, or the barrel was bent, or there was too much wind.
