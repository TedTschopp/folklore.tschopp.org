Stoop" ], "english_paragraphs": [ "Before the railway was built, livestock leased out for Sömmerung in the Glarus Alps was driven from Flums over the passes at Schönbühl and Wissmil. Old Jahn had agreed with another farmer that they would make the Alpfahrt into Glarus together. They intended to wait for one another by the huts at Fursch. But the other man did not come. As darkness fell, Jahn drove his livestock together into the enclosure and made a fire in the hut. When he later looked out through the door, a figure in a black cloak and Wetterhut approached the hut, entered the enclosure, and began driving the livestock out toward the Stollen. Jahn overcame his fear, ran after the livestock, and turned it back into the enclosure. The uncanny figure had vanished. By morning, however, so much snow had fallen that Jahn had to be glad merely to reach home again.\nJ. B.
