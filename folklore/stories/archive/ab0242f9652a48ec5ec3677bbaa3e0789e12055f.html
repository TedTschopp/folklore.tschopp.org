A young dwarf woman had been overtaken by the hour of childbirth and gladly accepted the aid of a woman who hurried over. To reward the service, she gave the woman a handful of coals to take home. The woman seems to have expected different thanks, for she threw away the coals along the way. A single little piece remained in her apron, and meanwhile, it had transformed into the purest gold. The recipient now saw that she had acted foolishly. She quickly went back and searched along the road and among the brush but found nothing more. She did, however, see many little dwarfs descending from the Mugg with burning torches.
