They are especially unruly, wild, and treacherous in the alpine country, and the Älpler would often fare badly with them if they did not have the Alpsegen, holy water, and above all a good conscience. An exceptionally wild Buzen dwells on the Gifi in the Valais Alps. Scarcely have the Betglocken from the village of Münster fallen silent in the evening when the accursed Buzen shoots like a firebrand across Alp and ridge. Woe to the solitary Älpler or mountain traveler who is still on the road. The Buzen is always around him, taunting and deceiving him viciously. It drives the livestock apart into the gaping Tobel. Whatever kind of storm lantern the traveler may carry, the Gifibuzen extinguishes it all at once. Suddenly, while everything upon the sleeping alpine pastures is utterly still, a Jauchzen that pierces marrow and bone shrills through the night. If the traveler, frightened to death, now runs away in mad haste without light or support, the Buzen trips him so that he falls into some pit or even over the Fluh. His heavy mountain shoes strode out bravely, and he went forward without stopping. In the distance, he saw the friendly little lights of Münster burning. He also heard the hour strike from the village church. He walked faster and faster.
