From time to time, however, substantial quantities of the precious liquor were stolen from him by mysterious means. This could only be done by magic. He had a Haspel, and one day he said to his assistant, Gehren-Mariä, “Keep going now and turn that Haspel a little for me!” Mariä began to turn it, faster and faster, until the Bernese man said, “Keep going, but do not turn so quickly, or she will do herself harm.” He then turned more slowly, and before long a woman came running, panting, nearly choking, and covered in sweat, carrying the remaining Enzenwasser. She was the so-called Golzner Loch from the little village, born at Golzer in the Maderan Valley, the mother of “Devil” and “Satan.” This is said to be true. At first I did not want to believe it, but Gehren-Mariä told it to me as certain truth. Yes, people always said that the Bernese could do more than others: they could bind people by magic, drive things back, and wish illness and death upon people and livestock.
