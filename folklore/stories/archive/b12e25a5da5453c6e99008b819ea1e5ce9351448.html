Many, many years ago—so long ago that the forebears could not remember it—a tiny little man would now and then come into the village; nobody knew where it came from. Someone had once seen it coming down from Tuf, and so it was called the Tufmannli. It told some people the truth, gave good advice, prevented many a misfortune, and helped in situations of discord, so that everyone had sincere respect for it. But it also had such curious habits, and in Schaan there had always been some unruly and malicious people. Once, when it had to pass them at the Spötterbank, they tried to make a mockery of it. At that, the little man flew into a boundless rage, wished the very worst upon them, and told the ringleader that for as long as he lived he must have an evil neighbor, a bad roof, and salted bread. He was now punished enough, and the others received their share as well; but the Tufmannli never came again, and many a household suffered badly for it.\nU.
