As the horse trotted briskly along the road, the rider thought of the monster who had once murdered a child wandering there. The robber had put the following questions to the little boy: “What is lovelier than the day?” The child answered: “A mother’s gaze!” “What is nobler than gold?” “A mother’s heart!” “What is sweeter than honey?” “A mother’s milk!” “What is softer than down?” “A mother’s lap!” “What is stronger than death?” “A mother’s love!” “What is harder than stone?” “A murderer’s heart!” At that, the murderer hurled the child against the rock with such force that it split and is still called the Mörderstein today.", "The man from Gampel had left the great block, overgrown with moss, lichens, and grass, behind him, and in half an hour he would reach the far edge of the forest. What are you doing?” The rider was badly frightened and believed himself lost, for in the robber who made the Pfynwald unsafe he recognized his former comrade Peter. He reined in the horse and replied: “My wife lies deathly ill in bed, and I have fetched medicine from the herbalist in Siders!” The other man said: “Do you no longer recognize your old comrade, who has become a murderer?
