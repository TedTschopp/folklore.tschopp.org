A shadow of sorrow passed across his face, and he told the questioning parents: ‘This child was born in an unfortunate hour, and three evil hours await him in his life. In the first he will want to hang himself; in the second, if he should survive the hour of temptation, lightning will threaten him. Unfortunately, my informant could no longer name the third danger. But if you follow my well-meant advice, we may hope that he will safely withstand all three hours. From earliest youth, teach and train the child to say of everything he undertakes and does, “In God's name.” Likewise, he should be taught to place his whole trust in divine Providence in every danger and to say, “Where God protects me, there I am protected.”’", "The anxious parents faithfully followed the wise advice. Later, when the child could speak, he too said, ‘In God's name,’ in all his actions. Wherever danger threatened, the parents said, and taught the child to say, ‘Where God protects me, there I am protected.’", "The words with their momentous meaning penetrated deep into the child's soul and became second nature to him. When he was seven years old, he took a rope and hurried with it beneath a tree to hang himself. The dangerous game repeated itself until the unfortunate hour had passed. Then the cat vanished, the child went home and said, ‘Ich ha nit chennä’—‘I couldn't do it.’ The first evil hour had been safely overcome.
