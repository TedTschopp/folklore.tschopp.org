He once had to take part in a campaign in which, on one particular day, friends and enemies were shot down in great numbers. Although unhurt, he too sought the ground so that he could hide behind the Lerchen and protect himself from enemy bullets. Then he clearly heard a dead man beside whom he lay tell him that he had no need to hide, for he would die not there but among the rocky clefts behind Mount Prabé in Savièse. Our fearful soldier did indeed escape unharmed and returned to his native village after completing his term of service.", "There, he certainly did not forget the prophecy he had heard in such a strange fashion on the battlefield in Naples. One day, however, the weather seemed so favorable and inviting that a great desire to hunt seized him. He threw his gun over his back and went up the mountain almost without willing it. Soon he reached the ridge of Mount Prabé—and he intended to go no farther. But a fine chamois buck caught his eye on the mountain beyond, almost within shooting range. Without thinking, he eagerly went after the beautiful animal, which advanced only as quickly as he was able to follow.
