On it stands a small chapel dedicated to Saint Eulogius, patron saint of smiths. According to deeds of sale, the smith in the ancient village of Schötz had to see to its upkeep, although he did not own the property. The reason is this. Anneli, the young daughter of a Schötz smith, practiced witchcraft. Her lover, a little man dressed in green, with a red beard and a swaying plume on his beret, is said to have turned her into a dapple-gray horse during a meeting on a green meadow. He rode her to the Schmiedbrücke in Schötz and had the horse newly shod there, the work being done by Anneli's own father. When he had finished, the horse spoke and revealed herself to her father. The rider then raced away with the horse over hill and dale as far as the spot where that little chapel now stands.
