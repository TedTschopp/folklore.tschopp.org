The lord of the castle and his wife had been very gentle toward their subjects; their daughter, however, behaved with extreme pride and love of splendor and harshly squeezed even their little bit of money from the poor people in these rugged high valleys. In the Swedish War, the castle was finally destroyed and the daughter slain by the soldiers.
