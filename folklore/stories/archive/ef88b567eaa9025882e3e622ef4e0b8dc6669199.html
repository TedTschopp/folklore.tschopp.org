Below Balzers, as deep darkness fell, some neatly dressed people called him aside from the road, and he encountered a splendid company. They told him to sit upon a platform where choice food and drink were set out. One gentleman merely warned him not to let anything disturb him, not to pay too much attention to anything, and especially not to drink anyone's health. This struck him as odd, but he kept silent, played, and enjoyed the refreshments. Before him, the company danced wildly and colorfully, and his cup was never empty. Yet no one concerned themselves with him further, so despite all the revelry, he eventually grew bored. Warmed by drink and forgetting the warning, he said inwardly over one cup, as was his habit: ‘Gsundheit Hans! Gseg' ders Gott, Hans! Fürchts der nüt, so gschieht der nüt’—‘Health to you, Hans! God bless you, Hans! Fear nothing, and nothing will happen to you.’ Scarcely had the words crossed his lips when everything vanished.
