Before the knight began his holy journey, however, he broke his ring into two halves. He gave one to his wife Susanna, kept the other himself, and said, “When you see this half again, it will be a sign to you that I am still alive. The devil then gently lifted the knight from the ground and in a few moments brought him safe and unharmed to his castle on Lake Thun. When Wernhard entered the castle gate, all the windows were brightly lit, for a celebration was taking place inside. Herr Wernhard presented himself as a fahrender Spielmann. The knight dropped his half of the ring into the cup from which his wife was to drink and went silently away to stand in a corner of the hall.
