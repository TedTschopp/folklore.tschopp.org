He kept it for himself and left the father to the poor-relief authorities. When the farmer died young, the small children pointed on Saturday evenings to a dark place beneath the chamber stairs and said, ‘Mother, look, there is Father.’ This repeated itself for some time. The woman saw nothing but was nevertheless afraid and had a Capuchin come from Mels. He learned the particulars and decided that the wrong committed must be put right. No money remained. The widow had to petition the municipal assembly to forgive her the debt. On the recommendation of the administrative council, this was unanimously approved according to the official minutes. From then on, the arme Seele of the departed father had peace and never appeared again.\nJ. B.
