The man of Grossprachtigen had a Maiensäss above Gurtnellen called ‘Berg.’ On this Berg he had to suffer terribly. All the servants he placed there died within a short time, and so it came about that no one else would take the position and the property could not be sold either. This caused great distress. At last there came two young men from Valais, bold and strong fellows who did not know what fear was and wished to enter the Ratsherr’s service. People told them they would soon be dead there, and the master himself admitted to the servants that this Berg was dangerous and that several men there had succumbed to a specter. The vigorous Valais men would not be deterred and declared that they would soon chase the specter away. In good spirits they went up to the Berg. On the third evening, while one of them was cooking, something threw soot down the chimney at him. He ran into the room with the pan and asked his companion whether they should eat first or chase the specter first. They agreed to chase it first and eat afterward. One seized a cudgel and the other a saber from pagan times. The first went up onto the Russdiele to drive the specter down. As he came up to the upper passage that led to the loft, the monstrous thing seized him by the hair. ‘Pull me upward all you like,’ he said. ‘Once I am up there, I shall drive you down soon enough.’ No sooner was he above than he drove the specter before him toward the only opening that led out of the Russdiele. At this dangerous point, however, the man with the saber waited resolutely. The monster leaped through the narrow passage in the form of a black cat, and in an instant the brave man dealt a terrible stroke that cut off the cat’s right forefoot. When they examined it more closely, however, it was a human hand, and a ring was still on one finger. Yet they were not afraid. On the fourth day the Ratsherr came to see whether the servants were still alive. They told him what had happened and showed him the hand they had kept. He recognized the ring as his wife’s; since the day before, she had been lying ill in bed at home and kept her right arm hidden and bandaged beneath the covers. A dreadful suspicion arose in him and was unfortunately confirmed when he afterward investigated at home.
