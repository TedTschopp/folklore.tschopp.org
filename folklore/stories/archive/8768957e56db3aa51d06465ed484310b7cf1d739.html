Evening had come and dark night was already beginning to fall. — With hypocritical courtesy, the knight promised to escort the cleric through the dangerous “Tiefe-Tobel” that extends between Bernegg Castle and neighboring Castiel. Overjoyed, the pious man accepted the offer and wished the nobleman God’s blessing for it. The two now set out in the deepest darkness, the knight in front with the torch but deliberately choosing a false path toward the Tobel. — Suddenly, in the middle of the forest, he extinguished the flame and with scornful laughter left the pilgrim standing helpless in the dreadful darkness. Meanwhile a mighty thunderstorm had gathered; lightning darted like fiery serpents and thunder echoed in the clefts. Rain poured down in torrents. — Then a glaring bolt of lightning shot through the air, set fire to the fir beneath which the merciless man had hidden for shelter from the storm, and stunned him as well. The fire spread rapidly and consumed the fir and the knight.
