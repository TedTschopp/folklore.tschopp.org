Beings and beliefs

A name can change its meaning from valley to valley. Begin with a subject, then follow the notes attached to a particular telling.

  • Ghosts, returning spirits and uncanny encounters
  • Witches, spells and enchanted objects
  • Little mountain dwellers and wild people
  • Dragons, giants and strange beasts
  • Saints, pilgrims and protective customs

Selected profiles of beings, objects and customs cover five early tales in the collection. The topic pages include the wider published collection.

Reading the sources

A local legend may name a bridge, a family or a hillside; a wonder tale may follow an impossible journey. The telling records a belief or a story, rather than establishing that its events occurred.

Each reading page keeps the English translation, reader notes and original text in separate sections. The source credit links to the edition used for that page. Different tellings of a similar story remain distinct where their wording or events differ.
